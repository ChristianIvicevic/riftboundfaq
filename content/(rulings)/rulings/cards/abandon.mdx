## Where does a Flow spell countered by Abandon go? [#flow-destination]

If no other replacement effects apply, it is banished rather than returned to its owner's hand.

Abandon's "Counter a spell" instruction targets a <Term item="finalization">finalized</Term> spell on the <Term item="chain">chain</Term>.<Rule number="355.10" /><Rule number="355.9.a.2" />
Countering would clear that spell from the chain and put it in the trash.<Rule number="426.1.a" /><Rule number="426.1.a.1" />
Abandon's later instruction replaces putting it in the trash with returning it to its owner's hand.<Rule number="158.2" /><Rule number="369.1" />
Because Abandon's counter, rather than the spell's own execution, makes it leave the chain, the delayed replacement effect created by <Flow /> also applies and would banish it instead.<Rule number="829.1.b.1" />

Both replacement effects apply to the same departure event, so the spell's controller chooses their order.<Rule number="372" />

The result is the same in either order.
If <Flow /> applies first, the spell is banished instead of going to the trash, so Abandon no longer has a qualifying event to replace.<Rule number="370.1" />
If Abandon applies first, it replaces the original event with the spell leaving the chain for its owner's hand.<Rule number="370.1.b" />
That replacement event still makes the spell leave the chain, so <Flow /> applies and banishes it instead.<Rule number="370.2" />
An additional replacement effect joins this ordering and can change the final destination.<Rule number="370.2" /><Rule number="372" />
