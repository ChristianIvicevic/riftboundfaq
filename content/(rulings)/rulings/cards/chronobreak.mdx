## In what order does Chronobreak play unit cards with different owners? [#unit-play-sequence]

The turn player begins playing the units they own, choosing their order.
Each other player then does the same in turn order.<Rule number="303.2.a" />

Those units wait on the <Term item="chain">chain</Term> until Chronobreak finishes <Term item="resolution">resolving</Term>, then process in that order:<Rule number="354.2" /><Rule number="354.3" />

<Steps>
<Step>
The first unit card put on the chain undergoes <Term item="finalization">finalization</Term> first.<Rule number="337.1.b" />
A unit that finalizes resolves immediately and enters before the next unit card undergoes finalization.<Rule number="337.2" />
</Step>
<Step>
Any play effect that unit triggers is added after every unit already waiting on the chain.<Rule number="383.4.a.2" />
</Step>
<Step>
The remaining unit cards undergo finalization in the order they were put on the chain.<Rule number="337.1.b" />
Any ordinary play effects then finalize oldest-first in the order they were added.<Rule number="337.1.b" /><Rule number="383.4.a.2" />
</Step>
</Steps>

## Can Chronobreak play back a unit token? [#unit-tokens]

No.
The unit token ceases to exist immediately after Chronobreak banishes it, so it no longer exists when Chronobreak would instruct its owner to play it.<Rule number="186" /><Rule number="186.1" />
