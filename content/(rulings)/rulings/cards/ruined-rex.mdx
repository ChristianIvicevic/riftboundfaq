## Does Ruined Rex's Deathknell deal damage before or after units are healed at the end of combat? [#heal-timing]

Ruined Rex's <Deathknell /> deals damage after the surviving units are healed.
The combat <Term item="cleanup">cleanup</Term> adds the ability as a <Term item="pending">pending</Term> item.<Rule number="808.1.d.2" />
That cleanup heals all surviving units before the pending ability can begin <Term item="finalization">finalization</Term>.<Rule number="468.1.a.1" /><Rule number="320" /><Rule number="334.2" />

If a legal enemy unit is available, the trigger's controller must choose one as its target during finalization.<Rule number="355.10" /><Rule number="403.2" /><Rule number="403.4.b" />
Once no pending items remain, the controller of the next item receives <Term item="priority">priority</Term> before the trigger <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term>.<Rule number="337.4" />
It deals 4 damage to the chosen enemy unit.

## Can Ruined Rex's Deathknell target the Mech tokens created by Ferrous Forerunner's Deathknell, if both die at the same time? [#ferrous-forerunner-mechs]

No.
The trigger's controller chooses its target before <Card name="Ferrous Forerunner" /> creates its Mech tokens.

When both units die in the same <Term item="cleanup">cleanup</Term>, their <Deathknell /> abilities become <Term item="pending">pending</Term> items.<Rule number="808.1.d.2" />
Each pending item <Term item="finalization">finalizes</Term> or leaves the <Term item="chain">chain</Term> before <Term item="priority">priority</Term> is awarded.<Rule number="337.1" /><Rule number="337.3" /><Rule number="337.4" />

The trigger's controller chooses its target during ability finalization.<Rule number="355.10" /><Rule number="403.2" />
Ferrous Forerunner creates its Mechs only when its ability later <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term>.<Rule number="340.1" />
Regardless of finalization order, those Mechs do not exist when the trigger's controller chooses its target.
That choice cannot change when Ferrous Forerunner's ability later resolves.<Rule number="355.15" />
