## How do I perform Drakehound Packmaster's reveal instruction with one Void Hatchling? [#single-replacement]

For each opponent's main deck, look at its current top card and choose whether to recycle it, then reveal the card now on top.

Void Hatchling replaces <Card name="Drakehound Packmaster" />'s reveal instruction with that sequence.<Rule number="370.1.b" />
If Drakehound Packmaster reveals from several opponents' decks at once, each deck's reveal is treated separately for replacement effects.<Rule number="373" />
One Void Hatchling can apply across all of those events in the same replacement sequence.<Rule number="373.2" />

Drakehound Packmaster then continues with the cards it revealed.

## How do I perform Drakehound Packmaster's reveal instruction with three Void Hatchlings? [#three-replacements]

For each opponent's main deck, apply the three replacement effects one at a time, making three separate look-and-recycle choices before revealing the card currently on top.

Each replacement changes <Card name="Drakehound Packmaster" />'s reveal into a look at the current top card, an optional recycle, and another reveal.<Rule number="370.1.b" />
When several replacement effects apply, they are ordered and applied one at a time.<Rule number="372" />
After one copy applies, it cannot apply again to the original reveal or any event that replaced it.<Rule number="370.2" />
The reveal created by that copy can still be replaced by another copy, so all three apply in sequence.<Rule number="370.1.b" /><Rule number="370.2" />
If Drakehound Packmaster reveals from several opponents' decks at once, each deck's reveal is treated separately.<Rule number="373" />
Each Void Hatchling can apply across all of those events in one replacement sequence.<Rule number="373.2" />

Each time, look only at the current top card and choose whether to recycle it.
If you leave it on top, the next replacement looks at the same card; if you recycle it, the next replacement looks at the new top card.
You never look at more than one card at a time or reorder any cards left on top.

After the third replacement, reveal the current top card and continue Drakehound Packmaster's instructions normally.
