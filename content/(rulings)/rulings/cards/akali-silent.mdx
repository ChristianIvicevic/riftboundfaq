## Can an opponent's Deathknell target Akali, Silent after combat damage? [#deathknell-targeting]

Yes, Akali can be targeted by enemy <Deathknell /> abilities until combat ends, as long as she remains at the battlefield with a combat designation.

Combat has three steps:

<Steps>
<Step>
**Combat Showdown Step.**<Rule number="466" />
</Step>
<Step>
**Combat Damage Step.**<Rule number="467" />
</Step>
<Step>
**Resolution Step.**<Rule number="468" />
The combat <Term item="cleanup">cleanup</Term> kills lethally damaged units.<Rule number="468.1" /><Rule number="323.4" />
A <Deathknell /> trigger is added to the <Term item="chain">chain</Term> before its unit moves to the trash.<Rule number="808.1.d.2" />
Any targets are chosen when those abilities <Term item="finalization">finalize</Term>.<Rule number="403.2" />
Those abilities fully <Term item="resolution">resolve</Term> before combat proceeds to determining the result.<Rule number="468.2" />
Combat ends only at the final part of this step.<Rule number="468.7" />
Only then is Akali's combat designation removed, so she stops being in combat and her restriction against enemy spells and abilities applies again.<Rule number="468.7.a" /><Rule number="740.2.c" />
</Step>
</Steps>

This is why <Card name="Ruined Rex" />'s <Deathknell /> can choose her after Rex dies to combat damage.
