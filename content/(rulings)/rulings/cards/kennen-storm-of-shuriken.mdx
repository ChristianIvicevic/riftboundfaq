## Can I play a spell at its Action or Reaction timing after Kennen, Storm of Shuriken grants it Flow? [#flow-timing]

Yes. A spell that Kennen grants <Flow /> keeps its existing timing permissions, whether it has <Action />, <Reaction />, or neither.
Playing the spell for its <Flow /> cost does not change the timing at which it can be played or any permission except the zone from which it can be played: your trash.<Rule number="829.1.b" /><Rule number="829.1.b.2" />

## Can Kennen, Storm of Shuriken grant Flow to a spell put into the trash by Minefield's ability triggered by the same conquer? [#minefield-flow]

No.
You must choose a spell already in your trash while Kennen's conquer ability is being <Term item="finalization">finalized</Term> on the <Term item="chain">chain</Term>.<Rule number="403.2" />

Kennen's ability and <Card name="Minefield" />'s ability trigger simultaneously from the same conquer and are added to the chain as <Term item="pending">pending</Term> items.<Rule number="383.3.d" />
Their order does not change the result because every pending item must be finalized before either ability can <Term item="resolution">resolve</Term>.<Rule number="337.1" /><Rule number="337.3" /><Rule number="337.4" />
Minefield therefore cannot put its two cards into your trash until after you have chosen a spell for Kennen's ability, and you cannot later change that choice.<Rule number="355.15" />
