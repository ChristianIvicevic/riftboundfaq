## If my Dazzling Aurora plays Akshan, Mischievous at the end of my turn, will the Dazzling Aurora I take from my opponent trigger that turn? [#aurora-timing]

No.
You gain control of the enemy <Card name="Dazzling Aurora" /> after its end-of-turn trigger condition has already been checked.

"At the end of your turn" refers to the Ending Step's end-of-turn point.<Rule number="317.1.a" /><Rule number="383.1" />
At that point, the enemy Dazzling Aurora is still controlled by your opponent, so its controller-relative "your turn" condition is not fulfilled.<Rule number="191.5.a" /><Rule number="383.2.c" />

Your Dazzling Aurora's end-of-turn ability plays Akshan after that point in the turn has been reached.
After Akshan enters the board, his play effect is added to the <Term item="chain">chain</Term> as a <Term item="pending">pending</Term> item.<Rule number="383.4.a" /><Rule number="383.4.a.2" />
You gain control of the enemy Dazzling Aurora only when that ability later <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term>.<Rule number="407.5" />

If you take control of an enemy Dazzling Aurora before the Ending Step and still control it at the end-of-turn point, it triggers normally.

## If my opponent and I each use Akshan, Mischievous to take the same gear, who controls it when either Akshan dies? [#dual-akshan-control]

After both play effects have <Term item="resolution">resolved</Term>, if either Akshan dies, the gear is controlled by the player whose control effect remains.
While both effects remain, the player whose effect was generated most recently controls the gear.

Each resolved play effect creates a delayed passive ability, a control effect that remains active until that Akshan leaves the board.<Rule number="391.4" /><Rule number="392" />
While both effects remain, they change the gear's controller in the same trait-altering layer.<Rule number="479.2.a" />
Neither effect changes the other's existence, scope, or result, so no dependency is established.<Rule number="480.1.a" /><Rule number="480.1.b" /><Rule number="480.1.c" />
Timestamp order therefore applies.<Rule number="482" />
Each effect's timestamp is established when it begins applying, and the newer effect applies last and determines who controls the gear.<Rule number="482.1" /><Rule number="482.3" />

When an Akshan dies, it moves from the board to the trash, ending that Akshan's control effect and leaving the other effect to determine control.<Rule number="429.2" />
