## Can Last Caress let me play an Ambush unit at a battlefield where I don't control any units? [#ambush-location]

Yes, Last Caress lets you play the next unit this turn to a battlefield where you control no units, as long as you could otherwise play it at that time and no restriction or mandatory requirement limits its location.<Rule number="355.17" /><Rule number="355.17.b" />
Last Caress independently makes every battlefield valid for that unit, even when <Ambush />'s own location permission does not apply.<Rule number="355.2.b" /><Rule number="822.3.a" />

This changes only where you may play the unit, not when.
Last Caress does not grant <Reaction />, and <Ambush /> grants <Reaction /> only while you are playing the unit to a battlefield where you control units.<Rule number="822.1.b" /><Rule number="813.4" />

## If I play Here to Help from face down, can Last Caress let me play a unit from my hand at a different battlefield? [#here-to-help-location]

No, if <Card name="Here to Help" /> was played from face down, Last Caress does not let you play the unit from your hand at a different battlefield.
Once you choose to play a unit, Here to Help's "to a battlefield you control" instruction is a mandatory requirement on its location.<Rule number="355.17" />
Because Here to Help was played from face down, the facedown rules also require that unit to be played at the same battlefield.<Rule number="811.1.d.5" />
These requirements do not conflict and must both be followed.<Rule number="355.17.a" />
Last Caress makes additional battlefields valid locations, but it does not remove either requirement.<Rule number="355.2.b" />
Any different battlefield fails the facedown requirement, and a battlefield you do not control also fails Here to Help's own requirement.

Last Caress's "even from face down" text applies when the next unit itself is played from face down.
Here to Help instead instructs you to play the unit from your hand, so that text does not override the requirements imposed by Here to Help and the facedown rules.
