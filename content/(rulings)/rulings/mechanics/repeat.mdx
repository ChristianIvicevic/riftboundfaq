## When do I decide whether to pay for Repeat? [#repeat-decision-timing]

Decide separately whether to pay each <Repeat /> cost while making the relevant choices as you play the spell or ability.<Rule number="355.1.a" /><Rule number="400" /><Rule number="820.1.c.2" /><Rule number="820.1.d" />
Each cost can be paid only once.<Rule number="820.1.c.3" />
For every <Repeat /> cost you choose to pay, make any required choices for its additional execution during that same step.<Rule number="820.2" />
Those choices can be the same as or different from the choices for the initial execution.<Rule number="820.2.a" />
After that step, the decisions and choices cannot change unless a spell or ability instructs otherwise.<Rule number="355.15" />

Opponents cannot respond while you make these choices or pay the costs because <Term item="finalization">finalization</Term> does not pass <Term item="priority">priority</Term>.<Rule number="337.1.a" /><Rule number="357" /><Rule number="405.1" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	When playing <Card name="Blood Rush" />, if you pay its <Repeat /> <Energy value={1} /> cost, choose one unit for the initial execution and one for the additional execution.<Rule number="820.2" />
	You can choose the same unit or different units.<Rule number="820.2.a" />
	Both choices are locked before you pay the spell's cost.<Rule number="355.15" /><Rule number="357" />
</Callout>

## What does Repeat repeat? [#repeated-instructions]

Each <Repeat /> cost you pay makes the spell or ability execute its instructions performed during <Term item="resolution">resolution</Term> one additional time.<Rule number="820.1.d" /><Rule number="820.1.d.2" /><Rule number="820.3" />
It does not play the spell or ability again or create another <Term item="chain">chain</Term> item.<Rule number="820.1.d" /><Rule number="820.3.a" />
The play steps and costs are not repeated because only resolution instructions execute again.<Rule number="820.1.c.1" /><Rule number="820.1.d.2" /><Rule number="820.3.a" />

You choose targets, modes, and other required decisions separately for each execution.<Rule number="820.2" />
The choices for an additional execution can differ from those for the initial execution.<Rule number="820.2.a" />

## What happens if a spell with Repeat is countered? [#repeated-spell-countering]

If a spell with <Repeat /> is countered, neither its initial execution nor any additional execution happens because the spell does nothing, and its paid <Repeat /> costs are not refunded because countering refunds no paid costs.<Rule number="426.1.a" /><Rule number="426.1.c" /><Rule number="426.1.c.1" /><Rule number="820.1.d" /><Rule number="820.3.a" />
