## Can opponents react to an Empower ability? [#empower-reactions]

Yes.
<Empower /> is an activated ability keyword,<Rule number="827.1" /> and activated abilities use the <Term item="chain">chain</Term>.<Rule number="377.3" />
After the ability is <Term item="finalization">finalized</Term> and no other cards or abilities are waiting to finalize, opponents have an opportunity to play Reactions before it <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term>.<Rule number="337.4" /><Rule number="407.4" />

## When can I activate an Empower ability? [#empower-timing]

By default, you can activate an <Empower /> ability whose source you control only while its source is not <Empowered /><Rule number="827.1.c.1" /> and during your own Main Phase when you have <Term item="priority">priority</Term> and no showdown, combat, or <Term item="chain">chain</Term> is in progress.<Rule number="310.1" /><Rule number="310.1.a" /><Rule number="381.1" />
These timing rules apply because <Empower /> is an activated ability.<Rule number="827.1" />

If the source has or gains permission to be empowered multiple times, it ignores <Empower />'s normal restriction against activating while already <Empowered />.<Rule number="827.1.c.5" />
An <Empower /> ability may also include text that changes its timing.<Rule number="827.1.c.4" />
If it has or gains <Action />, you may activate it during showdowns on any player's turn, but not while a chain exists.<Rule number="309.1" /><Rule number="309.1.a" /><Rule number="806.1.c.2" /><Rule number="806.4" />
If it has <Reaction />, it gains all of <Action />'s permissions and may also be activated during closed states on any player's turn.<Rule number="813.1.b" /><Rule number="813.1.c.2" />

## Does a "When I become Empowered" ability trigger if the unit leaves the board before its Empower ability resolves? [#removed-source-trigger]

No.
If the unit leaves the board before its <Empower /> ability <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term>, it does not become <Empowered />, so its "When I become <Empowered />" ability does not trigger.<Rule number="442.2" /><Rule number="828.1.d" />

Removing the unit does not counter its already-<Term item="finalization">finalized</Term> <Empower /> ability, so that ability remains on the <Term item="chain">chain</Term>.<Rule number="407.2" /><Rule number="426.1" /><Rule number="426.2.a" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	You activate <Card name="Mel, Defiant Soul" />'s <Empower /> ability and discard a spell to pay its cost.
	Your opponent responds with <Card name="Star-Crossed" />, returning Mel and one of their own units, each to its owner's hand.
	Star-Crossed resolves first, so Mel leaves the board while her <Empower /> ability remains on the chain.<Rule number="340.1" />
	Mel's <Empower /> ability then resolves, but Mel cannot become <Empowered /> while she is off the board, so her banish ability does not trigger.
</Callout>

When the <Empower /> ability resolves, it tries to empower the unit whose ability was activated.<Rule number="407.5" /><Rule number="827.1.c.1" />
Only a game object on the board can become <Empowered />.
While the original unit is off the board, it cannot become <Empowered /> or evaluate its triggered ability's condition.<Rule number="384.2" />

If the card returns from a non-board zone before the <Empower /> ability resolves, the returned card is a new object rather than the original unit.<Rule number="124" />

## Can a unit or gear without an Empower or Empowered ability become Empowered? [#empower-other-objects]

Yes, if a game effect instructs you to empower it.<Rule number="442.3.a" />
A unit or gear does not need a printed <Empower /> or <Empowered /> ability because the <Empowered /> state can apply to any game object on the board.<Rule number="442.2" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	<Card name="Sanction" /> can empower an unempowered legal unit and <Card name="Hextech Formula" /> can empower another gear; neither object needs a printed reference to <Empower /> or <Empowered />.
</Callout>
