## Where can I play a gear with Deploy? [#play-locations]

You can play a gear with <Deploy /> only to a battlefield.<Rule number="830.1.b" />
That permission covers any battlefield regardless of who controls it or whether units occupy it.<Rule number="830.1.b.1" />

## Can an effect play a gear with Deploy to a base? [#base-replay]

No.
While the gear has <Deploy />, it can be played only to a battlefield.<Rule number="054.2" /><Rule number="830.1.b" />

Suppose a gear with <Deploy /> that <Card name="Lady of Clockwork" /> has made into a unit gear is currently at base.
When <Card name="Temporal Breach" /> banishes it, Temporal Breach instructs its owner to play it back to that base.
The gear fails to <Term item="finalization">finalize</Term> because <Deploy /> restricts it to battlefields.<Rule number="355.17" /><Rule number="355.17.b" /><Rule number="358.1" />
The failed play is undone, returning the card to its owner's banishment.<Rule number="358.5" />
