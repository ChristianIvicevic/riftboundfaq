## What happens to attached equipment when a unit dies? [#equipment-after-death]

The equipment detaches at the unit's last location when the unit dies.<Rule number="719.5" /><Rule number="435.4.b" />

If that location is a battlefield, the detached equipment is recalled to its controller's base during the next cleanup.<Rule number="435.4.a" />
If that location is a base the equipment's controller does not control, the equipment is recalled to its controller's base during cleanup.<Rule number="323.7" />
If it is already at its controller's base, it remains there.<Rule number="323.7" />

## What happens to attached equipment when I take control of an enemy unit? [#equipment-control-change]

The equipment stays attached and keeps its existing controller; you take control only of the unit.<Rule number="718.1" /><Rule number="718.5.f" />

The equipment's controller can still choose it with <Weaponmaster /> while it is attached to the unit you now control.<Rule number="718.5.b" /><Rule number="821.1.c" />
If its <Equip /> cost can be paid and it can legally be detached and attached, it detaches and attaches to the newly played unit.<Rule number="821.1.c" /><Rule number="821.1.c.5" /><Rule number="434.1.f" />

## Do Equip, Quick-Draw, or Weaponmaster count as choosing a unit? [#equipment-unit-selection]

<Equip /> and <QuickDraw /> count as you choosing a unit because they target that unit; <Weaponmaster /> has you choose equipment, not the unit with <Weaponmaster />.<Rule number="383.4.b.3" />

- <Equip />: You choose a unit you control as the ability's target.<Rule number="818.1.b" /><Rule number="818.1.b.1" /><Rule number="818.1.c.2" />
- <QuickDraw />: Attaching does not inherently target, but the <QuickDraw /> ability mentions the unit it will attach to and therefore targets that unit.<Rule number="434.3" /><Rule number="819.1.d" /><Rule number="355.10" />
- <Weaponmaster />: You choose equipment, not the unit with <Weaponmaster />.<Rule number="821.1.c" /><Rule number="821.1.c.6" />

## Can opponents react to Equip, Quick-Draw, or Weaponmaster abilities? [#equipment-reactions]

Opponents can react before <Equip />, <QuickDraw />, or <Weaponmaster /> attaches equipment, but they cannot react before the <QuickDraw /> gear itself enters the board.

<Equip /> is an activated ability that uses the chain.<Rule number="818.1" /><Rule number="377.3" />
Other players have an opportunity to play Reactions before it resolves.<Rule number="406.4" />

The <QuickDraw /> gear resolves immediately when finalized and enters the board before its triggered ability is put on the chain.<Rule number="337.1.a" /><Rule number="337.2" /><Rule number="383.4.a.2" /><Rule number="819.1.d" />
Other players can react before that ability resolves and attaches the gear.<Rule number="406.4" />

<Weaponmaster /> is also a play effect put on the chain after its unit enters.<Rule number="821.1.b" /><Rule number="383.4.a.2" />
Other players can react before it resolves and attaches the chosen equipment.<Rule number="406.4" />
