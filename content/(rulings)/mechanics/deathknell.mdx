## When is a Deathknell ability added to the chain? [#trigger-timing]

A <Deathknell /> ability is added to the chain before its permanent moves to the trash.<Rule number="808.1.d.2" />
At that point it is a pending item, an ability waiting to be finalized.
Before the permanent leaves the board, the game records its location, attributes, and other information needed to process the ability.<Rule number="808.1.d.3" />

If the permanent's death is replaced and it is not sent to the trash, the pending ability is removed from the chain.<Rule number="808.1.d.1" />
Each instance of <Deathknell /> triggers separately, and the permanent's controller chooses the order in which those pending abilities are added.<Rule number="808.2" /><Rule number="808.2.a" />

## When does a Deathknell ability resolve if its unit dies during a cleanup? [#cleanup-timing]

It resolves only after the cleanup finishes.
During a combat cleanup, surviving units are therefore healed before the <Deathknell /> ability's controller chooses targets or the ability resolves.<Rule number="466.1.a.1" /><Rule number="402.2" /><Rule number="320" />

The ability becomes a pending item before the unit moves to the trash, but pending items cannot begin finalization until every cleanup task is complete.<Rule number="323.4" /><Rule number="323.5" /><Rule number="334.2" /><Rule number="337.1" />

## Does Deathknell resolve before the card or ability that killed the unit? [#killing-card-timing]

It depends on when the unit is killed.

- **Killed to pay a cost while a spell or ability is being finalized:** The <Deathknell /> resolves first.
  The original item is already pending when its cost is paid.<Rule number="354.2" /><Rule number="357.2" /><Rule number="401.1" /><Rule number="404.1" />
  It finishes finalizing before the newer <Deathknell />, which then resolves first.<Rule number="808.1.d.2" /><Rule number="359.3.b" /><Rule number="406.3" /><Rule number="340.1" />
  For example, if <Card name="Sacrifice" /> kills <Card name="Ruined Rex" /> as its additional cost, Ruined Rex's <Deathknell /> resolves before Sacrifice draws 2 and channels 1 rune exhausted.
- **Killed during resolution:** The killing card or ability finishes first, including when the kill pays a cost within its instructions.
  Costs within instructions in spells and activated abilities, and those appearing later in triggered abilities, are paid during resolution.<Rule number="204.3.b" />
  The <Deathknell /> is added before its permanent moves to the trash, but it cannot finalize or resolve until the current item finishes.<Rule number="808.1.d.2" /><Rule number="401.2" /><Rule number="158.3" />
  For example, after <Card name="Baited Hook" /> kills a unit, it finishes every remaining instruction before that unit's <Deathknell /> can resolve.
- **Killed by lethal damage:** The card or ability that dealt the damage finishes first because cleanup cannot begin during its resolution.<Rule number="321" /><Rule number="321.1" />
  The <Deathknell /> is added during the subsequent cleanup and cannot finalize until that cleanup ends.<Rule number="323.4" /><Rule number="323.5" /><Rule number="334.2" />
