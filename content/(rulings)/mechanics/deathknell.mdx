## When is a Deathknell ability added to the chain? [#trigger-timing]

After replacement effects are processed, an active <Deathknell /> ability is added to the <Term item="chain">chain</Term> as a <Term item="pending">pending</Term> item immediately before its permanent moves to the trash, provided the permanent is still being killed and sent there.<Rule number="383.2.c.3" /><Rule number="383.2.c.3.a" /><Rule number="721.2" /><Rule number="808.1.d.2" />
Before the permanent leaves the board, the game records its location, attributes, and other information needed to process the ability.<Rule number="808.1.d.3" />

Each instance of <Deathknell /> triggers separately.<Rule number="808.2" />
The permanent's controller chooses the order in which those pending abilities are added.<Rule number="808.2.a" />

## When does a Deathknell ability resolve if its unit dies during a cleanup? [#cleanup-timing]

It <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term> only after the <Term item="cleanup">cleanup</Term> finishes.<Rule number="320" />
During a combat cleanup, surviving units are therefore healed before the <Deathknell /> ability's controller chooses targets or the ability resolves.<Rule number="468.1.a.1" /><Rule number="403.2" />

The ability becomes a <Term item="pending">pending</Term> item before the unit moves to the trash.<Rule number="808.1.d.2" />
It cannot begin <Term item="finalization">finalization</Term> until all outstanding tasks are complete.<Rule number="334.2" />

## Does Deathknell resolve before the card or ability that killed the unit? [#killing-card-timing]

It depends on when the unit is killed.

- **Killed to pay a cost while a spell or ability is being <Term item="finalization">finalized</Term>:** The <Deathknell /> <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term> first.
  The original item is already <Term item="pending">pending</Term> when its cost is paid.<Rule number="354.2" /><Rule number="357.2" /><Rule number="402.1" /><Rule number="405.1" />
  It finishes finalizing before the newer <Deathknell />, which then resolves first.<Rule number="808.1.d.2" /><Rule number="359.3.b" /><Rule number="407.3" /><Rule number="340.1" />
  For example, suppose <Card name="Sacrifice" /> kills <Card name="Ruined Rex" /> as its additional cost and Ruined Rex's <Deathknell /> has a legal enemy unit to choose.<Rule number="403.4" />
  The <Deathknell /> resolves before Sacrifice draws 2 and channels 1 rune exhausted.
- **Killed during resolution:** The killing card or ability finishes first, including when the kill pays a cost within its instructions.
  Costs within instructions in spells and activated abilities, and those appearing later in triggered abilities, are paid during resolution.<Rule number="204.3.a" />
  The <Deathknell /> is added before its permanent moves to the trash.<Rule number="808.1.d.2" />
  It cannot finalize or resolve until the current item finishes.<Rule number="158.3" />
  For example, after <Card name="Baited Hook" /> kills a unit, it finishes every remaining instruction before that unit's <Deathknell /> can resolve.
- **A resolving card or ability leaves the unit with lethal damage:** The current item finishes first because <Term item="cleanup">cleanup</Term> cannot begin during its resolution.<Rule number="321" /><Rule number="321.1" />
  If the unit has lethal damage when cleanup reaches step 3a, it is killed then.<Rule number="323.4" />
  Its <Deathknell /> is added before the unit moves to the trash.<Rule number="808.1.d.2" />
  The <Deathknell /> cannot finalize until all outstanding tasks are complete.<Rule number="334.2" />
