## When is a Deathknell ability added to the chain? [#trigger-timing]

A <Deathknell /> ability is added to the chain before its permanent moves to the trash.<Rule number="808.1.d.2" />
At that point it is a pending item, an ability waiting to be finalized.
Before the permanent leaves the board, the game records its location, attributes, and other information needed to process the ability after finalization.<Rule number="808.1.d.3" />

If the permanent's death is replaced and it is not sent to the trash, the pending ability is removed from the chain.<Rule number="808.1.d.1" />
Each instance of <Deathknell /> triggers separately, and the permanent's controller chooses the order in which those pending abilities are added.<Rule number="808.2" /><Rule number="808.2.a" />

## When does a Deathknell ability resolve if its unit dies during a cleanup? [#cleanup-timing]

If it resolves, it resolves after the cleanup finishes.
The ability becomes a pending item, an ability waiting to be finalized, before the unit moves to the trash.<Rule number="323.4" /><Rule number="323.5" /><Rule number="808.1.d.2" />
Pending chain items remain unprocessed until every outstanding cleanup task is complete.<Rule number="334.1" /><Rule number="334.2" />

During a combat cleanup, surviving units are therefore healed before the <Deathknell /> ability is finalized or resolved.<Rule number="466.1.a.1" />
After the cleanup, the ability proceeds through finalization and any required choices, such as targets, are made.<Rule number="402.2" />
Once no pending items remain, the controller of the next chain item gains priority, and resolution waits until all players pass without adding another item.<Rule number="337.4" /><Rule number="339.1" />

## Does Deathknell resolve before the card or ability that killed the unit? [#killing-card-timing]

Assuming both relevant items resolve, <Deathknell /> resolves first only when the unit was killed to pay the cost of a spell or an ability that does not add resources.
If that cost belonged to a unit, gear, or ability that adds resources, that item resolves first.
If the unit was killed by an instruction while a spell or ability was resolving, the killing item finishes first.

### Killed as a cost [#killed-as-cost]

Assuming both relevant items resolve, <Deathknell /> resolves before a spell or non-resource-adding ability whose cost killed the unit, but after a unit, gear, or resource-adding ability whose cost did so.
The kill happens while the original card or ability is being finalized.<Rule number="357.2" /><Rule number="404.1" />
The resulting <Deathknell /> is added afterward as a pending item, an ability waiting to be finalized.<Rule number="808.1.d.2" />

When the original item is a spell or an ability that does not add resources, it finishes finalizing before the pending items, including the <Deathknell />, are processed.<Rule number="359.3.b" /><Rule number="406.3" />
The <Deathknell /> is newer than the original item after both are finalized, so it resolves first.<Rule number="340.1" />

A unit, gear, or ability that adds resources instead resolves immediately upon finalization, before the pending <Deathknell /> is processed.<Rule number="337.2" />
After that immediate resolution, the chain returns to its pending items and the <Deathknell /> can be finalized.<Rule number="340.3" />

### Killed during resolution [#killed-during-resolution]

Assuming both items resolve, the killing card or ability finishes before the <Deathknell /> resolves.
While a spell or ability is resolving, no other spell or ability can be finalized or resolved, including a triggered ability created by that resolution.<Rule number="158.3" />
The <Deathknell /> is still added as a pending item before its permanent moves to the trash, but its finalization waits for the current effect to finish.<Rule number="808.1.d.2" /><Rule number="401.2" />
The game finishes every effect of the killing item before addressing the new chain item.<Rule number="158.3.a" />
