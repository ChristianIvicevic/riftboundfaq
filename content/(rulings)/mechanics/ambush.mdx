## Can I use Ambush to play a unit to my base? [#ambush-base]

No.
<Ambush /> applies only when you play the unit to a battlefield where you control units: it makes that battlefield a valid location and gives the unit <Reaction /> while it is being played there.<Rule number="822.1.b" /><Rule number="822.1.c" />
You may still play the unit to your base under the normal unit-play permission, but only at its normal timing.<Rule number="355.2.a" />

## Can opponents react to a unit being played with Ambush? [#unit-play-reactions]

No.
<Ambush /> changes when the unit can be played,<Rule number="822.1.b" /> but the unit otherwise enters normally.
Finalizing it does not pass priority.<Rule number="337.1.a" />
It then resolves and enters the board immediately.<Rule number="337.2" /><Rule number="359.2.c" />

This is a common point of confusion with cards like <Card name="Kha'Zix, Mutating Horror" />, which has <Ambush /> and triggers if an enemy unit is alone when he attacks or defends.
An opponent cannot play a unit in response to Kha'Zix being placed on the chain in an attempt to break the "alone" condition before he enters.

## Who gets to act after I Ambush a unit during a showdown? [#ambush-showdown-focus]

After the <Ambush /> unit enters, ask whether anything remains on the chain.

- **Nothing remains on the chain:** If the unit started the chain and no ability triggered by its entry remains, the chain closes and focus passes to the next player.<Rule number="309.2" /><Rule number="347.1.b" />
- **Another item remains on the chain:** An ability triggered by the unit's entry can finalize and remain.<Rule number="383.2.c" /><Rule number="383.3" /><Rule number="406.2" />
  An older item can remain if the unit was played in response to it.<Rule number="337.2" /><Rule number="340.1" />
  The newest remaining item's controller gains priority, and focus does not pass.<Rule number="309.1" /><Rule number="337.4" /><Rule number="340.4" />
