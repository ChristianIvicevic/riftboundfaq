## Can I use Ambush to play a unit to my base? [#ambush-base]

No.
Choosing your base uses the normal unit-play permission.<Rule number="355.2.a" />

<Ambush /> is shorthand for "I may be played to a battlefield where you control units" and "I have <Reaction /> as long as I'm being played to a battlefield where you control units."<Rule number="822.1.b" />
Both the additional location permission and the conditional <Reaction /> apply only while the unit is being played to a battlefield where you control units.<Rule number="822.1.c" />

## Can opponents react to a unit being played with Ambush? [#unit-play-reactions]

No.
<Ambush /> grants the unit <Reaction /> timing, which determines when it can be played but does not give opponents priority before it enters.

Only the unit's controller can act before entry, and only by activating a <Reaction /> ability that uses <Add /> while paying the unit's costs.<Rule number="357.1.a" /><Rule number="429.3" />
Otherwise, finalizing the unit does not pass priority.<Rule number="337.1.a" />
The unit then resolves immediately and enters the board.<Rule number="337.2" /><Rule number="359.2.c" />

This is a common point of confusion with cards like <Card name="Kha'Zix, Mutating Horror" />, which has <Ambush /> and triggers if an enemy unit is alone when he attacks or defends.
An opponent cannot play a unit in response to Kha'Zix being placed on the chain in an attempt to break the "alone" condition before he enters.

## Who gets to act after I Ambush a unit during a showdown? [#ambush-showdown-focus]

After the <Ambush /> unit enters, the game processes the resulting cleanup and any pending items, cards or abilities waiting to finalize, before a player acts.
If the chain then empties and the unit started it, focus passes to the next player; if a finalized item, an item waiting to resolve, remains, its controller gains priority and focus does not pass.

Unit entry makes a cleanup outstanding, and abilities triggered by that entry can become pending items.<Rule number="319.6" /><Rule number="383.2.c" /><Rule number="383.3" />
Priority is not granted during the cleanup, and pending items are processed after it finishes.<Rule number="320" /><Rule number="334.2" />

If this processing leaves the chain empty and the <Ambush /> unit started it, the showdown returns to an open state and focus passes.<Rule number="340.2" /><Rule number="340.2.a" />

If a finalized item remains, including an older item that the unit was played in response to, the chain stays closed.<Rule number="309.1" />
The controller of the newest remaining item gains priority instead.<Rule number="337.4" /><Rule number="340.4" />
