## Can I use Ambush's Reaction timing at my base or a battlefield where I control no units? [#ambush-base]

Not from the standard <Ambush /> keyword alone: it grants the unit <Reaction /> only while you are playing it to a battlefield where you control units.<Rule number="822.1.b" /><Rule number="813.4" />
Normal unit-play permission makes your base a valid location,<Rule number="355.2.a" /> and <Card name="Last Caress" /> can make a battlefield where you control no units valid.<Rule number="355.2.b" />
Neither separate location permission expands <Ambush /> or satisfies its condition for granting <Reaction />.

An ability can explicitly expand <Ambush /> to other battlefields; when it does, <Ambush /> can grant <Reaction /> there.<Rule number="822.1.d" />
If the unit independently has or gains <Reaction />, you may instead rely on that separate timing permission.<Rule number="813.1.c.1" />

## Can opponents react to a unit being played with Ambush? [#unit-play-reactions]

No.
<Ambush /> changes when the unit can be played,<Rule number="822.1.b" /> but the unit otherwise enters normally.
<Term item="finalization">Finalizing</Term> it does not pass <Term item="priority">priority</Term>.<Rule number="337.1.a" />
It then <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term> and enters the board immediately.<Rule number="337.2" /><Rule number="359.2.c" />

This is a common point of confusion with cards like <Card name="Kha'Zix, Mutating Horror" />, which has <Ambush /> and triggers when he attacks or defends if an enemy unit is alone at his battlefield.
An opponent cannot play a unit in response to Kha'Zix being placed on the <Term item="chain">chain</Term> in an attempt to break the "alone" condition before he enters.

## Who gets to act after I Ambush a unit during a showdown? [#ambush-showdown-focus]

After the <Ambush /> unit enters, its entry causes a <Term item="cleanup">cleanup</Term>, during which <Term item="priority">priority</Term> and <Term item="focus">focus</Term> are not passed or awarded.<Rule number="319.6" /><Rule number="320.1" />
After that cleanup, ask whether anything remains on the <Term item="chain">chain</Term>.

- **Nothing remains on the chain:** If the unit started the chain and no other item remains after cleanup, the chain closes,<Rule number="330" /> and the next player gains focus and priority.<Rule number="347.1.b" /><Rule number="313.2" />
- **Another item remains on the chain:** An ability triggered by the unit's entry can <Term item="finalization">finalize</Term> and remain.<Rule number="383.2.c" /><Rule number="383.3" /><Rule number="407.2" />
  An older item can remain if the unit was played in response to it.<Rule number="337.2" /><Rule number="340.1" />
  The newest remaining item's controller gains priority, and focus does not pass.<Rule number="337.4" /><Rule number="340.4" /><Rule number="346" />
