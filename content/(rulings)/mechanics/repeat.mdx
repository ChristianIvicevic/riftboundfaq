## When do I decide whether to pay for Repeat? [#repeat-decision-timing]

You decide whether to pay a spell's <Repeat /> cost as part of playing it, before finalization finishes and players can respond.<Rule number="820.1.d" />
During this process, the spell is already on the chain as a pending item, meaning it is still completing the steps needed to become finalized.<Rule number="354.2" />
Once the spell is finalized, you cannot decide to pay the cost later.

In rules terms, <Repeat /> is an optional additional cost.<Rule number="820.1" />
You choose whether to pay it during the Make Relevant Choices step.<Rule number="355.1" /><Rule number="820.1.d" />
During this step, you also make any targets, modes, or other choices for the additional time the spell's instructions will be executed.
The rules call this the additional execution, and its choices may differ from those for the original execution.<Rule number="820.2" /><Rule number="820.2.a" />

## What does Repeat repeat? [#repeated-instructions]

<Repeat /> executes a spell or ability's instructions an additional time during resolution; it does not create another copy or chain item.<Rule number="820.1.d.1" /><Rule number="820.3.a" />
The spell or ability is played only once, so costs paid while playing it, including the <Repeat /> cost, are not paid a second time.<Rule number="820.3.a" />

Targets, modes, and other choices are made separately for each execution.<Rule number="820.2" /><Rule number="820.2.a" />
Those choices may differ between the original and additional executions.

## What happens if a spell with Repeat is countered? [#repeated-spell-countering]

The entire spell is countered, so neither its original instructions nor any additional execution from <Repeat /> happens.<Rule number="425.1.a" />

<Repeat /> does *not* create a second instance of the spell on the chain.<Rule number="820.3.a" />
Instead, it modifies how the spell resolves by executing its instructions an additional time.<Rule number="820.1.d.1" />
