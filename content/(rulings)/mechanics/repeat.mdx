## When do I decide whether to pay for Repeat? [#repeat-decision-timing]

Decide whether to pay <Repeat /> as you play the spell or ability, before opponents can respond.

<Steps>
<Step>
While making the relevant choices, choose whether to pay the <Repeat /> cost.<Rule number="355.1.a" /><Rule number="399" /><Rule number="820.1.d" />
</Step>
<Step>
If you choose to pay it, include <Repeat /> as an optional additional cost when determining the total cost.<Rule number="356.2.b.1" /><Rule number="399" /><Rule number="820.1.d" />
</Step>
<Step>
Pay the total cost and finish finalization.<Rule number="357" /><Rule number="359.3.a" /><Rule number="406.2" />
Finalization does not pass priority, so opponents cannot respond during these steps.<Rule number="337.1.a" />
</Step>
<Step>
Once a <Repeat /> spell or ability is finalized and no pending items remain, the normal response window opens.<Rule number="337.4" /><Rule number="406.4" />
The <Repeat /> decision is already locked in.<Rule number="355.15" />
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	When playing <Card name="Blood Rush" />, choose whether to pay its <Repeat /> <Energy value={1} />.
	If you pay, separately choose a target for each execution; both choices are locked in before you pay the spell's cost.<Rule number="820.2" /><Rule number="820.2.a" />
	Include the extra <Energy value={1} /> in the total cost and pay it before finalizing the spell.
	Only then can an opponent respond.
</Callout>

## What does Repeat repeat? [#repeated-instructions]

<Repeat /> executes a spell or ability's instructions an additional time during the same resolution; it does not create another copy or chain item.<Rule number="820.1.d.1" /><Rule number="820.3.a" />
Instructions handled outside resolution are not repeated.<Rule number="820.1.d.2" />
The spell or ability is played only once, so you determine and pay its play costs, including the <Repeat /> cost, only once.<Rule number="820.1.c.1" /><Rule number="820.1.c.3" /><Rule number="820.3.a" />

You choose targets and modes and make other choices separately for each execution.<Rule number="820.2" /><Rule number="820.2.a" />
You may make different choices for the original and additional executions.

## What happens if a spell with Repeat is countered? [#repeated-spell-countering]

Neither the initial nor any additional execution occurs, and the paid <Repeat /> cost is not refunded.

<Repeat /> does *not* create a second instance of the spell on the chain.<Rule number="820.3.a" />
Instead, it modifies how the spell resolves by executing its instructions an additional time.<Rule number="820.1.d.1" />

When a spell is countered, it does nothing and is cleared from the chain, so none of its executions happen.<Rule number="425.1.a" />
Countering does not refund additional costs paid to play the spell.<Rule number="425.1.c" /><Rule number="425.1.c.1" />
