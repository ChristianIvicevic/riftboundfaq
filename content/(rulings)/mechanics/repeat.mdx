## When do I decide whether to pay for Repeat? [#repeat-decision-timing]

Decide whether to pay a spell's <Repeat /> cost while making its relevant choices during finalization, before determining and paying its total cost.<Rule number="355.1.a" /><Rule number="820.1.d" />

If you choose to pay it, the <Repeat /> cost is included as an optional additional cost when the spell's total cost is determined.<Rule number="356.2.b.1" />
The spell's costs are then paid and its legality is checked before it finishes finalizing.<Rule number="357" /><Rule number="358" /><Rule number="359" />
Finalization does not pass priority, so you cannot wait for reactions before making the <Repeat /> decision.<Rule number="337.1.a" />

See [What is the process for playing a card?](/general-rules/playing-cards#play-process) for the complete play process.

## What does Repeat repeat? [#repeated-instructions]

<Repeat /> executes a spell or ability's instructions an additional time during the same resolution; it does not create another copy or chain item.<Rule number="820.1.d.1" /><Rule number="820.3.a" />
Instructions not performed during resolution are not repeated.<Rule number="820.1.d.2" />
The spell or ability is played only once, so the costs paid to play it, including the <Repeat /> cost, are determined and paid once.<Rule number="820.1.c.1" /><Rule number="820.1.c.3" /><Rule number="820.3.a" />

Targets, modes, and other choices are made separately for each execution.<Rule number="820.2" /><Rule number="820.2.a" />
Those choices may differ between the original and additional executions.

## What happens if a spell with Repeat is countered? [#repeated-spell-countering]

Neither the initial nor any additional execution occurs, and the paid <Repeat /> cost is not refunded.

<Repeat /> does *not* create a second instance of the spell on the chain.<Rule number="820.3.a" />
Instead, it modifies how the spell resolves by executing its instructions an additional time.<Rule number="820.1.d.1" />

When a spell is countered, it does nothing and is cleared from the chain, so none of its executions happen.<Rule number="425.1.a" />
Countering does not refund additional costs paid to play the spell.<Rule number="425.1.c" /><Rule number="425.1.c.1" />
