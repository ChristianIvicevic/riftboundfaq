## When do I decide whether to Show Off? [#decision-timing]

Decide whether to use <ShowOff /> while the card is being <Term item="finalization">finalized</Term>, not when it <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term>.
<ShowOff /> is shorthand for an optional "As you play" action.<Rule number="832.1.c" />
Its controller makes that choice during finalization.<Rule number="832.1.c.2" />

## Does picking a game object for Show Off trigger abilities that care when it is chosen? [#pick-targeting]

No.
Picking a game object for <ShowOff /> does not trigger an ability that cares when that object is chosen.
Abilities whose condition says "When you choose" trigger from that choice specifically when the appropriate game object is targeted.<Rule number="383.4.b.3" />
For board objects, <ShowOff /> tells you to pick a friendly object.<Rule number="832.1.c" />
Picked objects are not targeted.<Rule number="355.7" />

For example, picking <Card name="Irelia, Fervent" /> does not trigger her ability.
Picking a friendly unit does not trigger <Card name="Blade Dancer" /> or <Card name="Spirit Wheel" />.
Picking <Card name="Jae Medarda" /> does not trigger his ability even if the card with <ShowOff /> is a spell.

## Can I Show Off my chosen champion from my chosen champion zone? [#champion-zone]

No.
<ShowOff /> can reveal a qualifying card only from your hand or pick a qualifying friendly game object only from the board.<Rule number="832.1.c" />
The chosen champion zone is a non-board zone, so a chosen champion there is available through neither option.<Rule number="108" /><Rule number="108.3" />

## Can I use a facedown card to Show Off a card with Hidden? [#facedown-hidden]

No.
A facedown card is not a card with <Hidden />, so it cannot satisfy a <ShowOff /> condition that requires that characteristic.<Rule number="811.5" /><Rule number="811.5.a" />
For <Card name="Gutter Guardian" />, you may instead reveal a card with <Hidden /> from your hand or pick a non-facedown friendly card with <Hidden /> on the board.<Rule number="832.1.b.1" /><Rule number="832.1.c" />
