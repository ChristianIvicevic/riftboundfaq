## Can opponents react to an Empower ability? [#empower-reactions]

Yes.
<Empower /> is an activated ability keyword,<Rule number="827.1" /> and activated abilities use the chain.<Rule number="377.3" />
After the ability is finalized and no other cards or abilities are waiting to finalize, opponents have an opportunity to play Reactions before it resolves.<Rule number="337.4" /><Rule number="406.4" />

## When can I activate an Empower ability? [#empower-timing]

By default, you can activate it only while its source is not <Empowered /> and during your own Main Phase while no showdown, combat, or chain is in progress.<Rule number="310.1" /><Rule number="310.1.a" /><Rule number="312.2.a" /><Rule number="381" /><Rule number="827.1.c.1" />
<Empower /> follows these restrictions because it is an activated ability.<Rule number="827.1" />

Card text may override the default activation restriction or change an <Empower /> ability's timing.<Rule number="002" /><Rule number="827.1.c.4" />
If it explicitly has <Action />, you may activate it during showdowns on any player's turn.<Rule number="806.1.c.2" />
If it has <Reaction />, you may also activate it on any player's turn during closed states, while a chain exists.<Rule number="309.1" /><Rule number="813.1.b" /><Rule number="813.1.c.2" />

## Does a "When I become Empowered" ability trigger if the unit leaves the board before its Empower ability resolves? [#removed-source-trigger]

No.
If the unit leaves the board before its <Empower /> ability resolves, it does not become <Empowered />, so its "When I become <Empowered />" ability does not trigger.<Rule number="441.2" /><Rule number="828.1.d" />

Removing the unit does not counter its already-finalized <Empower /> ability, so that ability remains on the chain.<Rule number="406.2" /><Rule number="425.1" /><Rule number="425.2.a" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	You activate <Card name="Mel, Defiant Soul" />'s <Empower /> ability and discard a spell to pay its cost.
	Your opponent responds with <Card name="Star-Crossed" />, returning Mel and one of their own units, each to its owner's hand.
	Star-Crossed resolves first, so Mel leaves the board while her <Empower /> ability remains on the chain.<Rule number="340.1" />
	Mel's <Empower /> ability then resolves, but Mel cannot become <Empowered /> while she is off the board, so her banish ability does not trigger.
</Callout>

When the <Empower /> ability resolves, it tries to empower the unit whose ability was activated.<Rule number="406.5" /><Rule number="827.1.c.1" />
Only a game object on the board can become <Empowered />.
While the original unit is off the board, it cannot become <Empowered /> or evaluate its triggered ability's condition.<Rule number="384.2" />

If the card returns from a non-board zone before the <Empower /> ability resolves, the returned card is a new object rather than the original unit.<Rule number="124" />

## Can a unit or gear without an Empower or Empowered ability become Empowered? [#empower-other-objects]

Yes.
A unit or gear does not need a printed <Empower /> or <Empowered /> ability to become <Empowered />.

You can empower an object only when a game effect instructs you to do so.<Rule number="441.3.a" />
The <Empowered /> state can apply to any game object on the board.<Rule number="441.2" />

Normally, an object that is already <Empowered /> cannot become <Empowered /> again.<Rule number="441.1.b" />
An effect can permit an already <Empowered /> object to be empowered again, subject to the limits of that effect.<Rule number="441.1.c.1" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	<Card name="Sanction" /> can empower an unempowered legal unit and <Card name="Hextech Formula" /> can empower another gear; neither object needs a printed reference to <Empower /> or <Empowered />.
</Callout>
