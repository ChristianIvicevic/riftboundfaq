## Can opponents react to an Empower ability? [#empower-reactions]

Yes.
<Empower /> is an activated ability keyword,<Rule number="827.1" /> and activated abilities use the chain.<Rule number="377.3" />
After the ability is finalized and no pending items remain, opponents have an opportunity to play Reactions before it resolves.<Rule number="337.4" /><Rule number="406.4" />
See also [Can I react to triggered and activated abilities?](/general-rules/abilities#ability-reactions).

## When can I activate an Empower ability? [#empower-timing]

By default, you can activate it only during your own Main Phase while no showdown, combat, or chain is in progress.
An <Empower /> ability is an activated ability,<Rule number="827.1" /> and activated abilities can normally be activated only on their controller's turn during an open state.<Rule number="316.5.b" /><Rule number="381" />
Because <Empower /> has neither <Action /> nor <Reaction /> by default, it cannot be activated during a showdown or in response to another chain item.<Rule number="308.1.a" /><Rule number="309.1.a" />

An <Empower /> ability may have text that changes its timing.<Rule number="827.1.c.4" />
If it explicitly has <Action /> or <Reaction />, follow the permissions of that keyword instead.

## Does a "When I become Empowered" ability trigger if the unit leaves the board before its Empower ability resolves? [#removed-source-trigger]

No.
The <Empower /> ability still resolves after its source leaves the board,<Rule number="377.3" /><Rule number="406.5" /> but its source cannot become <Empowered /> because that state applies only to game objects on the board.<Rule number="441.2" />
The unit's triggered ability also cannot have its condition evaluated while its source is off the board, so its "When I become <Empowered />" ability does not trigger.<Rule number="384.2" /><Rule number="828.1.d" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	A player activates <Card name="Mel, Defiant Soul" />'s <Empower /> ability by discarding a spell.
	An opponent responds with <Card name="Star-Crossed" /> and returns Mel to her owner's hand.
	When the <Empower /> ability resolves, Mel is no longer a game object on the board and does not become <Empowered />.
	Her "When I become <Empowered />" ability does not trigger, so no enemy unit is banished.
</Callout>

## Can a unit or gear without an Empower or Empowered ability become Empowered? [#empower-other-objects]

Yes, when a game effect instructs a player to empower it<Rule number="441.3.a" />, it is on the board<Rule number="441.2" />, and it is not already <Empowered /><Rule number="441.1.b" />, unless that effect allows it to be empowered multiple times.<Rule number="441.1.c.1" />

The <Empowered /> state can apply to any game object on the board; the object does not need a printed <Empower /> or <Empowered /> ability.<Rule number="441.1" /><Rule number="441.2" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Examples">
	<Card name="Sanction" /> can empower any unit that is a legal choice, even if that unit has no printed reference to <Empower /> or <Empowered />.
	Once that unit is <Empowered />, effects such as <Card name="Aurok General" />'s ability can identify and affect it normally.

	<Card name="Hextech Formula" /> can empower another gear, even if that gear has no printed reference to <Empower /> or <Empowered />.
</Callout>
