## When can I play a spell using Flow? [#play-timing]

You can play it from your trash only when you otherwise have permission to play it at that timing.

<Flow /> lets you play the spell from your trash for its <Flow /> cost.<Rule number="829.1.b" />
It changes no other permissions or timing restrictions.<Rule number="829.1.b.2" />
For example, <Flow /> does not grant a spell <Reaction /> or let you play an <Action /> outside its normal timing.

## Where does a spell played using Flow go if it is countered? [#countered-destination]

It is banished unless another replacement effect changes that result.<Rule number="370.2" /><Rule number="372" />

An effect that says "Counter a spell" can target only a <Term item="finalization">finalized</Term> spell on the <Term item="chain">chain</Term>.<Rule number="355.10" /><Rule number="355.9.a.2" />
Countering clears the spell from the chain and would normally put it in the trash.<Rule number="426.1.a" /><Rule number="426.1.a.1" />
However, playing a spell using <Flow /> creates a delayed replacement effect that banishes it if it would leave the chain after becoming finalized, unless its own execution instructed it to leave.<Rule number="829.1.b.1" />
Countering, rather than the spell's own execution, makes it leave the chain, so the <Flow /> replacement effect banishes it instead.

## Which cost do effects use when they check a Flow spell's cost? [#printed-cost]

Effects use the spell's printed or copied cost, not its <Flow /> cost or the amount paid.<Rule number="206" />

A <Flow /> cost is an alternate cost that replaces the spell's base cost during <Term item="finalization">finalization</Term>.<Rule number="829.1.c.1" />
For example, <Card name="Defy" /> can counter the spell if its printed or copied cost is no more than <Energy value={4} /> and no more than <Universal />, even when its <Flow /> cost exceeds either limit.
