## Does a unit move when it enters the board? [#entering-board]

No.
Moving means changing a permanent's position from one space on the board to another.<Rule number="446.1" />
A card entering the board from another game zone is a zone change, not a move.<Rule number="446.2" />

Playing or creating a unit at a battlefield therefore does not fulfill an ability that triggers when the unit moves there.
Replacing where a unit enters the board also does not turn that entry into a move.

## Can I choose a unit's current location as its move destination? [#chosen-destination]

No.
A destination you choose must differ from the unit's current location, but if the card specifies the destination, you make no destination choice and the unit can still be a legal target while already there.<Rule number="002" /><Rule number="355.4" /><Rule number="355.4.a" /><Rule number="355.9.a" /><Rule number="355.9.b" />
If the unit is already at that specified destination when the instruction resolves, it cannot move because it would not change board spaces.<Rule number="446.1" />

## Does a later linked instruction still execute if a move is prevented or cannot happen? [#failed-move-linked-instructions]

If the move is prevented or cannot happen for a reason other than an illegal or unavailable target, yes, unless the linked instruction specifically requires the move to have occurred.
The [Vendetta FAQ](https://playriftbound.com/en-us/news/rules-and-releases/vendetta-rules-faq-and-clarifications) classifies the failed move as negated rather than ignored, so it does not cause other linked instructions to be ignored.

<Callout type="warn" title="Rules citation needed">
	Core Rules 1.4 still describes a move prevented by a location restriction as ignored.<Rule number="359.3.e.6" />
	It would therefore also ignore a later linked instruction under the current Core Rules wording.<Rule number="359.3.e.14.a" />
	The Vendetta FAQ takes precedence on this point until a later Core Rules Document says otherwise.
</Callout>
