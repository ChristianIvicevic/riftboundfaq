## Can triggered abilities trigger on an opponent's turn without Reaction? [#triggered-ability-timing]

Yes.
A triggered ability is put on the <Term item="chain">chain</Term> whenever its condition is met, regardless of whose turn it is, so it does not need <Reaction />.<Rule number="383.3" /><Rule number="383.3.c" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	<Card name="Blade Dancer" /> triggers whenever its controller's spell or ability targets a friendly unit.<Rule number="383.4.b.2" /><Rule number="383.4.b.3" />
	This can happen on an opponent's turn when you play a <Reaction /> spell that targets one of your units.
</Callout>

## Can I react to triggered and activated abilities? [#ability-reactions]

Yes, except for triggered and activated abilities that use <Add />.

Triggered and activated abilities use the <Term item="chain">chain</Term>.<Rule number="377.3" /><Rule number="383.3" />
They become <Term item="finalization">finalized</Term> chain items before resolving.<Rule number="407.2" />
Once no <Term item="pending">pending</Term> items remain, players have an opportunity to play legally timed <Reaction /> cards and abilities before those abilities <Term item="resolution">resolve</Term>.<Rule number="337.4" /><Rule number="407.4" />

A triggered or activated ability that uses <Add /> instead finalizes and resolves immediately without passing <Term item="priority">priority</Term>.<Rule number="430.2" /><Rule number="430.2.a" />

## Does removing the source stop an already finalized triggered ability from resolving? [#source-removal]

No.
Once the triggered ability has <Term item="finalization">finalized</Term>, it remains on the <Term item="chain">chain</Term> and <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term> like a spell even if its source leaves the board.<Rule number="407.2" /><Rule number="407.5" />

Source removal can still cause an instruction to be ignored if it needs current information from the missing source.
Source referents such as "here," "my," or "this battlefield" are normally checked when that instruction executes.<Rule number="359.3.f.1" /><Rule number="359.3.f.2" />
Information referenced from a triggered ability's trigger condition is instead recorded when the condition is fulfilled and remains available if the source later moves or leaves.<Rule number="359.3.f.3" />
If an instruction instead needs the source's current location and the source is no longer on the board, that information is unavailable, so instructions based on it are ignored.<Rule number="359.3.e.12" />

For example, if an opponent responds to <Card name="Ashe, Focused" />'s play effect with <Card name="Star-Crossed" /> and returns Ashe to her owner's hand, her ability still resolves, and all its instructions can execute because none requires Ashe to remain on the board.
By contrast, if Star-Crossed returns <Card name="Vi, Peacekeeper" /> before her attack trigger resolves, Vi cannot <Stun /> an enemy unit "here" because she no longer has a location for "here" to reference.

## When does the game check an "if" condition in a triggered ability? [#trigger-time-conditions]

An "if" immediately after a triggered ability's trigger condition is checked as part of that condition, while an "if" later in that ability's own effect is checked when its instruction executes.

- **Immediately after the trigger condition:** A trigger condition begins with "When," "At," or "The [Nth] time," and an additional conditional statement immediately following it is part of that condition.<Rule number="383.2.a" /><Rule number="383.2.a.1" />
  The game checks those requirements when it evaluates the trigger condition.<Rule number="383.2.c" />
  If they are not satisfied then, the ability is not placed on the <Term item="chain">chain</Term>.<Rule number="383.3" />
  For example, <Card name="Gutter Palace" /> checks the hand and battlefield quantities in its "if" condition at the start of its controller's Beginning Phase; if either is wrong then, its ability is not placed on the chain.
- **As part of the effect:** An "if" that belongs to the ability's own effect is checked when its instruction executes, normally during <Term item="resolution">resolution</Term> unless it states another timing.<Rule number="135.2.b.3" /><Rule number="383.2.b" />
  For example, <Card name="Loose Cannon" />'s ability is placed on the chain at the start of its controller's Beginning Phase.
  When it resolves, it checks the controller's hand size before drawing and draws only if they have one or fewer cards.

## When do I choose whether to use a triggered ability that says "you may"? [#optional-trigger-decision]

If "you may" begins the effect, choose during <Term item="finalization">finalization</Term>; if it appears later, choose during <Term item="resolution">resolution</Term>.

- **Initial "you may":** Choose while the <Term item="pending">pending</Term> ability is being finalized.<Rule number="383.3.a" />
  If you decline, the ability is removed from the <Term item="chain">chain</Term> and considered not to have triggered.<Rule number="383.3.a.2" />
  If you proceed, make its required choices.<Rule number="403.2" />
  Pay any costs incurred during finalization before the ability becomes a finalized chain item.<Rule number="405.1" /><Rule number="407.2" />
  For example, <Card name="Tideturner" /> begins its effect with "you may," so its controller decides whether to use the ability during finalization.
- **Later "you may":** If another effect instruction comes first, make the optional choice when resolution reaches that later instruction.<Rule number="383.3.a.3" />
  For example, <Card name="Ornn, Blacksmith" /> first instructs its controller to look at cards, then says "You may reveal a gear," so that choice is made during resolution.

## When do I pay a cost within a triggered ability? [#costs-within-instructions]

Pay a cost within instructions during <Term item="finalization">finalization</Term> if it begins the triggered effect or immediately follows an initial "you may";<Rule number="383.3.b" /><Rule number="383.3.b.1" /><Rule number="740.4.a.2" /> pay it during <Term item="resolution">resolution</Term> if it appears later.<Rule number="740.4.a.2.a" />
The "[do X] to [do Y]" form creates a cost within instructions: [do X] is the cost and [do Y] is its effect.<Rule number="740.4.a" />

If that cost is due during finalization and you decline to pay it, the <Term item="pending">pending</Term> ability is removed from the <Term item="chain">chain</Term> without finalizing.<Rule number="405.2" />

A separate instruction to pay resources or spend counters or XP followed by "If you do, [do Y]" is not a cost within instructions.<Rule number="205" />
That payment happens during resolution unless the instruction states another timing.<Rule number="135.2.b.3" />

For example, <Card name="Vayne, Hunter" /> says "When I conquer, you may pay <Energy value={1} /> to return me to my owner's hand."
The "pay <Energy value={1} /> to return me" form creates a cost within instructions immediately after the initial "you may," so you pay it during finalization.
If you pay, the ability finalizes and opponents may react before it resolves.<Rule number="407.4" />

By contrast, <Card name="Insightful Investigator" /> first has an opponent reveal their hand and only then offers a payment, so that cost within instructions appears later and you pay it during resolution.
