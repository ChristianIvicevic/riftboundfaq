## Can triggered abilities trigger on an opponent's turn without Reaction? [#triggered-ability-timing]

Yes.
When its condition is met, a triggered ability is placed on the chain on any player's turn, so it does not need <Reaction />.<Rule number="383.3" /><Rule number="383.3.c" />

The pending ability must still complete the remaining steps of playing before it can become finalized.<Rule number="400" /><Rule number="400.1" />
If an initial "you may" is declined, it is removed from the chain and considered not to have triggered.<Rule number="383.3.a" /><Rule number="383.3.a.2" />
It is also removed without finalizing if required legal choices are unavailable or its controller declines to pay a required trigger cost.<Rule number="402.4" /><Rule number="404.2" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	<Card name="Blade Dancer" /> triggers whenever you choose a friendly unit.
	This can happen on an opponent's turn when you play a <Reaction /> spell that chooses one of your units.
</Callout>

## Can I react to triggered and activated abilities? [#ability-reactions]

Yes, except for triggered and activated abilities that use <Add />.

Triggered and activated abilities use the chain and ordinarily become finalized chain items.<Rule number="377.3" /><Rule number="383.3" /><Rule number="406.2" />
Once no pending items remain, players have an opportunity to play Reactions before those abilities resolve.<Rule number="337.4" /><Rule number="406.4" />

An ability that adds resources instead resolves immediately after finalization without passing priority.<Rule number="337.2" /><Rule number="429.2" /><Rule number="429.2.a" />

## Does removing a triggered ability's source stop that ability? [#source-removal]

Removing the source does not by itself stop a pending or finalized triggered ability, but it can make the pending ability fail finalization or make an instruction fail if either needs the source.

When its condition is met, a triggered ability is placed on the chain as a pending item.<Rule number="383.3" /><Rule number="401.1" />
Source removal is not itself a counter instruction.<Rule number="425.1" /><Rule number="425.2.a" />
If the pending ability then lacks a required legal choice or its controller cannot pay a source-dependent cost, it is removed without finalizing.<Rule number="402.4" /><Rule number="404.2" />
Otherwise, it becomes a finalized chain item and executes like a spell.<Rule number="406.2" /><Rule number="406.5" />

Source referents such as "here," "my," or "this battlefield" are checked when the instruction executes.<Rule number="359.3.f.1" /><Rule number="359.3.f.2" />
If the source is no longer on the board, information such as its location is unavailable, and instructions that require that information are ignored.<Rule number="359.3.e.6" /><Rule number="359.3.e.12" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	If an opponent responds to <Card name="Ashe, Focused" />'s play effect with <Card name="Star-Crossed" /> and returns Ashe to her owner's hand, her ability still resolves.
	None of its instructions requires Ashe to remain on the board.

	If Star-Crossed returns <Card name="Vi, Peacekeeper" /> before her attack trigger resolves, Vi cannot <Stun /> an enemy unit "here."
	Vi no longer has a location for "here" to reference, so that instruction is ignored.
</Callout>

## When does the game check an "if" condition in a triggered ability? [#trigger-time-conditions]

Conditions immediately after a trigger condition are checked after the triggering event<Rule number="383.2.a.1" /><Rule number="383.2.c" />, whereas conditions after an effect instruction are checked during resolution.<Rule number="383.2.b" /><Rule number="359.3.d" /><Rule number="406.5" />

A triggered ability's condition begins with "When," "At," or "The [Nth] time," and an additional conditional statement immediately following that condition is also part of it.<Rule number="383.2.a" /><Rule number="383.2.a.1" />
Those requirements are checked after the triggering event has been processed.
If they are not satisfied then, the ability is not placed on the chain.<Rule number="383.2.c" /><Rule number="383.3" />

An "if" condition that comes after an effect instruction is instead part of the effect.<Rule number="383.2.b" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	<Card name="Sunken Temple" /> reads "When you conquer here with one or more <Mighty /> units, you may pay <Energy value={1} /> to draw 1."
	The <Mighty /> requirement is part of the trigger condition, so the ability is not placed on the chain unless a <Mighty /> unit participated in the conquer.

	<Card name="Gutter Palace" /> reads "At the start of your Beginning Phase, if you have exactly 4 cards in hand and exactly 4 units at battlefields, you win the game."
	The "if" condition immediately follows the trigger condition, so both quantities are checked at the start of the Beginning Phase.
	If either requirement is not satisfied then, the ability is not placed on the chain.

	<Card name="Loose Cannon" /> reads "At start of your Beginning Phase, draw 1 if you have one or fewer cards in your hand."
	This "if" condition follows the effect instruction "draw 1," so the ability is placed on the chain at the start of every Beginning Phase and checks hand size when it resolves.
</Callout>

## When do I choose whether to use a triggered ability that says "you may"? [#optional-trigger-decision]

Choose whether to use an initial "you may" instruction during finalization<Rule number="383.3.a" /><Rule number="402.1" />, whereas you choose whether to use a later one during resolution.<Rule number="383.3.a.3" />

If you decline, the pending ability is removed from the chain and considered not to have triggered.<Rule number="383.3.a.2" /><Rule number="402.1.a" />
If you proceed, you make its required choices and pay costs incurred during finalization before the ability becomes a finalized chain item.<Rule number="402.2" /><Rule number="404.1" /><Rule number="406.2" />

## When do I pay a cost in a triggered ability that says "you may"? [#costs-within-instructions]

Pay a cost at the beginning of a triggered effect or immediately after an initial "you may" during finalization<Rule number="383.3.b" /><Rule number="383.3.b.1" /><Rule number="740.4.a.2" />, whereas you pay a later cost during resolution.<Rule number="740.4.a.2.a" />

In rules text phrased as "[do X] to [do Y]," the [do X] instruction is a cost within instructions and [do Y] is its effect.<Rule number="740.4.a" />
If that cost appears at the beginning of a triggered effect or immediately after an initial "you may," it becomes the ability's base cost.<Rule number="383.3.b" /><Rule number="383.3.b.1" /><Rule number="740.4.a.2" />
If you decline to pay it, the pending ability is removed from the chain without finalizing.<Rule number="404.2" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	<Card name="Vayne, Hunter" /> reads "When I conquer, you may pay <Energy value={1} /> to return me to my owner's hand."
	The payment immediately follows the initial "you may," so you decide and pay during finalization.
	If you decline, the pending ability is removed from the chain.
	If you pay, the ability finalizes and opponents may react before it resolves.<Rule number="406.4" />
</Callout>
