## Can I react to units being played? [#unit-play-reactions]

Only the unit's controller can react before it enters, and only by activating an ability with <Reaction /> that uses <Add /> while paying the unit's costs.<Rule number="357.1.a" /><Rule number="429.3" />
That ability finalizes and resolves immediately during the payment step.<Rule number="429.3.a" />
Otherwise, a unit's finalization does not pass priority.<Rule number="337.1.a" />
It then resolves immediately and enters the board as part of that resolution.<Rule number="337.2" /><Rule number="359.2.c" />

If the unit has a play effect ("When you play me..."), that ability is put on the chain only after the unit has finalized and entered the board.<Rule number="383.4.a.2" />
Likewise, another permanent's trigger condition is evaluated after the unit enters.<Rule number="383.2.c" />
If that condition is fulfilled, the triggered ability is then put on the chain.<Rule number="383.3" />
If a triggered ability is put on the chain, the player granted priority may play a <Reaction /> after no pending items remain.<Rule number="337.4" /><Rule number="338.1.a.2" />
By then, the unit is already on the board.

## In what order are multiple pending chain items finalized and resolved? [#pending-item-order]

Pending chain items finalize in the order they were added, starting with the oldest;<Rule number="337.1" /><Rule number="337.1.b" /> finalized chain items resolve in the reverse order, starting with the newest;<Rule number="340.1" /> but units, gear, and abilities that use <Add /> resolve immediately when they finalize, even if other pending items still need to finalize.<Rule number="337.2" /><Rule number="337.3" />

## Can I play a reaction after one, but not all, items on the chain have resolved? [#partial-chain-reactions]

Yes, resolving one item does not commit the rest of the chain to resolve.
Before the next finalized item resolves, players receive priority and may add new <Reaction /> cards or abilities to the chain.<Rule number="340.4" /><Rule number="338.1.a" />
The next item resolves only after all players pass in sequence without adding another item.<Rule number="339.1" />

## Outside a showdown, do I lose control of a battlefield as soon as my last unit leaves it? [#empty-battlefield-control]

No, you lose control during the next cleanup if the battlefield is still empty and the chain is empty, making the turn open.<Rule number="190.4.c" /><Rule number="309.2" /><Rule number="323.6" />
If the chain still contains an item, the turn is closed and you keep control through that cleanup.<Rule number="309.1" /><Rule number="323.6" />
