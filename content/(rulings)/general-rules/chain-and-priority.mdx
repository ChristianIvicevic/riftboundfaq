## Can I react to units being played? [#unit-play-reactions]

No.
A unit enters the board before any player receives priority to react.<Rule number="337.2" /><Rule number="337.4" /><Rule number="359.2" />

If playing the unit triggers an ability, that ability is added to the chain only after the unit has entered.<Rule number="383.2.c" /><Rule number="383.3" /><Rule number="383.4.a.2" />
Any opportunity to react to that ability comes later and cannot stop the unit from entering.<Rule number="406.4" />

## What order does the game follow when several cards or abilities are waiting on the chain? [#pending-item-order]

The game first prepares pending items for resolution, a process called finalization, in the order they were added starting with the oldest.<Rule number="337.1" /><Rule number="337.1.b" />
A unit, gear, or ability that <Add />s resources resolves immediately when it finalizes.<Rule number="337.2" /><Rule number="429.2" /><Rule number="429.2.a" />
Other finalized items remain on the chain while the remaining pending items are processed.

Once every pending item has been processed, the controller of the next item on the chain receives priority.<Rule number="337.3" /><Rule number="337.4" />
After all players pass priority in sequence without adding an item, finalized chain items resolve in the opposite direction, starting with the newest.<Rule number="339.1" /><Rule number="340.1" />

## Can I play a reaction after one, but not all, items on the chain have resolved? [#partial-chain-reactions]

Yes.
After the newest item resolves, players receive another opportunity to react before the next item resolves if the chain still has a finalized item and no pending items remain.<Rule number="340.4" />

The controller of the newest remaining item gains priority first.<Rule number="340.4" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	You put <Card name="Singularity" /> on the chain targeting an opponent's unit.
	Your opponent reacts with <Card name="Wind Wall" /> to counter Singularity, and you react with <Card name="Stupefy" />.
	Once Stupefy resolves and you draw a card, priority is back to your opponent as the most recent item is their Wind Wall.
	After they pass priority, you can react again before Wind Wall resolves.
</Callout>

## Outside a showdown, do I lose control of a battlefield as soon as my last unit leaves it? [#empty-battlefield-control]

No, not immediately.

Outside a showdown, control of an empty battlefield is checked during a cleanup, not at the moment the last unit leaves.
You lose control only if the battlefield is still empty and the turn is in an open state.<Rule number="190.4.c" /><Rule number="323.6" />

If the chain still contains an item when a cleanup occurs, the turn is in a closed state and you keep control through that cleanup.<Rule number="309.1" />
You can lose control during a later cleanup after the turn returns to an open state if the battlefield remains empty.
