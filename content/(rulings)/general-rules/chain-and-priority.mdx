## Can I react to units being played? [#unit-play-reactions]

You cannot react to another player's unit before it enters the board.
<Term item="finalization">Finalizing</Term> the unit does not pass <Term item="priority">priority</Term>; the unit instead <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term> immediately and enters the board.<Rule number="337.1.a" /><Rule number="337.2" /><Rule number="359.2.c" />

The unit's controller has one exception: while paying the unit's costs, they may activate an ability with <Reaction /> that uses <Add />.<Rule number="357.1.a" />
That ability finalizes and resolves immediately during payment.<Rule number="444.2.c" />

After the unit enters, its play effect and any other fulfilled triggered abilities are put on the <Term item="chain">chain</Term>.<Rule number="383.2.c" /><Rule number="383.3" /><Rule number="383.4.a.2" />
You may react once those abilities finalize and priority is granted, but the unit is already on the board by then.<Rule number="337.4" /><Rule number="338.1.a" /><Rule number="338.1.a.2" />

## In what order are multiple pending chain items finalized and resolved? [#pending-item-order]

<Term item="pending">Pending</Term> items undergo <Term item="finalization">finalization</Term> from oldest to newest, finalized items still waiting on the <Term item="chain">chain</Term> <Term item="resolution">resolve</Term> from newest to oldest, and units, gear, and <Add /> abilities resolve as soon as they finalize.

- **Pending items:** Take them through finalization in the order they were added, from oldest to newest, until each item finalizes or leaves the chain.<Rule number="337.1" /><Rule number="337.1.b" />
- **Finalized items:** Among those that remain on the chain, the newest resolves first.<Rule number="340.1" />
- **Immediate resolution:** Units, gear, and abilities that use <Add /> resolve when they finalize, even if other pending items still need to finalize.<Rule number="337.2" />

## Can I play a reaction after one, but not all, items on the chain have resolved? [#partial-chain-reactions]

Yes.
If another <Term item="finalization">finalized</Term> item remains after one item <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term>, the newest remaining item's controller receives <Term item="priority">priority</Term> before it resolves once any <Term item="pending">pending</Term> items have finalized or left the <Term item="chain">chain</Term>.<Rule number="337.1" /><Rule number="340.3" /><Rule number="340.4" />
That player may use this priority to play a legally timed <Reaction />.<Rule number="338.1.a" /><Rule number="338.1.a.2" />
The chain therefore does not have to resolve in its entirety once resolution begins; a new item can be added between two resolutions.

## Outside a showdown, do I lose control of a battlefield as soon as my last unit leaves it? [#empty-battlefield-control]

If a <Term item="chain">chain</Term> is still in progress or combat is ongoing at that battlefield, your last unit leaving does not make you lose control during the resulting <Term item="cleanup">cleanup</Term>.

Battlefield control is checked during cleanup.
An item remaining on the chain keeps the empty battlefield under your control.<Rule number="323.6" /><Rule number="330" /><Rule number="309.1" />
An ongoing combat at that battlefield also prevents control of that battlefield from changing.<Rule number="190.4.b" />
Removing the chain's final item causes a later cleanup to check again; if the battlefield is still empty and no combat is ongoing there, you lose control then.<Rule number="190.4.c" /><Rule number="319.5" /><Rule number="309.2" />
