## When does a showdown close? [#showdown-close]

A showdown closes only after every player passes focus in sequence without playing a spell or activating an ability.<Rule number="347.2.a" /><Rule number="348" />
It does not close merely because a unit leaves or is killed.

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	You control a battlefield with a 2 might <Card name="Stalwart Poro" /> on it.
	Your opponent moves their <Card name="Ravenbloom Student" /> into that battlefield starting a showdown.
	When combat opens, Stalwart Poro becomes a defender and its <Shield /> increases its might to 3.
	Once your opponent gains focus, they play <Card name="Hextech Ray" /> targeting Stalwart Poro.
	Hextech Ray resolves, marking 3 damage on it.
	During the resulting cleanup, Stalwart Poro is killed for lethal damage.<Rule number="323.5" />
	The showdown remains open, and you gain focus when the chain empties.<Rule number="346" />
	You may still play a legally timed card or ability before players begin passing focus again.<Rule number="347.1" />
</Callout>

## What happens if an opposing player's unit moves into an existing non-combat showdown? [#non-combat-showdown]

When an opposing player's unit becomes present during a non-combat showdown, combat is staged and the showdown becomes a combat showdown during the cleanup following that unit's arrival.<Rule number="316.8.b.1.a" /><Rule number="323.9" /><Rule number="323.14" />

When combat opens, the player whose units applied contested status is the attacker, and the opposing player is the defender.<Rule number="464.2.c.1" /><Rule number="464.2.c.2" />
Their units at that battlefield receive the matching attacker or defender designations then.<Rule number="464.2.c.3" />
Eligible attack and defend triggers are checked when those designations are gained.<Rule number="383.4.e" /><Rule number="383.4.f" />

## When do attack and defend triggers happen? [#attack-defend-triggers]

An attack or defend trigger is checked when its unit or player first gains the matching attacker or defender designation during a combat.<Rule number="383.4.e" /><Rule number="383.4.f" />
Units already present gain their designations when combat opens, while units that become present later gain them during the cleanup following their arrival.<Rule number="464.2.c.3" /><Rule number="464.2.c.3.a" />
If the trigger has another condition, that condition must also be fulfilled at that time.<Rule number="383.4.e.2.b" /><Rule number="383.4.f.2.b" />

### Units can join combat late and still trigger [#late-combat-triggers]

If a unit becomes present at a battlefield where combat is already in progress, it gains its controller's attacker or defender designation during the next cleanup.<Rule number="323.2.a" /><Rule number="464.2.c.3.a" />
Its eligible "When I attack" or "When I defend" abilities trigger then, after their other conditions are checked.<Rule number="383.4.e" /><Rule number="383.4.f" />

### Can attack and defend abilities trigger more than once per combat? [#once-per-combat-triggers]

An attack or defend trigger's condition is checked only once per combat, even if its unit loses and regains the matching designation.<Rule number="383.4.e.2.a" /><Rule number="383.4.f.2.a" />

A unit that leaves the combat battlefield loses its designation during the next cleanup.<Rule number="323.2.c" />
If it returns during the same combat, it regains its controller's designation during a later cleanup, but its attack or defend trigger is not checked again.<Rule number="323.2.a" /><Rule number="383.4.e.2.a" /><Rule number="383.4.f.2.a" />
