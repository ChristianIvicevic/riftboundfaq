## How is a card's total cost determined? [#total-cost-determination]

A card's total cost is determined in five stages, in this order:

<Steps>
<Step>
Start with the card's base costs, replacing them or setting them to zero as instructed.<Rule number="356.1" />
</Step>
<Step>
Add all mandatory additional costs and any optional additional costs you chose to pay.<Rule number="356.2" />
</Step>
<Step>
Add all applicable cost increases.<Rule number="356.3" />
</Step>
<Step>
Reduce the card's cost by all applicable discounts.<Rule number="356.4" />
</Step>
<Step>
Apply any effects that modify the resulting total cost.<Rule number="356.5" />
</Step>
</Steps>

Discounts that apply to one specific part of a cost are applied before discounts that apply to the total cost.<Rule number="356.4.d" />
If multiple discounts apply within either group, you may choose their order.<Rule number="356.4.c.1" /><Rule number="356.4.d.1" />
Any minimum imposed by a discount applies only to that discount, so a later discount can reduce the cost further.<Rule number="356.4.e" />

## Are paid costs refunded if a card or ability is countered? [#countered-costs]

No.
Countering does not refund any costs paid to play a card, activate an ability, or trigger an ability, including additional costs.<Rule number="425.1.c" /><Rule number="425.1.c.1" />

## What are optional additional costs? [#optional-additional-costs]

Optional additional costs are additional costs you may choose to pay.<Rule number="356.2.b.1" />
Their rules text uses both "may" and the exact phrase "as an additional cost."
<Accelerate /> and <Repeat /> are keyworded optional additional costs.<Rule number="805.1.a" /><Rule number="820.1.d" />

You choose whether to pay an optional additional cost while making the relevant choices for the card or ability being played.<Rule number="355.1.a" /><Rule number="399" />
If chosen, it is added while determining the total cost before applicable discounts are applied.<Rule number="356.2" /><Rule number="356.4" />
The optional additional cost counts as paid even if discounts reduce it to zero.<Rule number="356.4.f" /><Rule number="356.4.f.1" />

## What costs still apply when I play a card ignoring one or more of its costs? [#ignored-costs]

The exact phrase determines which costs remain.
Mandatory additional costs, any optional additional costs you choose, and cost increases are included while determining the total in every case.<Rule number="355.1.a" /><Rule number="356.1.b.3" /><Rule number="356.2" /><Rule number="356.3" />

- **"Ignoring its energy cost" or "ignoring its power cost":** Only the named base cost becomes zero; the other base cost still applies.<Rule number="356.1.b.2" />
- **"Ignoring its cost":** Both the base energy cost and base power cost become zero.<Rule number="356.1.b.1" />
- **"Ignoring any and all costs":** This phrase then sets the entire resulting total to zero.<Rule number="356.5.a" />
  This includes non-standard costs paid through game actions such as killing a unit.<Rule number="356.7" />
  A chosen optional additional cost counts as paid even though it adds nothing to the final total.<Rule number="356.4.f.1" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">

<Card name="Fizz, Trickster" /> sets the chosen spell's base energy cost to <Energy value={0} />.
If an opponent controls <Card name="Helm of Suppression" />, Helm's cost increase is applied afterward, adding <Energy value={1} />, or <Energy value={1} /><Universal /> if Helm is <Empowered />.

</Callout>

## Can players react while costs are being paid? [#cost-reaction-window]

No normal reaction window opens while a cost is being paid.<Rule number="357" /><Rule number="404" /><Rule number="337.1.a" /><Rule number="204.3.b" /><Rule number="158.3" /><Rule number="204.4.b" />

The limited exception is a resource payment: the player paying may activate an eligible <Add /> ability with <Reaction /> to provide those resources.<Rule number="444.2.c" />
That ability <Term item="finalization">finalizes</Term> and <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term> immediately without passing <Term item="priority">priority</Term>.<Rule number="429.2.a" /><Rule number="429.3.a" />

Ordinary reactions resume only when game procedure next allows a player to act; by then, the cost has already been paid.<Rule number="337.4" /><Rule number="340.2" /><Rule number="340.4" />
