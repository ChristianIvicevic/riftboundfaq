## Does Sunken Temple trigger if I conquer with a unit that is only Mighty due to Assault? [#mighty-from-assault]

Yes, if <Assault /> raises at least one conquering unit's might to 5 or more.
Sunken Temple's <Mighty /> requirement is part of its trigger condition, so the game checks it after the conquer occurs.<Rule number="383.2.a.1" /><Rule number="383.2.c" />

The conquer occurs while combat is still ongoing.<Rule number="466.5.d" />
Combat ends afterward, when attacker and defender designations are removed.<Rule number="466.7" /><Rule number="466.7.a" />
Until then, the unit retains its attacker designation and <Assault /> bonus.<Rule number="807.1.c" /><Rule number="807.1.d.1" />
A unit on the board is <Mighty /> whenever its current might is 5 or more.<Rule number="708" /><Rule number="710" />
