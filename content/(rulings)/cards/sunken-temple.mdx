## Does Sunken Temple trigger if I conquer with a unit that is only Mighty due to Assault? [#mighty-from-assault]

Yes, if <Assault /> raises the might of at least one of your conquering units to 5 or more.<Rule number="708" /><Rule number="710" />
The conquer occurs before combat ends.<Rule number="466.5.d" /><Rule number="466.7" />
The unit's attacker designation is removed only afterward, so its <Assault /> bonus still applies when Sunken Temple checks whether it is <Mighty />.<Rule number="466.7.a" /><Rule number="807.1.c" /><Rule number="807.1.d.1" />
