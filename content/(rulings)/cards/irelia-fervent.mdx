## If I target Irelia, Fervent with a spell or ability and it gets countered, does she still get +1 might? [#targeting-before-counter]

Yes.
Irelia becomes a target while the spell or ability is making its relevant choices during finalization.<Rule number="355.5" /><Rule number="355.7" />
After that item finalizes, her triggered ability is added as a pending item and finalized before players receive priority.<Rule number="383.4.b.2" /><Rule number="383.4.b.3" /><Rule number="337.3" /><Rule number="337.4" />
Countering the original spell or ability afterward prevents only that item from resolving, not Irelia's already-triggered ability.<Rule number="425.1.a" />

See [Can triggered abilities trigger on an opponent's turn without Reaction?](/general-rules/abilities#triggered-ability-timing) for the choices and costs that a pending triggered ability must complete before it finalizes.

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	<Card name="Blade Dancer" /> works the same way.
	Her triggered ability reads "When you choose a friendly unit, you may exhaust me and pay <Universal /> to ready it."
	The ability is added before the original spell can be countered.
	If Blade Dancer's controller chooses to perform the ability and pays its costs, it also finalizes before the original spell can be countered.
</Callout>

## Does Blade Dancer's ability trigger Irelia, Fervent's +1 might? [#blade-dancer]

Only if Blade Dancer's ability successfully readies Irelia; it does not trigger her by choosing her.
"It" in "ready it" automatically identifies the friendly unit from Blade Dancer's trigger condition instead of making another choice, so that unit is not a target of the ability.<Rule number="355.10.d" /><Rule number="355.10.d.1" />
Blade Dancer's controller must choose to perform the ability and pay its costs before it can finalize.<Rule number="383.3.a" /><Rule number="404.1" />
If the ability resolves and successfully readies an exhausted Irelia, that action fulfills the other half of her trigger condition.<Rule number="415.1" />
An already-ready Irelia cannot be readied again, so her ability does not trigger.<Rule number="415.1.b" /><Rule number="415.1.c" />

## Does Irelia, Fervent get +1 might when she readies during the Awaken Phase? [#awaken-phase-readying]

Yes, but only if she was exhausted and becomes ready.
During the Awaken Phase, the turn player readies every game object they control that can be readied.<Rule number="315.1.b" />
That action fulfills Irelia's trigger condition when it changes her from exhausted to ready.<Rule number="415.1" />
An already-ready Irelia cannot be readied again, so no readying event occurs and her ability does not trigger.<Rule number="415.1.b" /><Rule number="415.1.c" />

## Does a Repeat spell targeting Irelia, Fervent for both instructions give her +1 might twice? [#repeat-targeting-twice]

Yes, if both Repeat choices target Irelia, each choice triggers her ability separately.
Paying <Repeat /> makes the spell execute its instructions an additional time, with choices made separately for each execution.<Rule number="820.1.d" /><Rule number="820.2" /><Rule number="820.2.a" />
Choosing Irelia for both executions therefore targets her twice.<Rule number="355.7" />
After the spell finalizes, one instance of Irelia's triggered ability is added as a pending item for each targeting event.<Rule number="383.4.b.2" /><Rule number="383.4.b.3" />

See [What does Repeat repeat?](/mechanics/repeat#repeated-instructions) for the complete repeated-instruction and choice procedure.

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	You play <Card name="Feral Strength" /> and pay its <Repeat /> cost, choosing Irelia for both instructions.
	After Feral Strength finalizes, Irelia's ability triggers twice — once per targeting event — and both triggers are added to the chain.
	When her triggers resolve, she receives +1 and +1 from her own ability.
	When the spell resolves, Irelia receives +2 and +2 from the spell's two instructions.
	She gets +6 might total this turn.
</Callout>
