## If I target Irelia, Fervent with a spell or ability and it gets countered, does she still get +1 might? [#targeting-before-counter]

Yes, if you are Irelia's controller and choose her as the target; countering the original spell or ability does not counter her separate +1 might ability.
As Irelia's controller, you fulfill her trigger condition by choosing her while playing the original spell or ability.<Rule number="411.1" /><Rule number="411.4" /><Rule number="355.5" /><Rule number="355.7" /><Rule number="399" />
After the original item finalizes, Irelia's triggered ability is added as a separate chain item.<Rule number="383.4.b.2" /><Rule number="383.4.b.3" />
Countering the original item afterward clears only that item, not Irelia's separate ability.<Rule number="425.1.a" />

## Does Blade Dancer's ability trigger Irelia, Fervent's +1 might? [#blade-dancer]

<Card name="Blade Dancer" />'s ability can trigger Irelia once by readying an exhausted Irelia controlled by the same player, but it does not trigger her by choosing her.
"It" in "ready it" automatically identifies the friendly unit from Blade Dancer's trigger condition instead of making another choice, so that unit is not a target of the ability.<Rule number="355.10.d" /><Rule number="355.10.d.1" />
Irelia's controller fulfills her trigger condition when Blade Dancer's ability changes her from exhausted to ready.<Rule number="411.1" /><Rule number="411.4" /><Rule number="415.1" />
An already-ready Irelia cannot be readied again, so her ability does not trigger.<Rule number="415.1.b" /><Rule number="415.1.c" />

## Does Irelia, Fervent get +1 might when she readies during the Awaken Phase? [#awaken-phase-readying]

Yes, once, when her controller readies her from exhausted to ready during their Awaken Phase.
During their Awaken Phase, Irelia's controller readies every game object they control that can be readied, and changing an exhausted Irelia to ready creates her trigger.<Rule number="315.1.b" /><Rule number="411.1" /><Rule number="411.4" /><Rule number="415.1" />
An already-ready Irelia cannot be readied again, so no readying event occurs and her ability does not trigger.<Rule number="415.1.b" /><Rule number="415.1.c" />

## Does a Repeat spell targeting Irelia, Fervent for both instructions give her +1 might twice? [#repeat-targeting-twice]

Yes, if Irelia's controller chooses her both times; each choice is a separate targeting event, so Irelia triggers twice.
Paying <Repeat /> makes the spell execute its instructions an additional time, with choices made separately for each execution.<Rule number="820.1.d" /><Rule number="820.2" /><Rule number="820.2.a" />
Irelia's controller creates those two targeting events by choosing her once for each execution.<Rule number="411.1" /><Rule number="411.4" /><Rule number="355.7" />
After the spell finalizes, one instance of Irelia's triggered ability is added as a pending item for each targeting event.<Rule number="383.4.b.2" /><Rule number="383.4.b.3" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	You control Irelia, play <Card name="Feral Strength" />, and pay its <Repeat /> cost, choosing her for both instructions.
	After Feral Strength finalizes, Irelia's ability triggers twice, once per targeting event, and both triggers are added to the chain.
	Irelia's two +1 modifiers and Feral Strength's two +2 modifiers give her +6 might total this turn.
</Callout>
