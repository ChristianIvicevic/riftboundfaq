## Does Diana, Lunari's ability resolve before or after attack and defend abilities from the start of combat? [#trigger-resolution-order]

If the showdown begins as combat opens and the relevant abilities resolve, Diana's ability resolves after the attack and defend abilities generated by that opening; if combat opens during an existing showdown, that combat opening does not trigger Diana's "when a showdown begins here" ability.

When combat opens with a new showdown, start-of-showdown effects happen before units gain the attacker and defender designations.<Rule number="464.2.b" /><Rule number="464.2.c.3" />
Diana's ability is therefore added before the attack and defend abilities triggered by those designations.<Rule number="383.4.e" /><Rule number="383.4.f" /><Rule number="464.2.e" />
Pending items finalize oldest-first, so Diana's ability finalizes before those later abilities.<Rule number="337.1.b" />
Finalized items resolve newest-first, so each of those attack and defend abilities resolves before Diana's ability if it remains on the chain.<Rule number="340.1" />

When combat instead opens during an existing showdown, the showdown does not begin again.<Rule number="464.1" /><Rule number="464.2" />

## When do I pay the 1 energy for Diana, Lunari's ability? [#ability-finalization-cost]

You pay it while Diana's triggered ability is being finalized, before opponents receive the normal opportunity to play Reactions to the ability.<Rule number="406.4" />

Because "you may" is the first part of the effect, you choose whether to perform the ability during finalization.<Rule number="383.3.a" />
The immediately following "pay <Energy value={1} /> to <Predict />" is a cost within instructions, so the payment becomes the triggered ability's base cost and must be paid for the ability to finalize.<Rule number="383.3.b" /><Rule number="383.3.b.1" /><Rule number="740.4.a" />
While paying, you may activate an eligible <Add /> ability with <Reaction /> to provide the energy.<Rule number="444.2.c" />

If you decline to pay, the pending ability is removed from the chain without finalizing.<Rule number="404.2" />
If you pay, it becomes a finalized chain item, and opponents may react before it resolves.<Rule number="406.2" /><Rule number="406.4" />
When it resolves, you <Predict />, reveal the top card of your main deck, and draw that card if it is a spell.

<Callout type="warn" title="Errata">
	Diana received functional errata with the release of Vendetta.
	Older printings say "you may pay <Energy value={1} />. If you do, ..."; her current text says "you may pay <Energy value={1} /> to ..." instead.
	This changes the payment from an instruction performed during resolution into a cost paid while finalizing the ability.
	CR 444.2 still describes Diana using her pre-errata behavior and incorrectly places the payment during resolution.<Rule number="444.2" />
</Callout>
