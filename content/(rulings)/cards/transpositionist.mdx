## Can Transpositionist still play a unit when an Empowered Gangplank, Naval replaces the return? [#gangplank-replacement]

When an <Empowered /> <Card name="Gangplank, Naval" /> replaces Transpositionist's return, you may still play a unit that has less might than Gangplank's current might after the +3 might increase applies, provided that unit is in your hand or chosen champion zone or a separate effect permits you to play it from another zone.<Rule number="420.1.a" /><Rule number="420.3.b" />

Replacing Gangplank's return does not stop Transpositionist's next instruction because it refers to Gangplank and his might, not whether the return happened.<Rule number="359.3.e.14.a" /><Rule number="359.3.e.14.b" /><Rule number="407.5" />
Gangplank remains on the board and remains a legal target, so the ability can still read his might.<Rule number="359.3.e.12.a" />
It reads that value when the later instruction executes, after the +3 might increase applies.<Rule number="359.3.f.2" />

An otherwise unmodified Gangplank has 9 might at that point, so the unit must have less than 9 might.
