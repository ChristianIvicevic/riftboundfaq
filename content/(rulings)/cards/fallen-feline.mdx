## Does Fallen Feline stop a named spell already on the chain? [#existing-spells]

No.
A spell already on the <Term item="chain">chain</Term> has already started its play because playing a card means placing it there and queuing it for <Term item="finalization">finalization</Term>.<Rule number="420.1" />
While Fallen Feline is at a battlefield, it prevents opponents from starting another play of a spell with the chosen name, but it does not stop the spell already on the chain from finalizing or <Term item="resolution">resolving</Term>.

If that spell later instructs an opponent to play another spell with the chosen name, Fallen Feline can still make the new play impossible without preventing that spell from finalizing.<Rule number="358.3.a" />
The play instruction is negated, so the second spell stays in its current zone.<Rule number="359.3.e.6" />
The spell already on the chain continues resolving and does as much as it can.<Rule number="055" />

## When can I react to Fallen Feline's play effect? [#naming-timing]

You can react before its controller names a spell, but once the name is chosen, it is too late to start playing that spell while Fallen Feline remains at a battlefield.
The name is chosen only when the play effect <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term>,<Rule number="355.18" /> after players have had the opportunity to react to the <Term item="finalization">finalized</Term> ability.<Rule number="407.4" />
