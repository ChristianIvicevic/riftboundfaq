## Does Fallen Feline stop a named spell already on the chain? [#existing-spells]

No.
Fallen Feline prevents opponents from playing further spells with the chosen name while it is at a battlefield, but it does not affect spells already on the chain.

To play a card means to place it on the chain and queue it for finalization.<Rule number="419.1" />
A pending spell has already been placed on the chain and queued for finalization,<Rule number="329.1" /><Rule number="354.2" /> and a finalized spell has progressed beyond that point.<Rule number="329.3" />
Fallen Feline therefore does not stop either from finalizing or resolving.

See also [What does "play" mean on a card?](/general-rules/playing-cards#play-definition).
