## Does a Reflection token with Temporary lose Temporary after Shady Spectacles makes it a copy of a unit that doesn't have Temporary? [#copying-temporary]

It depends on where <Temporary /> came from.
The Reflection token keeps <Temporary /> if a separate effect granted it, but loses the keyword if it had <Temporary /> only in rules text copied from another unit.

Shady Spectacles replaces the token's copyable traits, the characteristics inherited through a copy effect, including its rules text.<Rule number="477.1.b" /><Rule number="477.1.b.1" /><Rule number="477.1.b.1.a" />
The separate keyword grant applies afterward in the ability-altering layer.<Rule number="477.2.a" />
Because the grant has no stated duration, it continues for as long as the token remains on the board.<Rule number="801.3.a.3" />
