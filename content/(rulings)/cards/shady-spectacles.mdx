## Does Shady Spectacles copy Temporary from the chosen unit? [#copying-temporary]

Shady Spectacles copies <Temporary /> when the keyword comes from the chosen unit's card, its token definition, or a copy effect, but not when another effect granted it.

For example, copying <Card name="Keeper of Masks" /> gives the equipped unit <Temporary /> because the keyword is printed on Keeper of Masks.
If a unit has <Temporary /> only because it received the keyword from <Card name="Shadow's Call" />, <Card name="Deceiver" />, or <Card name="Mirror Image" />, Shady Spectacles does not copy it.

In rules terms, copy effects use copyable traits: a card's printed or already-copied traits, or the traits a token had when it was created.<Rule number="479.1.b" /><Rule number="479.1.b.1" />
A separate keyword grant changes what the unit currently has without changing those copyable traits.<Rule number="479.1.c" /><Rule number="479.3.a" />
