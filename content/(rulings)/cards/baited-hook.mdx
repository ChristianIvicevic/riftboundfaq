## Can my opponent react before Baited Hook kills my unit? [#kill-reaction-window]

Yes.
"Kill a friendly unit" comes after the ":" in Baited Hook's activated ability, so it is an effect instruction rather than part of the activation cost.<Rule number="377.1" />
That instruction targets a friendly unit, and you choose that target while the ability is being finalized.<Rule number="355.10" /><Rule number="402.2" />

After Baited Hook's ability is finalized, opponents have an opportunity to play Reactions before it resolves and kills the target.<Rule number="377.3.b.2" /><Rule number="406.4" />
The later might limit refers to the unit affected by the kill, so these are linked instructions.<Rule number="359.3.e.14" />
After the unit enters the trash, Baited Hook uses its might from immediately before that zone change.<Rule number="359.3.e.13" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	You activate Baited Hook, targeting a friendly unit with 4 might.
	In response, your opponent plays <Card name="Stupefy" />, reducing that unit's might by 1 down to 3.
	As the newer chain item, Stupefy resolves first, so the unit has 3 might when Baited Hook kills it.<Rule number="340.1" />
	Baited Hook therefore lets you banish a unit with up to 4 might from among the top 5 cards of your main deck.
</Callout>

## What happens if the targeted unit is removed before Baited Hook's ability resolves? [#illegal-target]

The unit is not killed, you still look at the top 5 cards, but you cannot banish or play a unit from among them and must recycle every card you looked at.

If the friendly unit is no longer a legal target when the ability resolves, it is unaffected and the kill instruction is ignored.<Rule number="359.3.e.2" /><Rule number="359.3.e.5" />
Its might is then null, so the calculation used to choose a unit from the top 5 is ignored.<Rule number="359.3.e.12" />
The independent instruction to recycle the rest still happens, and no card was banished, so every card you looked at is recycled.<Rule number="359.3.e.11" />

## Does a unit's Deathknell resolve before or after the unit played by Baited Hook and its triggers? [#deathknell-order]

If the <Deathknell /> uses <Add />, it resolves before the played unit enters; otherwise, the played unit enters before the <Deathknell /> can resolve, and any triggered ability added because that unit was played resolves before the <Deathknell /> if both abilities resolve.<Rule number="337.2" /><Rule number="429.2" />
Baited Hook adds the <Deathknell /> before the played unit.<Rule number="808.1.d.2" /><Rule number="354.2" />
That append order makes the <Deathknell /> finalize first because pending items finalize oldest-first.<Rule number="337.1.b" />
If the <Deathknell /> does not use <Add />, later play triggers resolve before it because finalized abilities resolve newest-first.<Rule number="340.1" />
The unit enters immediately after it finalizes.<Rule number="337.2" /><Rule number="359.2.c" />
Its play effect and other fulfilled play triggers are added only after it enters.<Rule number="383.2.c" /><Rule number="383.3" /><Rule number="383.4.a.2" />

### Resolution Sequence [#deathknell-order-sequence]

For a <Deathknell /> that does not use <Add />:

1. Baited Hook kills the chosen unit while its ability is resolving.
2. The killed unit's <Deathknell /> is added above Baited Hook as a pending item before the unit enters the trash.<Rule number="808.1.d.2" />
3. Baited Hook continues resolving and plays the selected unit, adding that unit above the <Deathknell /> as another pending item.<Rule number="158.3.a" /><Rule number="354.2" />
4. Baited Hook recycles the remaining cards and finishes resolving.
5. The <Deathknell /> finalizes first because it is the oldest pending item.<Rule number="337.1.b" />
6. The played unit finalizes next and immediately enters the board.<Rule number="337.2" /><Rule number="359.2.c" />
7. The unit's play effect and other abilities triggered by its play are added after it enters, above the <Deathknell />.<Rule number="383.2.c" /><Rule number="383.3" /><Rule number="383.4.a.2" />
8. Once no pending items remain, players receive priority.<Rule number="337.4" />
9. After all players pass, the newest finalized item resolves.<Rule number="339.1" /><Rule number="340.1" />
10. Among the items created by Baited Hook's resolution, the later play triggers therefore resolve before the <Deathknell />.

## If Baited Hook kills Glasc Mixologist and plays Karthus, Eternal, does Glasc Mixologist's Deathknell trigger twice? [#karthus-deathknell-timing]

No.
Glasc Mixologist's <Deathknell /> is processed before <Card name="Karthus, Eternal" /> enters, so Karthus's passive ability cannot create another trigger for Glasc's earlier death.

When Baited Hook kills <Card name="Glasc Mixologist" />, Glasc's <Deathknell /> is added to the chain as a pending item before he enters the trash.<Rule number="808.1.d.2" />
Baited Hook then continues resolving and may play Karthus, adding him as a newer pending item.<Rule number="158.3.a" /><Rule number="354.2" />

After Baited Hook finishes, Glasc's older pending ability is processed until it finalizes or leaves the chain before Karthus is finalized.<Rule number="337.1" /><Rule number="337.1.b" />
Karthus then enters immediately when he finalizes.<Rule number="337.2" /><Rule number="359.2.c" />
His passive ability is active only while he is on the board, so it was not active when Glasc's <Deathknell /> was added.<Rule number="365.1" />
Karthus entering does not process Glasc's earlier death again.
If Glasc's <Deathknell /> resolves, it can play only one unit from the trash.
