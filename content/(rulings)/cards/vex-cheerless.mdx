## Does Vex, Cheerless increase the cost of enemy spells played from Hidden? [#hidden-cost-increase]

Yes, while Vex is in combat, she adds <Energy value={1} /><Universal /> to an enemy spell played from Hidden before discounts are applied.

Playing a card from Hidden ignores its base cost,<Rule number="811.1.b" /> but Vex's increase is applied afterward.<Rule number="356.1.b.3" />
Additional costs or other increases can raise the spell's cost further, and later discounts can reduce it.<Rule number="356.2" /><Rule number="356.3" /><Rule number="356.4" />
This increase does not apply to units or gear played from Hidden because Vex modifies only spell costs.

## Does Vex, Cheerless reduce Deflect costs when I play spells? [#deflect-cost-reduction]

Yes, while Vex is in combat, her discount applies to <Deflect /> costs when your spell targets an opponent's permanent.

<Deflect /> is a mandatory additional cost.<Rule number="356.2.a.2" />
When your spell targets an opponent's permanent with <Deflect />, that additional cost is added before Vex's discount is applied.<Rule number="809.1.d" /><Rule number="356.2" /><Rule number="356.4" />

Vex's discount can offset resources added by <Deflect /> because discounts can reduce additional costs.<Rule number="356.4.f" />
Vex's discount cannot reduce the spell's cost below Vex's printed minimum of <Energy value={1} />, but another total-cost discount applied afterward can reduce it further.<Rule number="356.4.d.1" /><Rule number="356.4.e" />
