## When exactly does Astral Heron trigger, and which card gets its discount? [#trigger-timing]

Astral Heron triggers after your first card of the turn finishes resolving, and if its triggered ability resolves, the next card you play afterward gets the discount.
In a trigger condition, "When you play" is checked only when the act of playing that card is completed by its resolution.<Rule number="350.1" /><Rule number="419.4.a" />
If a spell is countered instead of resolving, it does not trigger Astral Heron, and the first card you later resolve that turn can trigger it instead.<Rule number="419.4.a.1" />

When the condition is fulfilled, the triggered ability is placed on the chain and must resolve before the discount exists.<Rule number="383.3" /><Rule number="390.4" />
Once it resolves, "your next card" means the next card you place on the chain and queue for finalization.<Rule number="419.1" />
Astral Heron's discount is applied while determining that card's total cost, before you pay the cost and finish finalizing it.<Rule number="356.4" /><Rule number="357" />

See [What does "play" mean on a card?](/general-rules/playing-cards#play-definition) for the complete distinction between starting, finalizing, and completing a play.

## Does Astral Heron's ability trigger if it's your first card played to a battlefield this turn? [#self-trigger]

Yes, if Astral Heron is your first card played that turn and enters a battlefield as it resolves.

A unit resolves immediately after finalizing.<Rule number="337.2" />
During that resolution, it enters the board at its chosen location.<Rule number="359.2.c" />
Only after Astral Heron finishes resolving is it considered played for trigger conditions.<Rule number="350.1" /><Rule number="419.4.a" />
Triggered abilities of permanents can have their conditions evaluated while their source is on the board.<Rule number="384.2" />
Astral Heron is therefore at the battlefield when the game evaluates its trigger condition after the completed play.<Rule number="383.2.c" />

The immediately following "if I'm at a battlefield" is part of the trigger condition and is checked at that time.<Rule number="383.2.a" /><Rule number="383.2.a.1" />

## Does Astral Heron trigger if your first card moves it to base? [#moved-during-resolution]

No, if the spell leaves Astral Heron at base when it finishes resolving.

A card is not considered played, and abilities that trigger from playing it are not evaluated, until that card finishes resolving.<Rule number="350.1" /><Rule number="419.4.a" />
Astral Heron's trigger condition is therefore checked only after the spell's movement instructions have been completed.<Rule number="383.2.c" />
Because "if I'm at a battlefield" is part of that trigger condition, the ability is not placed on the chain if Astral Heron is at base by then.<Rule number="383.2.a.1" />

This differs from moving Astral Heron after its triggered ability has already been placed on the chain, when the condition has already been satisfied.

## Once Astral Heron's triggered ability is on the chain, can killing or moving it stop the cost reduction? [#removed-in-response]

No.
Once the triggered ability is on the chain, killing or moving Astral Heron does not stop it from creating the discount if it resolves.

"If I'm at a battlefield" is part of the trigger condition, so it is not checked again when the ability resolves.<Rule number="383.2.a.1" />
The effect does not need Astral Heron's location or continued presence.
When the triggered ability resolves, it creates a delayed ability that is not associated with Astral Heron and remains applicable at its specified time even if Astral Heron has left the board.<Rule number="390.4" /><Rule number="392" />

## If the spell that receives Astral Heron's discount is later countered, is the discount granted to a different card instead? [#discount-after-counter]

No.
Once the discount has reduced the next spell's cost, countering that spell does not transfer the discount to another card.

Astral Heron's triggered ability creates a delayed passive ability that applies only during the specified window for "your next card."<Rule number="390.4" /><Rule number="391" />
The discount applies while determining that card's total cost, and the reduced cost is then paid before the card finishes finalizing.<Rule number="356.4" /><Rule number="357" />
By the time the finalized spell can be countered, that specified window has ended.

Countering the spell does not undo its finalization or refund any costs paid to play it.<Rule number="425.1.a" /><Rule number="425.1.c" />
