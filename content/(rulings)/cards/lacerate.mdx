## Can Lacerate target a unit with more than 3 might? [#targeting-restriction]

Yes, Lacerate can target any otherwise legal unit regardless of its might.
"Choose a unit" targets a unit on the board and adds no might-based targeting restriction.<Rule number="355.7" /><Rule number="355.9.a" /><Rule number="355.9.a.1" />
The later condition "if it has 3 might or less" is checked only when the kill instruction executes during resolution.<Rule number="359.3.d" />

See [Does "Kill a unit if it has 3 might or less" mean I can only choose a unit with 3 might or less?](/general-rules/targeting#target-restrictions) for the general distinction between target descriptions and effect conditions.
