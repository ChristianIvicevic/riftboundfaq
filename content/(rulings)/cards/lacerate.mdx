## Can Lacerate target a unit with more than 3 might? [#targeting-restriction]

Yes, Lacerate can target any otherwise legal unit regardless of its might.
"Choose a unit" targets a unit and adds no might-based targeting restriction.<Rule number="355.7" /><Rule number="355.9.b" />
The later condition "if it has 3 might or less" is checked only when the kill instruction executes during <Term item="resolution">resolution</Term>.<Rule number="359.3.d" />
