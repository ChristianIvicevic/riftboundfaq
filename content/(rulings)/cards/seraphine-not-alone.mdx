## Can Seraphine, Not Alone survive combat damage that becomes lethal when other exhausted units die in the same combat? [#might-loss-survival]

Yes, if Seraphine, Not Alone does not have lethal damage when the combat <Term item="cleanup">cleanup</Term> checks her while those other exhausted units are still on the board.
The cleanup checks all units for lethal damage only once, so damage that becomes lethal after those units leave does not kill Seraphine during that cleanup.<Rule number="323.4" />

### Three-Recruit Sequence [#recruit-sequence]

Suppose Seraphine has 3 damage marked and gets +3 might from three other exhausted 1-might Recruit tokens, each with 1 damage marked.
She therefore has 4 might.

<Steps>
<Step>
**Lethal-damage check:** Seraphine's 3 marked damage is less than her current 4 might, while each Recruit's 1 marked damage equals its 1 might.<Rule number="142.4.b" />
Only the Recruits have lethal damage during the check, so the cleanup kills them and not Seraphine.<Rule number="323.4" />
</Step>
<Step>
Once the Recruits leave the board, they are no longer exhausted units for Seraphine's ability to count.<Rule number="415.2" />
Her continuously evaluated might bonus therefore drops from +3 to +0, returning her to 1 might.<Rule number="478.1" /><Rule number="479.4.a" />
Her 3 marked damage is now lethal.<Rule number="142.4.b" />
That cleanup does not check her again.
</Step>
<Step>
**Combat healing:** The combat special cleanup next heals all units, clearing Seraphine's marked damage.<Rule number="468.1.a.1" />
</Step>
<Step>
The Recruits' departure causes another cleanup after the combat cleanup finishes.<Rule number="319.6" /><Rule number="322" />
Seraphine has no marked damage when that cleanup checks for lethal damage, so she survives.<Rule number="323.4" />
</Step>
</Steps>
