## Does Lotus Trap affect how much damage needs to be assigned in combat? [#combat-assignment]

Yes, Lotus Trap's doubling applies while combat damage is assigned, so the assigning player uses the doubled amount to determine lethal assignment.
A replacement effect that would apply to the resulting damage is considered to apply to the assignment instead.<Rule number="465.2.c.5" />

Assigning damage and dealing damage are still distinct steps.<Rule number="465.2.c.1" /><Rule number="465.2.c.1.a" />
A unit must have lethal damage assigned to it in full before any remaining damage may be assigned elsewhere,<Rule number="465.2.c.3" /> so the doubling changes how much of the attacker's damage the unit ties up.
For an undamaged 3 might unit under Lotus Trap, the minimum is 2 damage: assigning 1 doubles to 2 and is not lethal, while assigning 2 doubles to 4 and is lethal.<Rule number="465.2.c.4.a" />
When that damage is dealt, it is not doubled again because the replacement was already applied during assignment.<Rule number="465.2.c.5" />
The unit marks 4 damage.<Rule number="417.1.b" />
It is killed during cleanup for having lethal damage.<Rule number="142.4.b" /><Rule number="323.5" />

If another damage replacement also applies, the unit's controller chooses their order during assignment, which can change both the assigned and dealt amounts.<Rule number="465.2.c.5" />
