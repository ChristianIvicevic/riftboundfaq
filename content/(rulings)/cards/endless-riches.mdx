## How long do you skip your Draw Phase? [#draw-phase-duration]

You skip every Draw Phase you would have for as long as Endless Riches remains on the board.

"Skip your Draw Phase" is a passive ability and a replacement effect that replaces your Draw Phase with nothing.<Rule number="364.2" /><Rule number="443.1.a" />
Passive abilities of permanents become active when the permanent enters the board and are typically active only while it remains there.<Rule number="359.2.a" /><Rule number="365.1" />
Once Endless Riches leaves the board, this ability is no longer active, so your later Draw Phases occur normally.

This is not a delayed ability that applies only to your next Draw Phase or only during a particular turn.
Delayed abilities specify the window during which they apply,<Rule number="390" /> but Endless Riches specifies no such window.

## Where does a spell played by Fizz, Trickster or Kai'Sa, Evolutionary go? [#recycle-destination]

It is recycled.
The "then recycle it" text on <Card name="Fizz, Trickster" /> and <Card name="Kai'Sa, Evolutionary" /> creates a delayed replacement effect that replaces the spell leaving the chain with recycling it.<Rule number="390.3.a" />
Endless Riches also creates a replacement effect that would replace the spell going to the trash with banishing it.

The result is the same regardless of the order in which the spell's controller applies these replacement effects.<Rule number="372" />
If the delayed replacement is applied first, the spell is recycled instead of going to the trash, so Endless Riches no longer applies.
If Endless Riches is applied first, the spell would be banished instead of going to the trash, but it is still leaving the chain, so the delayed replacement replaces that event with recycling it.<Rule number="370.2" />
