## How long does Endless Riches make me skip my Draw Phase? [#draw-phase-duration]

You skip every Draw Phase you would have for as long as Endless Riches remains on the board.

"Skip your Draw Phase" is a passive ability and a replacement effect that replaces your Draw Phase with nothing.<Rule number="364.2" /><Rule number="443.1.a" />
Passive abilities of permanents become active when the permanent enters the board and are typically active only while it remains there.<Rule number="359.2.a" /><Rule number="365.1" />
Once Endless Riches leaves the board, this ability is no longer active, so your later Draw Phases occur normally.

## Can I hide cards from my trash while Endless Riches is on the board? [#hide-from-trash]

No, Endless Riches does not allow you to hide cards from your trash.

Endless Riches grants permission only to play cards from your trash.
Hiding is a separate action that is normally available only from your hand or Chosen Champion zone.<Rule number="811.1.b" /><Rule number="811.1.c.1" />

## Where does a spell played by Fizz, Trickster or Kai'Sa, Evolutionary go while Endless Riches is in play? [#recycle-destination]

Ordinarily, it is recycled to the bottom of its owner's main deck.<Rule number="416.1" /><Rule number="416.1.a" /><Rule number="416.1.c" />
The "then recycle it" text on <Card name="Fizz, Trickster" /> and <Card name="Kai'Sa, Evolutionary" /> creates a delayed replacement effect for a <Term item="finalization">finalized</Term> spell leaving the <Term item="chain">chain</Term> other than through its own effect.<Rule number="390.3.a" />
Endless Riches creates another replacement effect that would replace the spell going to the trash with banishing it.<Rule number="369.1" />

When both replacement effects apply to a spell that would go to the trash, the spell's controller chooses the order in which to apply them.<Rule number="372" />
Either order recycles the spell.

- **Fizz or Kai'Sa first:** The spell is recycled instead of going to the trash, so Endless Riches no longer applies.
- **Endless Riches first:** The spell would be banished instead of going to the trash, but it is still leaving the chain, so the delayed replacement replaces that event with recycling it.<Rule number="370.2" />

One boundary applies: if the spell's own effect instructs it to leave the chain during <Term item="resolution">resolution</Term>, follow that instruction, except Endless Riches still banishes it if that instruction would put it in the trash.<Rule number="390.3.a" /><Rule number="369.1" />
