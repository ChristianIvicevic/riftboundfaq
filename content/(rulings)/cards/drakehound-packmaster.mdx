## Can I decline to play the card chosen from those revealed by Drakehound Packmaster? [#revealed-card-costs]

Drakehound Packmaster requires you to attempt to play the chosen card: you may stop the play by declining a positive energy or power payment,<Rule number="445.2" /> but you cannot decline a zero resource cost<Rule number="445.1.b" /> or a non-standard additional cost that is mandatory or that you chose,<Rule number="445.1.a" /><Rule number="356.2.a.1" /><Rule number="356.2.b.1" /> and if such a non-standard cost cannot be paid, the play fails instead.<Rule number="203.3" /><Rule number="358.2" />
Declining the resource payment or failing to pay a required cost undoes the play and returns the card to its owner's banishment.<Rule number="056.2" /><Rule number="354" /><Rule number="445.2.a" /><Rule number="358.5" />

The instruction to play the card is not optional, so you carry it out as far as possible using the normal play process.<Rule number="055" /><Rule number="420.3" /><Rule number="420.3.b" />
Drakehound Packmaster sets the card's base energy and power costs to zero, but mandatory additional costs, optional additional costs you choose, and cost increases still apply.<Rule number="356.1.b.1" /><Rule number="356.1.b.3" /><Rule number="356.2" /><Rule number="356.3" />
You choose whether to pay an optional additional cost before the total cost is determined.<Rule number="355.1.a" />

### Zero resource cost [#zero-resource-cost]

If the card's total energy and power cost is zero, that cost is paid without performing the Pay game action and cannot be declined.<Rule number="445.1.b" />
Zero therefore gives you no way to cancel the play during <Term item="finalization">finalization</Term>.

### Positive resource cost [#positive-resource-cost]

If the total includes a positive energy or power cost, you may choose not to pay those resources.<Rule number="445.2" />
This applies whether the resource cost came from a mandatory additional cost or an optional additional cost you chose.<Rule number="356.2.a.1" /><Rule number="356.2.b.1" />
Declining does not remove only that additional cost; it undoes the entire play.<Rule number="445.2.a" />

### Non-standard costs [#non-standard-costs]

The choice to decline the Pay game action applies only to energy and power.<Rule number="445.1.a" />
A mandatory non-standard additional cost, or an optional non-standard additional cost you chose, must be paid if possible.<Rule number="356.2.a.1" /><Rule number="356.2.b.1" /><Rule number="357.2" />
You may omit an optional cost when making that earlier choice, but after choosing it, you cannot decline it during payment.<Rule number="355.1.a" /><Rule number="355.15" />

If the required game action is impossible, the cost cannot be paid.<Rule number="203.3" />
The card then fails its cost check, its play is undone, and it returns to its owner's banishment.<Rule number="358.2" /><Rule number="358.5" />

If the total contains both a positive resource cost and a non-standard cost, you may decline the resource payment before performing the non-standard cost because energy and power are paid first.<Rule number="357.1" /><Rule number="357.2" /><Rule number="445.2" />

Examples of cards with mandatory non-standard additional costs include <Card name="Cruel Patron" />, <Card name="Sacrifice" />, and <Card name="Ekko, Ingenious" />.
