## Can Glasc Mixologist's Deathknell target a unit that died from the same event? [#same-event-target]

Yes: every unit that died from the same event is already in the trash when you choose Glasc Mixologist's target.
Any of those units can be legal if it is in your trash and meets Glasc's cost and rune restrictions.
The choice happens during ability finalization.<Rule number="402.2" />

During a cleanup, step 3a adds all relevant death triggers from units with lethal damage<Rule number="323.4" /> before step 3b moves those units to the trash.<Rule number="323.5" />
Those pending abilities remain unprocessed until the cleanup is complete.<Rule number="334.1" /><Rule number="334.2" />

When one effect instruction of a resolving spell or ability kills multiple units, their deaths are simultaneous.<Rule number="370.1.a.2" />
Each relevant <Deathknell /> becomes pending before its unit moves to the trash.<Rule number="808.1.d.2" />
No pending ability can finalize until the current spell or ability finishes resolving.<Rule number="158.3" />

## Can Glasc Mixologist's Deathknell play a unit to the battlefield where he died? [#play-to-same-battlefield]

Yes, as long as you controlled that battlefield when Glasc Mixologist died.

**Outside a showdown**, the <Deathknell /> trigger remains on the chain as a pending item from the moment it is queued.
Because a chain exists, the turn remains in a closed state,<Rule number="309.1" /> and cleanup step 4 — which makes unoccupied battlefields uncontrolled — only applies during an open state.<Rule number="323.6" />
The battlefield stays under your control, so when the unit is finalized you can choose it as a valid destination.<Rule number="355.2.a" />

**During a showdown**, control of the battlefield cannot change until instructed by the steps of the combat or showdown,<Rule number="190.4.b" /> so the question does not arise.

## What happens when Glasc Mixologist is my only defender and his Deathknell plays a unit where he died? [#deathknell-combat-result]

If at least one attacker survives, the attackers heal before the new unit enters, neither player wins the original combat, and a second combat is staged immediately.
If no attacker survives, you win the original combat with the new unit and no second combat is staged.
Combat result is determined only after the combat cleanup and its associated chain items finish.<Rule number="466.2" />

1. The combat cleanup heals all units before Glasc's pending <Deathknell /> can be processed.<Rule number="466.1.a.1" /><Rule number="334.1" /><Rule number="334.2" />
   Because Glasc was your only defender, no defenders remain during cleanup step 3d, so the surviving attackers are not recalled.<Rule number="466.1.a.2" />
2. After the cleanup, Glasc's <Deathknell /> finalizes and resolves, playing the new unit to the battlefield.
3. If at least one attacker remains, both players have units there when combat result is determined.
   The result is "No Result."<Rule number="466.3.d" />
   A fresh combat is staged at that battlefield.<Rule number="466.3.d.1" />
4. If no attacker remains, you are the only player with a unit there and win the combat.<Rule number="466.3.a" />
   The No Result instruction that stages a follow-up combat does not apply.<Rule number="466.3.d.1" />
