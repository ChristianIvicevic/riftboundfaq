## Can Glasc Mixologist's Deathknell target a unit that died from the same event? [#same-event-target]

Yes, if it is a non-token unit card in your trash that meets Glasc's cost and rune restrictions.
Glasc's <Deathknell /> chooses its target when the ability finalizes, not when the ability first becomes pending.<Rule number="402.2" />
By then, every non-token unit killed by the same event has moved to its owner's trash.<Rule number="428.2" />
A killed token instead ceases to exist immediately after entering the trash, so it cannot be targeted.<Rule number="186.1" />

During a cleanup, relevant death triggers from units with lethal damage become pending before those units move to the trash.<Rule number="323.4" /><Rule number="323.5" />
Those pending abilities cannot be finalized until the cleanup is complete, so Glasc's target is chosen only after the killed unit cards have moved to the trash.<Rule number="334.2" />

When one effect instruction of a resolving spell or ability kills multiple units, their deaths are simultaneous.<Rule number="370.1.a.2" />
Each relevant <Deathknell /> becomes pending before its unit moves to the trash.<Rule number="808.1.d.2" />
No pending ability can finalize until the current spell or ability finishes resolving.<Rule number="158.3" />

## Can Glasc Mixologist's Deathknell play a unit to the battlefield where he died? [#play-to-same-battlefield]

Yes, as long as you controlled that battlefield when Glasc Mixologist died.

**Outside a showdown**, Glasc's <Deathknell /> is added as a pending item before he moves to the trash and remains pending throughout that cleanup.<Rule number="808.1.d.2" /><Rule number="334.2" />
That pending ability keeps the chain in progress throughout the cleanup, so you do not lose control of the battlefield even if Glasc's death leaves it empty.<Rule number="330" /><Rule number="309.1" /><Rule number="323.6" />
You can therefore choose that battlefield as a valid destination when the unit is finalized.<Rule number="355.2.a" />

**During a showdown**, control of the battlefield cannot change until instructed by the steps of the combat or showdown,<Rule number="190.4.b" /> so the question does not arise.

## What happens when Glasc Mixologist is my only defender and his Deathknell plays a unit where he died? [#deathknell-combat-result]

The original combat result is determined only after Glasc's chosen unit enters and every associated chain item finishes.<Rule number="466.2" />
At that point, the result depends on which players have units at the battlefield:

- **Only your units remain:** You win the original combat.<Rule number="466.3.a" />
- **Only your opponent's units remain:** Your opponent wins the original combat.<Rule number="466.3.a" />
- **Both players have units there:** Neither player wins, and a second combat is staged.<Rule number="466.3.d" /><Rule number="466.3.d.1" />
- **Neither player has units there:** Neither player wins, and no second combat is staged.<Rule number="466.3.d" /><Rule number="466.3.d.1" />

### Resolution Sequence [#combat-result-sequence]

<Steps>
<Step>
The combat cleanup heals all units before Glasc's pending <Deathknell /> can be finalized.<Rule number="466.1.a.1" /><Rule number="334.2" />
Because Glasc was your only defender, no defenders remain during cleanup, so the surviving attackers are not recalled.<Rule number="466.1.a.2" />
</Step>
<Step>
After the cleanup, Glasc's <Deathknell /> resolves and begins playing the chosen unit.
The unit becomes pending while the ability finishes.<Rule number="354.2" /><Rule number="354.3" />
</Step>
<Step>
The unit then finalizes and enters the battlefield exhausted.<Rule number="359.2.c" />
Any play effect and other resulting chain items also finish before combat result is determined.<Rule number="383.4.a.2" />
</Step>
<Step>
The players' units then determine who won the original combat and whether another combat is staged.
</Step>
</Steps>
