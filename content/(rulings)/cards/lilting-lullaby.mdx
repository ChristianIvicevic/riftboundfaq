## If my opponent targets my spell with Lilting Lullaby and I counter my own spell in response, can I still play spells? [#own-counter]

Yes.
Countering your spell clears it from the chain, so it is an illegal target when Lilting Lullaby resolves.<Rule number="425.1.a" /><Rule number="355.9.a.2" /><Rule number="359.3.e.2" />
Lilting Lullaby still resolves, but its counter instruction is ignored because the target is illegal.<Rule number="359.3.e.1" /><Rule number="359.3.e.5" />
The spell-play restriction is linked to that ignored instruction through "its controller," so the restriction is also ignored.<Rule number="359.3.e.14" /><Rule number="359.3.e.14.a" />
