## Does Applied Researchers reduce Repeat, Flow, and Deflect costs? [#other-spell-costs]

Yes, provided Applied Researchers is <Empowered /> and the cost belongs to a spell.
Its <Empowered /> ability grants this discount only while it has the <Empowered /> status.<Rule number="828.1.b.1" /><Rule number="828.1.c" />
See [When can I activate an Empower ability?](/mechanics/empower#empower-timing) for the normal timing of its <Empower /> ability.

<Repeat /> is an optional additional cost.<Rule number="820.1" /><Rule number="820.1.c.1" />
<Deflect /> imposes a mandatory additional cost on a spell or ability that targets the permanent with <Deflect />.<Rule number="356.2.a.2" /><Rule number="809.1.d" />
Discounts can reduce both kinds of additional costs, including to zero.<Rule number="356.4.f" />

A <Flow /> cost is an alternate cost that replaces a spell's base cost during finalization.<Rule number="829.1.c.1" />
Applied Researchers' discount is applied afterward when determining the spell's total cost.<Rule number="356.1" /><Rule number="356.4" />
See [How is a card's total cost determined?](/general-rules/costs-and-payments#total-cost-determination) for the complete ordered cost procedure.

Because Applied Researchers' discount applies only to spells, it cannot reduce a <Repeat /> or <Deflect /> cost paid while playing an ability.<Rule number="820.1.a" /><Rule number="809.1.d" />

## Can Applied Researchers reduce Shock Blast's cost to zero? [#shock-blast-cost]

Yes, if Applied Researchers is <Empowered />, you can play <Card name="Shock Blast" /> for <Energy value={0} />.
Applied Researchers itself satisfies Shock Blast's requirement that you control something that is <Empowered />, so both cards' discounts apply.

Both abilities discount Shock Blast's total cost during the Determine Total Cost step of playing it.<Rule number="356.4" />
Discounts that apply to the total cost may be applied in any order.<Rule number="356.4.d" /><Rule number="356.4.d.1" />
A minimum imposed by one discount applies only to that discount.<Rule number="356.4.e" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	Shock Blast starts with its base cost of <Energy value={3} /><Mind />.
	Apply Researchers' discount first, reducing that cost by <Energy value={1} /><Universal /> to <Energy value={2} /> and satisfying its minimum of <Energy value={1} />.
	Then apply Shock Blast's own discount, reducing the remaining energy cost by <Energy value={2} /> to <Energy value={0} />.
	Applying the discounts in the opposite order would leave the spell costing <Energy value={1} />, but you may choose the order that results in the lower cost.
</Callout>
