## Can an opponent's Deathknell target Akali, Silent after combat damage? [#deathknell-targeting]

Yes, Akali can be targeted by enemy <Deathknell /> abilities until combat ends, as long as she remains at the battlefield with a combat designation.

Combat has three steps:

<Steps>
<Step>
**Combat Showdown Step.**<Rule number="464" />
</Step>
<Step>
**Combat Damage Step.**<Rule number="465" />
</Step>
<Step>
**Resolution Step.**<Rule number="466" />
The combat <Term item="cleanup">cleanup</Term> triggers <Deathknell /> abilities from lethally damaged units.<Rule number="466.1" /><Rule number="323.4" />
Any targets are chosen when those abilities <Term item="finalization">finalize</Term>.<Rule number="402.2" />
Those abilities fully <Term item="resolution">resolve</Term> before combat ends.<Rule number="466.2" />
Combat ends only at the final part of this step.<Rule number="466.7" />
Only then does Akali stop being in combat and become untargetable by enemy spells and abilities.<Rule number="466.7.a" /><Rule number="740.2.c" />
</Step>
</Steps>

This is why <Card name="Ruined Rex" />'s <Deathknell /> can choose her after Rex dies to combat damage.
