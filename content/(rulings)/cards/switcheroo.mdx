## How does Switcheroo work? [#might-swap]

Switcheroo reverses the units' current might when it resolves, but it does not exchange their base might or set either unit's might to a new value.
The Swap action determines the difference between the two current values, then creates a might increase for the lower value and an equal might decrease for the higher value.<Rule number="433.1" /><Rule number="433.1.a" /><Rule number="433.1.b" />
Each increase or decrease lasts for Switcheroo's stated duration, this turn.<Rule number="433.1.a" />
If the units have equal might when Switcheroo resolves, the Swap has no effect.<Rule number="433.1.c" />

## Are existing equipment bonuses or passive effects counted again after Switcheroo resolves? [#existing-modifiers]

No, each existing modifier is counted once as part of the unit's current might, and it continues applying normally after Switcheroo resolves.<Rule number="476.1" /><Rule number="477.3.a" /><Rule number="477.3.d" />
Switcheroo calculates one difference from the complete current values and creates separate increase and decrease effects; it does not remove, reapply, or replace the existing modifiers.<Rule number="433.1.a" /><Rule number="433.1.b" />
For example, if a unit has 2 base might and an active +3 bonus, its current might is 5 for the Swap calculation.
After the Swap, the +3 remains one part of that unit's might rather than being added a second time.

## What happens when equipment detaches or passives stop applying after Switcheroo? [#later-modifier-changes]

Only the modifier that stops applying changes the unit's might.<Rule number="476.3" /><Rule number="477.3.a" />
Switcheroo's increase or decrease keeps its originally calculated amount until end of turn.
The Swap creates effects with fixed amounts when it resolves, so later changes do not cause it to recalculate the difference.<Rule number="433.1.a" /><Rule number="433.1.b" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	<Card name="Wuju Bladesman - Starter" /> grants a friendly unit +2 might while it defends alone.
	Suppose your <Card name="Pakaa Cub" /> has 3 might before that bonus and defends alone, while an opposing <Card name="Determined Sentry" /> attacks with 1 might.
	The bonus from Wuju Bladesman raises Pakaa Cub to 5 current might.
	Your opponent plays Switcheroo targeting both units.
	Switcheroo gives Pakaa Cub -4 might and Determined Sentry +4 might, leaving them at 1 and 5 might respectively.
	If Pakaa Cub stops defending alone, it loses that +2 bonus but keeps Switcheroo's -4 decrease, leaving it at -1 might.
</Callout>

## Can Switcheroo result in negative might? [#negative-might]

Yes, a unit can have negative might if a bonus included in Switcheroo's calculation stops applying while Switcheroo's decrease remains.<Rule number="433.1.a" /><Rule number="433.1.b" /><Rule number="476.3" />
Spells, abilities, and combat-damage assignment treat negative might as 0.<Rule number="143.2.b" />
Might increases and decreases instead use the actual negative value.<Rule number="143.2.b.1" />
The unit does not die merely for having 0 or negative might.
At cleanup, it is killed only if it has a non-zero amount of marked damage equal to or greater than its might.<Rule number="142.4.b" /><Rule number="323.5" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	A <Card name="Scuttle Crab" /> at -2 might is targeted with <Card name="Punch First" />, which reads "Give a unit +5 Might this turn."
	Scuttle Crab ends up with 3 might because the +5 increase uses its actual -2 value.
</Callout>
