## What happens if I play Elnuk Herd from face down to a battlefield with no other friendly units? [#self-target]

Elnuk Herd is the only legal target of its play effect, so it gets +3 might this turn and then recycles itself.

The play effect is added to the <Term item="chain">chain</Term> only after Elnuk Herd enters the battlefield.<Rule number="383.4.a.2" />
Its current text says "a friendly unit," so the ability can target Elnuk Herd itself because an ability and its source are separate objects.<Rule number="355.9.c" /><Rule number="355.10" />
Because Elnuk Herd was played from face down, the play effect must choose a target at that battlefield.<Rule number="811.1.d.3" />
With no other friendly unit there, Elnuk Herd must be chosen during <Term item="finalization">finalization</Term>.<Rule number="403.2" /><Rule number="403.4.b" />

When the ability <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term>, it gives Elnuk Herd +3 might before recycling it because Elnuk Herd was played from face down.

<Callout type="warn" title="Errata">
	Elnuk Herd received functional errata.
	Older printings say "another friendly unit"; the current text says "a friendly unit."
	Removing "another" allows Elnuk Herd to target itself.
</Callout>
