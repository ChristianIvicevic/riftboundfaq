## Does Abandoned Hall's ability trigger if the spell is countered? [#countered-spell]

No.
Abandoned Hall's "when a player plays a spell" condition is fulfilled only when that spell finishes <Term item="resolution">resolving</Term>.<Rule number="419.4.a" />
A countered spell does not resolve and is not considered played for triggered abilities, so Abandoned Hall's ability is not put on the <Term item="chain">chain</Term>.<Rule number="419.4.a.1" />

## Can a unit moved into or away from Abandoned Hall by a spell be targeted? [#movement-eligibility]

After the spell <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term>, Abandoned Hall can choose only a unit at Abandoned Hall controlled by the player who played that spell.<Rule number="190.6.c" /><Rule number="355.9.b" /><Rule number="355.10" />

- **Moved into Abandoned Hall:** The unit can be chosen if the spell's player controls the unit when Abandoned Hall's triggered ability <Term item="finalization">finalizes</Term>.
- **Moved away from Abandoned Hall:** The unit cannot be chosen, even if the spell's player controls the unit, because the unit is no longer at Abandoned Hall.

Abandoned Hall's trigger condition is not fulfilled until the spell has finished resolving.<Rule number="419.4.a" />
The resulting ability is then added as a <Term item="pending">pending</Term> item.<Rule number="383.3" /><Rule number="401.1" />
Abandoned Hall's triggered ability chooses its target when the ability finalizes.<Rule number="402.2" />

## Can Abandoned Hall's triggered ability choose a unit that a spell plays there? [#replay]

Yes, if the player who played the spell controls that unit when the ability <Term item="finalization">finalizes</Term>.
The spell starts playing the unit during its own <Term item="resolution">resolution</Term>, so the unit waits on the <Term item="chain">chain</Term> as a <Term item="pending">pending</Term> item.<Rule number="354.2" /><Rule number="354.3" />
When the spell finishes resolving, Abandoned Hall's triggered ability is added as a newer pending item.<Rule number="419.4.a" /><Rule number="383.3" /><Rule number="401.1" />
The unit finalizes before that newer ability because pending items finalize in append order.<Rule number="337.1.b" />
It immediately resolves and enters Abandoned Hall.<Rule number="337.2" /><Rule number="359.2.c" />
When the triggered ability later finalizes, it can select the unit played by the spell as its target.<Rule number="190.6.c" /><Rule number="355.9.b" /><Rule number="355.10" /><Rule number="402.2" />
