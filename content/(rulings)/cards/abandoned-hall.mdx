## Does Abandoned Hall's ability trigger if the spell is countered? [#countered-spell]

No.
Abandoned Hall's "when a player plays a spell" condition is fulfilled only when that spell finishes resolving.<Rule number="419.4.a" />
A countered spell does not resolve and is not considered played for triggered abilities, so Abandoned Hall's ability is not put on the chain.<Rule number="419.4.a.1" /><Rule number="425.1.b" />

See [What does "play" mean on a card?](/general-rules/playing-cards#play-definition) for the complete distinction between starting, finalizing, and completing a play.

## Can a unit moved into or away from Abandoned Hall by a spell be targeted? [#movement-eligibility]

After the spell resolves, the player who played it can target a unit they control at Abandoned Hall, including one moved there by that spell but not one moved away.<Rule number="190.6.c" /><Rule number="355.9.b" /><Rule number="355.10" />
Abandoned Hall's trigger condition is evaluated only after the spell's resolution has completed.<Rule number="383.2.c" /><Rule number="419.4.a" />
The resulting ability is then added as a pending item.<Rule number="383.3" />
Its target is selected when that ability finalizes.<Rule number="402.2" />

## Can a unit replayed here by a spell be chosen by Abandoned Hall's triggered ability? [#replay]

Yes, if the unit's replay succeeds at Abandoned Hall and the player who played the spell controls the replayed unit.
The spell starts playing the unit during its own resolution, so the unit waits on the chain as a pending item.<Rule number="354.2" /><Rule number="354.3" />
When the spell finishes resolving, Abandoned Hall's triggered ability is added as a newer pending item.<Rule number="419.4.a" /><Rule number="383.2.c" /><Rule number="383.3" />
The unit finalizes before that newer ability because pending items finalize in append order.<Rule number="337.1.b" />
It immediately resolves and enters Abandoned Hall.<Rule number="337.2" /><Rule number="359.2.c" />
When the triggered ability later finalizes, it can select the replayed unit as its target.<Rule number="190.6.c" /><Rule number="355.9.b" /><Rule number="355.10" /><Rule number="402.2" />

See [In what order are multiple pending chain items finalized and resolved?](/general-rules/chain-and-priority#pending-item-order) for the general ordering rules.

### Resolution Sequence [#replay-sequence]

Taking <Card name="Arcane Shift" /> replaying its controller's own unit as an example:

1. Arcane Shift banishes the friendly unit.
2. The card's owner starts playing it, moving it to the chain as a pending item.<Rule number="354.2" /><Rule number="419.3" />
   The play process waits while Arcane Shift continues resolving.<Rule number="354.3" />
3. Arcane Shift deals its damage, banishes itself, and finishes resolving.
4. Abandoned Hall's trigger condition is now fulfilled, so its ability is added after the unit as another pending item.<Rule number="383.2.c" /><Rule number="383.3" /><Rule number="419.4.a" />
5. After outstanding tasks finish, the unit finalizes before Abandoned Hall's newer pending ability.<Rule number="334.2" /><Rule number="337.1.b" />
6. The unit immediately resolves and enters Abandoned Hall.<Rule number="337.2" /><Rule number="359.2.c" />
7. When Abandoned Hall's ability later finalizes, it can target the replayed unit controlled by the player who played Arcane Shift.<Rule number="190.6.c" /><Rule number="191.1" /><Rule number="355.9.b" /><Rule number="355.10" /><Rule number="402.2" />
