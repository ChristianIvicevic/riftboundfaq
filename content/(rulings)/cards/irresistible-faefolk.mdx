## If Irresistible Faefolk moves to an open battlefield outside a showdown and pulls an enemy unit there, who is the attacker? [#combat-attacker]

You, the player who moved Irresistible Faefolk, are the attacker.
In this setup, Irresistible Faefolk applies contested status for you when it moves to the battlefield.<Rule number="190.3.a.1" />

When Irresistible Faefolk's ability moves the enemy unit there, the following cleanup stages combat because opposing units are present at the contested battlefield.<Rule number="319.8" /><Rule number="323.9" />
When combat opens, the player whose unit applied contested status is the attacker.<Rule number="464.2.c.1" />
The opponent whose unit was pulled in is the defender.<Rule number="464.2.c.2" />

If a non-combat showdown is already ongoing there when the enemy unit arrives, see [What happens if an opposing player's unit moves into an existing non-combat showdown?](/general-rules/showdowns#non-combat-showdown) for the conversion procedure.

## If I play Irresistible Faefolk directly to a battlefield, does its ability trigger? [#playing-vs-moving]

No.
A move is defined as a permanent changing its position from one location on the board to another.<Rule number="446.1" />
Playing a card involves it entering the board from a non-board zone (such as your hand), which is a zone change, not a move.<Rule number="446.2" />

See [Does a unit move when it enters the board?](/general-rules/movement#entering-board) for the general entry and movement distinction.

## If an opponent responds to my move trigger by removing Irresistible Faefolk from the battlefield, does the enemy unit still move? [#removed-in-response]

Yes, if the triggered ability resolves and its move instruction can still be followed.
Removing Irresistible Faefolk does not counter or remove the ability from the chain.<Rule number="425.1" /><Rule number="425.2.a" />

Irresistible Faefolk's trigger condition is moving to a battlefield, and "that battlefield" in the effect is a referent to the battlefield named in that trigger condition.
Information referenced from a trigger condition is checked when the trigger condition is fulfilled, not when the ability resolves.<Rule number="359.3.f.3" />
The battlefield was therefore fixed when Irresistible Faefolk moved there, so the move instruction does not need Irresistible Faefolk's location when it executes.
