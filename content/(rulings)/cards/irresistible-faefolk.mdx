## If Irresistible Faefolk moves to an uncontested, uncontrolled battlefield outside a showdown and pulls an enemy unit there, who is the attacker? [#combat-attacker]

Irresistible Faefolk's controller is the attacker.
Irresistible Faefolk reaches the battlefield before its triggered ability can pull the enemy unit.<Rule number="383.2.c" />
It contests that battlefield for its controller because no one controls it and it was not already contested.<Rule number="190.3.a.1" />

Its ability then pulls the enemy unit there.
With opposing units now at the contested battlefield, the next <Term item="cleanup">cleanup</Term> marks it for combat.<Rule number="319.8" /><Rule number="323.9" />
When that combat begins, Irresistible Faefolk's controller is the attacker and the pulled unit's controller is the defender because Irresistible Faefolk was the unit that contested the battlefield.<Rule number="464.2.c.1" /><Rule number="464.2.c.2" />

## If I play Irresistible Faefolk directly to a battlefield, does its ability trigger? [#playing-vs-moving]

No.
A move is defined as a permanent changing its position from one location on the board to another.<Rule number="446.1" />
When you play Irresistible Faefolk, it enters the board from a non-board zone (such as your hand), which is a zone change, not a move.<Rule number="446.2" />

## If an opponent responds to my move trigger by removing Irresistible Faefolk from the battlefield, does the enemy unit still move? [#removed-in-response]

Yes, removing Irresistible Faefolk by itself does not stop the enemy unit from moving.
Removing Irresistible Faefolk is not a counter instruction.<Rule number="425.1" /><Rule number="425.2.a" />
Its already-<Term item="finalization">finalized</Term> ability remains on the <Term item="chain">chain</Term> and executes like a spell.<Rule number="406.5" />

Irresistible Faefolk's trigger condition identifies the battlefield it moved to, so the ability records that battlefield when it triggers.<Rule number="359.3.f.3" />
Removing Irresistible Faefolk later does not erase that destination, so the chosen enemy unit can still move there when the ability <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term>.
