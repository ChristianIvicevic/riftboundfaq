## If Sacrifice is countered, is the killed unit returned? [#countered-additional-cost]

No.
Killing a friendly <Mighty /> unit is an additional cost paid while playing Sacrifice.<Rule number="356.7" /><Rule number="357.2" />
Countering the spell later does not refund that cost, so the killed unit is not returned.<Rule number="425.1.c" /><Rule number="425.1.c.1" />
See [Are paid costs refunded if a card or ability is countered?](/general-rules/costs-and-payments#countered-costs) for the general rule.

## Can my opponent interact before the Mighty unit chosen for Sacrifice's additional cost is killed? [#cost-reaction-window]

No normal reaction window opens between choosing the <Mighty /> unit and killing it.
Costs are paid during step 4 of finalization, and finalization does not pass priority.<Rule number="357.2" /><Rule number="337.1.a" />
During that step, Sacrifice's controller may activate an eligible <Add /> ability with <Reaction /> when resources are required, even without priority.<Rule number="357.1.a" /><Rule number="429.3" /><Rule number="429.3.a" />
This limited exception does not create a normal reaction window or permit an opponent to play a spell like <Card name="Stupefy" /> between choosing and killing the unit.

The unit killed as the cost is also not a target because objects referenced only as part of a cost are excluded from targeting.<Rule number="355.10.c" />
See [Can players react while costs are being paid?](/general-rules/costs-and-payments#cost-reaction-window) and [What makes something a target?](/general-rules/targeting#target-definition) for the complete procedures.

## Does the Deathknell of the unit killed as the cost resolve before Sacrifice draws and channels? [#deathknell-before-spell]

If the killed unit's <Deathknell /> is finalized and resolves, yes.
Sacrifice kills the unit while paying a non-standard additional cost during finalization.<Rule number="356.7" /><Rule number="357.2" />
The <Deathknell /> is therefore added above Sacrifice as a pending item before the unit enters the trash.<Rule number="808.1.d.2" />
See [Killed as a cost](/mechanics/deathknell#killed-as-cost) for how this order applies to other cards and abilities.

### Resolution Sequence [#deathknell-sequence]

1. Sacrifice is put on the chain and proceeds through finalization.
2. Its controller pays the additional cost by killing a friendly <Mighty /> unit.
3. That unit's <Deathknell /> is added above Sacrifice as a pending item before the unit moves to the trash.<Rule number="808.1.d.2" />
4. Sacrifice finishes finalizing.<Rule number="359.3.a" />
5. The pending <Deathknell /> is then finalized.<Rule number="359.3.b" />
6. Once no pending items remain, the controller of the newest item gains priority.<Rule number="337.4" />
7. After all players pass priority without adding another item, the chain proceeds to resolution.<Rule number="339.1" />
8. The <Deathknell /> is newer than Sacrifice, so it resolves first.<Rule number="340.1" />
9. Sacrifice draws 2 and channels 1 rune exhausted only when Sacrifice later resolves.

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
  You kill <Card name="Rift Herald" />, whose <Deathknell /> reads "Play a unit from your hand to your base, ignoring its Energy cost," to pay Sacrifice's cost.
  If Rift Herald's <Deathknell /> resolves and the instructed play succeeds, you play a unit from your pre-draw hand while ignoring its energy cost but still paying its power cost.<Rule number="356.1.b.2" />
  Sacrifice resolves afterward, so you then draw 2 and channel 1 rune exhausted.
</Callout>
