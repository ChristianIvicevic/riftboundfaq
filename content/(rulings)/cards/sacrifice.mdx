## If Sacrifice is countered, is the killed unit returned? [#countered-additional-cost]

No.
Killing a friendly <Mighty /> unit is an additional cost paid while playing Sacrifice.<Rule number="356.7" /><Rule number="357.2" />
Countering the spell later does not refund that cost, so the killed unit is not returned.<Rule number="425.1.c" /><Rule number="425.1.c.1" />

## Can my opponent play a reaction after I choose the Mighty unit for Sacrifice's additional cost but before I kill it? [#cost-reaction-window]

No.
Your opponent has no normal reaction window after you choose the <Mighty /> unit but before you kill it.
Costs are paid during step 4 of finalization, and finalization does not pass priority.<Rule number="357.2" /><Rule number="337.1.a" />
During that step, as Sacrifice's controller, you may activate an eligible <Add /> ability with <Reaction /> when resources are required, even without priority.<Rule number="357.1.a" />
This limited exception does not create a normal reaction window or permit your opponent to play a spell like <Card name="Stupefy" /> between choosing and killing the unit.

## Does the Deathknell of the unit killed as the cost resolve before Sacrifice draws and channels? [#deathknell-before-spell]

Yes.
The unit is killed while Sacrifice's additional cost is being paid.<Rule number="356.7" /><Rule number="357.2" />
Its <Deathknell /> is therefore added above Sacrifice as a pending ability.<Rule number="808.1.d.2" />
The <Deathknell /> resolves before Sacrifice draws and channels.<Rule number="337.2" /><Rule number="340.1" />

### Resolution Sequence [#deathknell-sequence]

<Steps>
<Step>
Sacrifice moves to the chain as a pending spell.<Rule number="354.2" />
Its controller then pays the additional cost by killing a friendly <Mighty /> unit.
</Step>
<Step>
That unit's <Deathknell /> is added above Sacrifice as a pending ability before the unit moves to the trash.<Rule number="808.1.d.2" />
</Step>
<Step>
Sacrifice finishes finalizing.<Rule number="359.3.a" />
The pending <Deathknell /> then finalizes.<Rule number="337.1" /><Rule number="359.3.b" /><Rule number="406.2" />
</Step>
<Step>
The <Deathknell /> resolves before Sacrifice.
Sacrifice then resolves, drawing 2 and channeling 1 rune exhausted.<Rule number="359.3.d" />
</Step>
</Steps>
