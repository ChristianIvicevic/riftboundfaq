## Can I banish and play Nocturne, Horrifying if Burn or Minefield puts it into my trash? [#burn-and-minefield]

No.
Neither <Burn /> nor <Card name="Minefield" /> instructs you to look at or reveal Nocturne, so neither effect fulfills the condition of Nocturne's replacement effect.

<Callout type="warn" title="Rules citation needed">
	"Look" does not have a standalone numbered action definition in Core Rules 1.4.
	Unlike reveal, the rules do not expressly state that a player looks at cards only when instructed.
	This ruling relies on treating "look at" as an instructed event; an official definition is still needed.
</Callout>

Nocturne's replacement-effect condition, "As you look at or reveal me from the top of your deck," does not itself instruct you to look at or reveal it.<Rule number="135.2.b.6" /><Rule number="369.1" />
It can apply only while a qualifying look or reveal event is being processed.<Rule number="370.1" />

<Burn /> puts cards from the top of your main deck directly into your trash.<Rule number="440.1" />
Minefield's printed ability, "When you conquer here, put the top 2 cards of your Main Deck into your trash," likewise contains no look or reveal instruction.
In either case, Nocturne becomes public when it reaches the trash,<Rule number="108.2.d" /> but becoming public does not mean a player looked at or revealed it from the top of their deck.
