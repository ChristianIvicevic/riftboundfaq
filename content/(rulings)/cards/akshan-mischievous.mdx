## If my Dazzling Aurora plays Akshan, Mischievous at the end of my turn, will the Dazzling Aurora I take from my opponent trigger that turn? [#aurora-timing]

No.
You gain control of the enemy <Card name="Dazzling Aurora" /> after its end-of-turn trigger condition has already been checked.

"At the end of your turn" refers to the Ending Step's end-of-turn point.<Rule number="317.1.a" /><Rule number="383.1" />
At that point, the enemy Dazzling Aurora is still controlled by your opponent, so its controller-relative "your turn" condition does not refer to your turn.<Rule number="191.4.a" /><Rule number="383.2.c" />

Your Dazzling Aurora's end-of-turn ability plays Akshan after that point in the turn has been reached.
After Akshan enters the board, his play effect is added to the chain as a pending item.<Rule number="383.4.a" /><Rule number="383.4.a.2" />
You gain control of the enemy Dazzling Aurora only when that ability later resolves.<Rule number="406.5" />

If you take control of an enemy Dazzling Aurora before the Ending Step, it triggers normally at the end of that turn.

## If my opponent and I each use Akshan, Mischievous to take the same gear, who controls it when either Akshan dies? [#dual-akshan-control]

The most recently played Akshan takes precedence while both are on the board.
If either Akshan dies, the surviving Akshan controls the gear.

Each resolved play effect creates a delayed passive ability, a control effect that remains active until that Akshan leaves the board.<Rule number="390.4" /><Rule number="391" />
While both effects remain, they independently change the gear's controller in the same trait-altering layer.<Rule number="477.1.a" /><Rule number="478" />
The effect established most recently applies last and determines who controls the gear.<Rule number="480" /><Rule number="480.1" /><Rule number="480.3" />

When an Akshan dies, that Akshan's control effect ends and the surviving effect determines control.<Rule number="428.2" />
