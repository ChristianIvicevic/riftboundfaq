## Does Brynhir Thundersong stop cards already on the chain? [#existing-cards]

No.
A card already on the chain has already started its play because playing a card means placing it there and queuing it for finalization.<Rule number="419.1" />
Once Brynhir Thundersong's play effect resolves, it prevents opponents from starting further plays that turn, but it does not stop cards already on the chain from finalizing or resolving.

If one of those cards later instructs an opponent to play another card, Brynhir can still make the new play impossible.
That instruction is skipped, so the second card stays in its current zone while the card already on the chain continues resolving.<Rule number="055" /><Rule number="358.3.a" />

See also [What does "play" mean on a card?](/general-rules/playing-cards#play-definition).
For the interaction with Promising Future, see [Does Brynhir Thundersong stop an opponent's card chosen with Promising Future from being played?](/cards/promising-future#brynhir-thundersong).
