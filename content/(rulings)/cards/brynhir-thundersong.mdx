## Does Brynhir Thundersong stop cards already on the chain? [#existing-cards]

No.
Once Brynhir Thundersong's play effect resolves, opponents cannot play further cards that turn, but cards already on the chain are unaffected.

To play a card means to place it on the chain and queue it for finalization.<Rule number="419.1" />
A pending card has already been placed on the chain and queued for finalization,<Rule number="329.1" /><Rule number="354.2" /> and a finalized card has progressed beyond that point.<Rule number="329.3" />
Brynhir Thundersong therefore does not stop either from finalizing or resolving.

See also [What does "play" mean on a card?](/general-rules/playing-cards#play-definition).
