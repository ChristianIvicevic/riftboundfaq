## Does Brynhir Thundersong stop cards already on the chain? [#existing-cards]

No.
A card already on the <Term item="chain">chain</Term> has already started its play because playing a card means placing it there and queuing it for <Term item="finalization">finalization</Term>.<Rule number="419.1" />
Once Brynhir Thundersong's play effect <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term>, it prevents opponents from starting further plays that turn, but it does not stop cards already on the chain from finalizing or resolving.

If one of those cards later instructs an opponent to play another card, Brynhir can still make the new play impossible.
Brynhir prevents that new play, so the second card stays in its current zone while the card already on the chain continues resolving.<Rule number="358.3.a" />
