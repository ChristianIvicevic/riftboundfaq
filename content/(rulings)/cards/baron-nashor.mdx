## Does Baron Nashor move to the Baron Pit when it's created as part of playing him? [#creation-movement]

No.
Baron Nashor enters the <Card name="Baron Pit" /> directly because his ability replaces entry at his original play location with entry at the newly added Baron Pit.<Rule number="369.3" />
Moving means changing a permanent's position from one space on the board to another, while entering the board from another zone is a zone change.<Rule number="446.1" /><Rule number="446.2" />
See [Does a unit move when it enters the board?](/general-rules/movement#entering-board) for the general entry and movement distinction.

## What happens after Baron Nashor enters the Baron Pit? [#baron-pit-showdown]

A showdown is staged and normally opens during the resulting cleanup.<Rule number="316.8.b" /><Rule number="323.8" /><Rule number="323.12" />
If more than one showdown could begin at that point, the turn player chooses which one begins.<Rule number="323.12" />
The Baron Pit remains uncontrolled until that showdown or a resulting combat ends.<Rule number="439.4.b" /><Rule number="190.4" />

The showdown closes after every player passes focus in sequence without playing a spell or activating an ability.<Rule number="347.2.a" /><Rule number="348" />
See [When does a showdown close?](/general-rules/showdowns#showdown-close) for the general closing procedure.
If only Baron Nashor's controller's units remain, that player gains control and conquers if they have not scored the Baron Pit this turn.<Rule number="190.4" /><Rule number="469.1" />

## Does the Baron Pit need to be scored to win by conquering? [#baron-pit-victory]

Yes, when the potential winning point would come from conquering.
At match point, a player gains the final point from a conquer only if they have scored every battlefield on the board that turn, including the Baron Pit.<Rule number="471.1.b" /><Rule number="471.1.b.1" />
If they have not scored every battlefield, they draw a card instead.<Rule number="471.1.b.1" />

## Can a unit survive combat damage that becomes lethal when Baron Nashor dies in the same combat? [#might-loss-survival]

Yes, if the unit does not have lethal damage while Baron Nashor's +2 might is still applying.
A cleanup has one step that kills all units with lethal damage at that time.<Rule number="323.5" />
Baron Nashor and the other unit reach that step together, so the other unit still has +2 might when its lethal damage is checked.<Rule number="323.5" /><Rule number="365.1" />
Baron Nashor then leaves and the bonus disappears.<Rule number="365.1" />
The other unit now has lethal damage, but it isn't killed because this cleanup's lethal-damage kill step has already passed.<Rule number="323.5" />
The combat cleanup heals the other unit before the cleanup caused by Baron Nashor's departure can begin.<Rule number="319.6" /><Rule number="322" /><Rule number="466.1.a.1" />

### Resolution Sequence [#might-loss-survival-sequence]

Suppose Baron Nashor is assigned lethal combat damage and a friendly <Card name="Evelynn, Entrancing" /> is assigned 2 combat damage.

1. **Step 3b:** Baron Nashor is killed because he has lethal damage, while Evelynn survives because her 2 marked damage is less than her current 4 might: 2 printed might plus Baron Nashor's +2.<Rule number="142.4.b" /><Rule number="323.5" />
2. Baron Nashor's passive ability stops applying when he leaves the board, so Evelynn returns to 2 might after step 3b has finished.<Rule number="365.1" />
3. **Step 3c:** The combat special cleanup heals all units, clearing Evelynn's marked damage.<Rule number="466.1.a.1" />
4. Baron Nashor's departure causes another cleanup.<Rule number="319.6" /><Rule number="322" /> Evelynn has no marked damage when that cleanup reaches its lethal-damage kill step, so she is not killed.<Rule number="323.5" />

## Can I play Baron Nashor while an opponent's Mageseeker Warden is at a battlefield? [#warden-play-lock]

Yes.
You choose your base as Baron Nashor's play location because <Card name="Mageseeker Warden" /> makes it the only legal option.<Rule number="355.2" /><Rule number="355.2.a" />

If the <Card name="Baron Pit" /> is not on the board, Baron Nashor's "as you play me" instruction adds it and his replacement effect changes his entry from your chosen base to the Baron Pit.<Rule number="135.2.b.3" /><Rule number="369.3" />

If the Baron Pit is already on the board, Baron Nashor does not add it, so the conditional replacement does not apply and he enters your chosen base.<Rule number="359.2.c" /><Rule number="369.3" />
