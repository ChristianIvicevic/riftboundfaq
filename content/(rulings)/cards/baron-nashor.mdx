## Does Baron Nashor move to the Baron Pit when it's created as part of playing him? [#creation-movement]

No.
Baron Nashor enters the <Card name="Baron Pit" /> directly; he does not move there.

Moving means changing position between spaces on the board, while entering the board from another zone is a zone change.<Rule number="448.1" /><Rule number="448.2" />
When Baron Nashor creates the Baron Pit, his replacement effect changes his entry location from the originally chosen location to the Baron Pit.<Rule number="369.3" />

## Can Hunters' Circle reduce Baron Nashor's cost when he creates the Baron Pit? [#hunters-circle-discount]

Yes, if you choose <Card name="Hunters' Circle" /> as Baron Nashor's play location and he is the first unit or gear you play there on your turn; Hunters' Circle reduces his cost by <Energy value={1} /> even though he enters the <Card name="Baron Pit" /> instead.

A unit's play location is chosen before its cost is determined, so Hunters' Circle's discount applies.<Rule number="355.2" /><Rule number="356.4" />
Baron Nashor's replacement effect later changes only where he enters.<Rule number="369.3" />
It does not change his chosen play location.<Rule number="355.15" />
His cost has already been determined and paid by then, so it is not recalculated.<Rule number="356" /><Rule number="357" /><Rule number="359.2.c" />

## Does The Exile make Baron Nashor enter a newly created Baron Pit ready? [#exile-ready-entry]

If you control <Card name="The Exile" /> and choose a battlefield you control as Baron Nashor's play location, Baron Nashor enters the newly created <Card name="Baron Pit" /> ready regardless of which replacement effect applies first.

The Exile checks the battlefield Baron Nashor was played to, not the battlefield where he enters.
The chosen battlefield remains his play location when his replacement effect changes his entry location to the Baron Pit.<Rule number="355.2" /><Rule number="355.15" /><Rule number="369.3" />
One replacement effect changes the entry location, and the other makes Baron Nashor enter ready.
Applying either one first leaves the other applicable to Baron Nashor's modified entry.<Rule number="370.1" /><Rule number="370.2" />
The effects can be applied in either order.<Rule number="372" />
Both orders therefore produce the same result.

## What happens after Baron Nashor enters the Baron Pit? [#baron-pit-showdown]

Baron Nashor's entry contests the newly created <Card name="Baron Pit" /> and leads to a non-combat showdown there.
The Baron Pit remains uncontrolled until that showdown closes.

### Entry and Showdown Opening [#showdown-opening-sequence]

<Steps>
<Step>
The Baron Pit is created uncontrolled.<Rule number="440.4.b" />
</Step>
<Step>
Baron Nashor enters it and contests it for his controller.<Rule number="190.3.a.1" />
</Step>
<Step>
His entry causes a <Term item="cleanup">cleanup</Term>, which stages a showdown at the Baron Pit.<Rule number="319.6" /><Rule number="316.8.b" />
</Step>
<Step>
That showdown normally begins during the cleanup.
If multiple showdowns are ready to begin, the turn player chooses which one begins.<Rule number="323.11" />
</Step>
<Step>
The Baron Pit remains uncontrolled while the showdown is ongoing.<Rule number="190.4.b" />
</Step>
</Steps>

### Closing and Conquest [#closing-conquest]

The showdown closes after every player passes <Term item="focus">focus</Term> in sequence without playing a card or activating an ability.<Rule number="347.2.a" /><Rule number="348" />
If Baron Nashor's controller is then the only player with units there, they establish control of the Baron Pit and conquer it if they have not scored it that turn.<Rule number="348.2.a" /><Rule number="348.2.a.1" />

## After Baron Nashor creates the Baron Pit, does it need to be scored to win by conquering? [#baron-pit-victory]

Yes, when the potential winning point would come from conquering.
At match point, a player gains the final point from a conquer only if they have scored every battlefield on the board that turn, including the <Card name="Baron Pit" />.<Rule number="473.1.b" /><Rule number="473.1.b.1" />
If they have not scored every battlefield, they draw a card instead.<Rule number="473.1.b.1" />

## Can a unit survive combat damage that becomes lethal when Baron Nashor dies in the same combat? [#might-loss-survival]

Yes, if the unit does not have lethal damage when the <Term item="cleanup">cleanup</Term> checks it while Baron Nashor's +2 might still applies.
The cleanup checks all units for lethal damage only once, so a unit that becomes lethal after Baron Nashor leaves is not checked again during that cleanup.<Rule number="323.4" />

### Steps [#might-loss-survival-sequence]

Suppose Baron Nashor is assigned lethal combat damage and a friendly <Card name="Evelynn, Entrancing" /> is assigned 2 combat damage.

<Steps>
<Step>
**Lethal-damage check:** Baron Nashor has lethal damage, while Evelynn's 2 marked damage is less than her current 4 might: 2 printed might plus Baron Nashor's +2.<Rule number="142.4.b" />
Of the two, only Baron Nashor has lethal damage during the check, so the cleanup kills him and not Evelynn.<Rule number="323.4" />
</Step>
<Step>
Baron Nashor's passive ability stops applying when he leaves the board.<Rule number="365.1" />
Evelynn therefore returns to 2 might after the lethal-damage check has finished.
Her 2 marked damage is now lethal.<Rule number="142.4.b" />
That cleanup does not check her again.
</Step>
<Step>
**Combat healing:** The combat special cleanup heals all units, clearing Evelynn's marked damage.<Rule number="468.1.a.1" />
</Step>
<Step>
Baron Nashor's departure causes another cleanup.<Rule number="319.6" /><Rule number="322" />
Evelynn has no marked damage when that cleanup checks for lethal damage, so she is not killed.<Rule number="323.4" />
</Step>
</Steps>

## Can I play Baron Nashor while an opponent's Mageseeker Warden is at a battlefield? [#warden-play-lock]

Yes.
You choose your base as Baron Nashor's play location because <Card name="Mageseeker Warden" /> makes it the only legal option.<Rule number="355.2" /><Rule number="355.2.a" />

If the <Card name="Baron Pit" /> is not on the board, Baron Nashor's "as you play me" instruction adds it and his replacement effect changes his entry from your chosen base to the Baron Pit.<Rule number="135.2.b.3" /><Rule number="369.3" />

If the Baron Pit is already on the board, Baron Nashor does not add it, so the conditional replacement does not apply and he enters your chosen base.<Rule number="359.2.c" /><Rule number="369.3" />
