## Does Baron Nashor move to the Baron Pit when it's created as part of playing him? [#creation-movement]

No.
Baron Nashor enters the <Card name="Baron Pit" /> directly; he does not move there.

Moving means changing position between spaces on the board, while entering the board from another zone is a zone change.<Rule number="446.1" /><Rule number="446.2" />
When Baron Nashor creates the Baron Pit, his replacement effect changes his entry location from the originally chosen location to the Baron Pit.<Rule number="369.3" />

## What happens after Baron Nashor enters the Baron Pit? [#baron-pit-showdown]

Baron Nashor's entry contests the newly created <Card name="Baron Pit" /> and leads to a non-combat showdown there.
The Baron Pit remains uncontrolled until that showdown closes.

### Entry and Showdown Opening [#showdown-opening-sequence]

<Steps>
<Step>
The Baron Pit is created uncontrolled.<Rule number="439.4.b" />
</Step>
<Step>
Baron Nashor enters it and contests it for his controller.<Rule number="190.3.a.1" />
</Step>
<Step>
His entry causes a cleanup, which stages a showdown at the Baron Pit.<Rule number="319.6" /><Rule number="316.8.b" />
</Step>
<Step>
That showdown normally begins during the cleanup.
If multiple showdowns are ready to begin, the turn player chooses which one begins.<Rule number="323.12" />
</Step>
<Step>
The Baron Pit remains uncontrolled while the showdown is ongoing.<Rule number="190.4.b" />
</Step>
</Steps>

### Closing and Conquest [#closing-conquest]

The showdown closes after every player passes focus in sequence without playing a spell or activating an ability.<Rule number="347.2.a" /><Rule number="348" />
If Baron Nashor's controller is then the only player with units there, they establish control of the Baron Pit and conquer it if they have not scored it that turn.<Rule number="348.2.a" /><Rule number="348.2.a.1" />

## Does the Baron Pit need to be scored to win by conquering? [#baron-pit-victory]

Yes, when the potential winning point would come from conquering.
At match point, a player gains the final point from a conquer only if they have scored every battlefield on the board that turn, including the <Card name="Baron Pit" />.<Rule number="471.1.b" /><Rule number="471.1.b.1" />
If they have not scored every battlefield, they draw a card instead.<Rule number="471.1.b.1" />

## Can a unit survive combat damage that becomes lethal when Baron Nashor dies in the same combat? [#might-loss-survival]

Yes, if the unit does not have lethal damage when the cleanup checks it while Baron Nashor's +2 might still applies.
The cleanup checks all units for lethal damage only once, so a unit that becomes lethal after Baron Nashor leaves is not checked again during that cleanup.<Rule number="323.5" />

### Resolution Sequence [#might-loss-survival-sequence]

Suppose Baron Nashor is assigned lethal combat damage and a friendly <Card name="Evelynn, Entrancing" /> is assigned 2 combat damage.

<Steps>
<Step>
**Lethal-damage check:** Baron Nashor has lethal damage, while Evelynn's 2 marked damage is less than her current 4 might: 2 printed might plus Baron Nashor's +2.<Rule number="142.4.b" />
Of the two, only Baron Nashor has lethal damage during the check, so the cleanup kills him and not Evelynn.<Rule number="323.5" />
</Step>
<Step>
Baron Nashor's passive ability stops applying when he leaves the board.<Rule number="365.1" />
Evelynn therefore returns to 2 might after the lethal-damage check has finished.
Her 2 marked damage is now lethal.<Rule number="142.4.b" />
That cleanup does not check her again.
</Step>
<Step>
**Combat healing:** The combat special cleanup heals all units, clearing Evelynn's marked damage.<Rule number="466.1.a.1" />
</Step>
<Step>
Baron Nashor's departure causes another cleanup.<Rule number="319.6" /><Rule number="322" />
Evelynn has no marked damage when that cleanup checks for lethal damage, so she is not killed.<Rule number="323.5" />
</Step>
</Steps>

## Can I play Baron Nashor while an opponent's Mageseeker Warden is at a battlefield? [#warden-play-lock]

Yes.
You choose your base as Baron Nashor's play location because <Card name="Mageseeker Warden" /> makes it the only legal option.<Rule number="355.2" /><Rule number="355.2.a" />

If the <Card name="Baron Pit" /> is not on the board, Baron Nashor's "as you play me" instruction adds it and his replacement effect changes his entry from your chosen base to the Baron Pit.<Rule number="135.2.b.3" /><Rule number="369.3" />

If the Baron Pit is already on the board, Baron Nashor does not add it, so the conditional replacement does not apply and he enters your chosen base.<Rule number="359.2.c" /><Rule number="369.3" />
