## When do I pay 1 energy and return a unit for Emperor's Dais? [#ability-finalization-cost]

You pay the energy and return the unit while the triggered ability is being finalized, before opponents receive the normal opportunity to play Reactions to the ability.<Rule number="406.4" />

Because "you may" is the first part of the effect, you choose whether to perform the ability during finalization.<Rule number="383.3.a" />
The immediately following "pay <Energy value={1} /> and return a unit ... to play" is a cost within instructions, so both actions form the triggered ability's base cost and must be completed for the ability to finalize.<Rule number="383.3.b" /><Rule number="383.3.b.1" /><Rule number="404.1" /><Rule number="740.4.a" />
While paying the energy, you may activate an eligible <Add /> ability with <Reaction /> to provide it.<Rule number="444.2.c" />

Returning the unit is part of the cost, so that unit is not a target and returns to its owner's hand during finalization rather than resolution.<Rule number="355.10.c" /><Rule number="355.10.c.1" />
If no legal unit can be chosen, the pending ability is removed from the chain without finalizing.<Rule number="402.4" />
If you decline to pay the entire cost, the pending ability is removed from the chain without finalizing.<Rule number="404.2" />
If you complete the cost, the ability becomes a finalized chain item, and opponents may react before it resolves.<Rule number="406.2" /><Rule number="406.4" />
When it resolves, you play a 2 Might Sand Soldier unit token at Emperor's Dais.

<Callout type="warn" title="Errata">
	Emperor's Dais received functional errata with the release of Vendetta.
	Older printings say "you may pay <Energy value={1} /> and return a unit you control here to its owner's hand. If you do, play" the Sand Soldier token; its current text says "you may pay <Energy value={1} /> and return a unit you control here to its owner's hand to play" the token instead.
	This changes the energy payment and unit return from instructions performed during resolution into one cost completed while finalizing the ability.
</Callout>
