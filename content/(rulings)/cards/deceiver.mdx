## How do Reactions that remove units interact with Deceiver's Reflection? [#copy-target-sequence]

Deceiver's controller chooses the copy target when the separate reflexive trigger undergoes <Term item="finalization">finalization</Term>, after the <Card name="Reflection" /> token enters: if responses made before the Reflection is played to that battlefield remove every legal copy target from that battlefield, the reflexive trigger leaves the <Term item="chain">chain</Term> without finalizing and the ready Reflection neither becomes a copy nor gains <Temporary />; if, after the Reflection is played to that battlefield and its copy target is chosen, a response removes that target before the reflexive trigger <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term>, the Reflection does not become a copy but still gains <Temporary />.

The interaction follows this sequence:

<Steps>
<Step>
**Deceiver's original trigger finalizes.**
The copy target is not chosen yet because choices for a reflexive trigger generated by an item are not made while that item is finalized.<Rule number="355.5.b" />
Once no <Term item="pending">pending</Term> items remain, opponents may respond before the original trigger resolves.<Rule number="337.4" /><Rule number="406.4" />
</Step>
<Step>
**The original trigger resolves.**
It starts playing a ready Reflection token at the battlefield recorded by "there."<Rule number="359.3.f.3" />
The token waits as a pending chain item while the original trigger finishes resolving.<Rule number="354.2" /><Rule number="354.3" />
The reflexive trigger is then added after the token.<Rule number="388.1" />
</Step>
<Step>
**The Reflection enters, then the reflexive trigger reaches finalization.**
Pending items finalize in the order they were added, so the Reflection finalizes, resolves immediately, and enters before the newer reflexive trigger.<Rule number="337.1.b" /><Rule number="337.2" /><Rule number="359.2.c" />
There is no response window between the Reflection entering and the target choice because every pending item must finalize or leave the chain before players can respond.<Rule number="337.3" /><Rule number="337.4" />
Deceiver's controller must choose another unit at that battlefield as the reflexive trigger's target.<Rule number="355.10" /><Rule number="359.3.f.3.b" /><Rule number="402.2" />
If no other unit there is a legal target, the reflexive trigger is removed without finalizing,<Rule number="402.4" /> so the Reflection remains ready but does not become a copy or gain <Temporary />.
</Step>
<Step>
**If a legal target exists, the reflexive trigger finalizes.**<Rule number="406.2" />
Once no pending items remain, opponents may respond before that trigger resolves.<Rule number="337.4" /><Rule number="406.4" />
If the chosen unit is no longer legal when the trigger resolves,<Rule number="359.3.e.2" /> the copy instruction cannot be followed, so the Reflection does not become a copy.<Rule number="359.3.e.5" /><Rule number="359.3.e.7" />
The reflexive trigger still resolves and gives the Reflection <Temporary />, doing as much of its effect as possible.<Rule number="055" /><Rule number="359.3.e.1" /><Rule number="406.5" />
</Step>
</Steps>
