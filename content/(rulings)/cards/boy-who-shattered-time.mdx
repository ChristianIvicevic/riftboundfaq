## If a unit survives Boy Who Shattered Time's end-of-turn kill, will it die on a later turn? [#end-turn-survival]

Under the official [Radiance ruling](https://playriftbound.com/en-us/news/announcements/core-rules-radiance-patch-notes-effective-october-16-2026/), a unit whose death is replaced, for example by <Card name="Unlicensed Armory" /> or by <Card name="Mordekaiser, Twice Slain" /> replacing his own death, will not try to kill itself again on a later turn.

Boy Who Shattered Time grants the unit "Kill me at end of turn."
The rules treat "at end of turn" and "at the end of this turn" as synonymous, so its trigger condition occurs only during the current turn's Ending Step.<Rule number="359.3.e.16" />
When that ability <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term>, either replacement causes its kill not to happen.<Rule number="370.1.a.1" /><Rule number="370.1.b" />
The triggered ability still finishes resolving.<Rule number="407.5" />
Its specified time has then passed, so it cannot trigger again on a later turn.<Rule number="391.2" /><Rule number="392" />

## Does a unit affected by Boy Who Shattered Time still die if it is banished and played again? [#replayed-unit]

No.
If it is banished and played again before the ability triggers, the returned card is a new unit without the granted kill ability.
Moving from the board to banishment makes the card a new object,<Rule number="124" /> and its temporary modifications, including the granted ability, cease to be tracked.<Rule number="124.1" />

A legally timed effect can also banish and replay it after the kill ability is already on the <Term item="chain">chain</Term>.
The ability remains on the chain, but "me" refers to its original source, not the returned card, which is a new unit.<Rule number="359.3.f.1" /><Rule number="359.3.f.2" /><Rule number="407.2" /><Rule number="407.5" />

<Card name="Ekko, Revolutionary" />'s <Action /> ability and <Card name="Chronobreak" /> can use the first timing window, before the kill ability triggers.
Neither can be used in response once the ability is on the chain because neither has <Reaction />.<Rule number="309.1.a" />
By contrast, <Card name="Temporal Breach" /> can respond to the kill ability if it was played from face down after gaining <Reaction />.<Rule number="811.1.b" />
