## Can Thrill of the Hunt let me conquer a battlefield again on my opponent's turn by banishing and replaying the only unit I own and control there? [#score-on-opponents-turn]

No, if the unit is replayed to the same battlefield, you keep control throughout the sequence and therefore do not conquer it again.
Thrill of the Hunt starts playing the banished unit during its own resolution, so the unit waits on the chain as a pending item.<Rule number="354.2" /><Rule number="354.3" />
The pending unit remains on the chain through the following cleanup.<Rule number="354.4" />
Because that chain item remains, the chain exists and the turn is closed.<Rule number="330" /><Rule number="331.1" />
If the turn were open, that cleanup would make you lose control of the empty battlefield; because the turn is closed, you retain control instead.<Rule number="323.6" />
After outstanding tasks finish, the unit finalizes.<Rule number="334.2" /><Rule number="337.1" />
It immediately resolves and re-enters the battlefield under your control.<Rule number="191.1" /><Rule number="337.2" /><Rule number="359.2.c" />
Because you never lost control, the returning unit does not apply Contested, the status that stages a new showdown.<Rule number="190.3.a.1" /><Rule number="323.8" />
You therefore do not gain control again and cannot Conquer the battlefield through this sequence.<Rule number="469.1" />
