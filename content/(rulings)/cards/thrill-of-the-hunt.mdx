## If I own and control a unit at a battlefield I control, can Thrill of the Hunt make me conquer that battlefield again on my opponent's turn? [#score-on-opponents-turn]

No.
Thrill of the Hunt instructs the banished unit's owner to play it to any battlefield.
Because you own the unit in this scenario, you choose that battlefield.
If you choose the original battlefield, the unit remains on the <Term item="chain">chain</Term> throughout <Term item="cleanup">cleanup</Term> while being played again, so you keep control until it returns.<Rule number="330" /><Rule number="354.4" /><Rule number="309.1" /><Rule number="323.6" />
Because you do not gain control again, you do not conquer the battlefield again.<Rule number="469.1" />

The following sequence applies when you choose the original battlefield.

<Steps>
<Step>
**Unit becomes <Term item="pending">pending</Term>:** Thrill of the Hunt banishes the unit and instructs you, as its owner, to play it before the resulting cleanup runs.<Rule number="319.6" /><Rule number="321.1" />
The unit moves to the chain as a pending item while Thrill of the Hunt finishes <Term item="resolution">resolving</Term>.<Rule number="354.2" /><Rule number="354.3" />
</Step>
<Step>
**Cleanup:** The pending unit keeps the chain in progress while cleanup runs, so cleanup does not remove your battlefield control.
</Step>
<Step>
**Unit enters:** After cleanup, the unit <Term item="finalization">finalizes</Term> and enters the same battlefield.<Rule number="334.2" /><Rule number="337.1" /><Rule number="359.2.c" />
</Step>
</Steps>
