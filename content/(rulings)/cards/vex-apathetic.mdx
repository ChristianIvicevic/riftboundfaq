## Does Vex, Apathetic's triggered ability target the unit that was just played? [#stun-targeting]

No.
Vex's trigger condition identifies the unit that was just played, and the later word "it" refers back to that information.<Rule number="383.2.a" /><Rule number="359.3.f.3" />
Vex's controller never chooses among units, so the unit is not a target.<Rule number="355.10.d" /><Rule number="355.10.d.1" />

An ability that prevents enemy spells or abilities from choosing the unit therefore does not stop Vex from stunning it.<Rule number="757" /><Rule number="758" />
<Deflect /> also adds no cost because Vex's ability does not choose the unit.<Rule number="809.1.d" />
