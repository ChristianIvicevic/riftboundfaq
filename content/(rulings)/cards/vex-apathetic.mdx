## Does Vex, Apathetic's triggered ability target the unit that was just played? [#stun-targeting]

No.
The later word "it" automatically identifies the unit from Vex's trigger condition instead of asking Vex's controller to choose one.<Rule number="355.10.d" /><Rule number="355.10.d.1" />

An ability that prevents enemy spells or abilities from choosing the unit therefore does not stop Vex from stunning it.
<Deflect /> also adds no cost because Vex's ability does not choose the unit.<Rule number="809.1.d" />
