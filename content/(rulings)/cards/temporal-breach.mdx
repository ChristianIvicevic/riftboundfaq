## Can Temporal Breach banish a unit at a different battlefield if played from Hidden? [#hidden-targeting-restriction]

No.
"Banish a unit" targets the mentioned unit,<Rule number="355.10" /> and a spell played from <Hidden /> must choose its targets from the battlefield where it was hidden.<Rule number="811.1.d.2" />
A unit at another battlefield or a base is therefore not a legal target.

If Temporal Breach is played normally, the <Hidden /> restriction does not apply.
It may target an otherwise legal unit at any battlefield or base.<Rule number="355.9.a" /><Rule number="811.3" />

## Does a unit replayed by Temporal Breach keep temporary modifications or statuses? [#replayed-unit-state]

No.
Moving from the board to banishment makes the unit a new game object and clears its temporary modifications and statuses.<Rule number="056.1" /><Rule number="124" /><Rule number="124.1" />
For example, <Card name="Stupefy" />'s might reduction no longer applies, and the replayed unit is no longer <Empowered />.

## What happens to equipment attached to a unit replayed by Temporal Breach? [#attached-equipment]

The equipment detaches at the unit's previous location and does not reattach to the replayed unit.
Moving the unit from the board to banishment detaches its attached cards at that last location.<Rule number="719.5" /><Rule number="435.4.b" />
The replayed unit is a new game object, and Temporal Breach does not instruct the equipment to attach again.<Rule number="124" />

During the next cleanup, detached equipment at a battlefield or a base its controller does not control is recalled to its controller's base.<Rule number="435.4.a" /><Rule number="323.7" />
Equipment already at its controller's base remains there.<Rule number="323.7" />

## Does a replayed unit trigger its attack or defend ability again during the same combat? [#replayed-combat-triggers]

Yes, if the unit is replayed to the battlefield where combat is occurring and its attack or defend condition is otherwise fulfilled.
The banished unit becomes a new game object, so the replayed unit has not had its attack or defend condition checked during that combat.<Rule number="124" /><Rule number="383.4.e.2.a" /><Rule number="383.4.f.2.a" />
During the cleanup after it re-enters, it gains the same attacker or defender designation as its controller.<Rule number="323.2.a" /><Rule number="464.2.c.3.a" />
Gaining that designation checks the corresponding attack or defend trigger.<Rule number="383.4.e" /><Rule number="383.4.f" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
  <Card name="Ahri, Inquisitive" />'s "When I attack or defend" ability triggers when she first gains the defender designation.
  If Temporal Breach then banishes and replays her to that battlefield, the new Ahri object gains the designation during cleanup and can trigger the ability again.
</Callout>

## Does Ravenbloom Conservatory trigger again if Temporal Breach replays my only defending unit? [#ravenbloom-conservatory]

No.
"You" in <Card name="Ravenbloom Conservatory" />'s ability means its controller, so it checks its "When you defend here" condition when that player gains the defender designation as combat opens.<Rule number="190.6.d" /><Rule number="383.4.f" /><Rule number="464.2.c.3" />
That defend condition is checked only once per combat.<Rule number="383.4.f.2.a" />

Temporal Breach makes the replayed unit a new game object, not Ravenbloom Conservatory.<Rule number="124" />
The defending player keeps the defender designation until combat ends, so the unit's return does not cause that player to gain it again.<Rule number="466.7.a" />

## What happens if Temporal Breach banishes a token unit? [#token-unit]

The token ceases to exist in banishment, so its owner cannot replay it.
Tokens can exist only on the board or the chain and cease to exist immediately after entering another non-board zone.<Rule number="056.1" /><Rule number="186" /><Rule number="186.1" />
Temporal Breach then ignores the impossible replay instruction.<Rule number="055" />

## Can I pay optional additional costs, like Accelerate, when replaying a unit banished by Temporal Breach? [#optional-additional-costs]

Yes.
Playing the unit "ignoring its cost" sets only its base energy and power costs to zero.<Rule number="356.1.b.1" />
[Additional costs apply afterward](/general-rules/costs-and-payments#ignored-costs) and can raise the total cost above zero.<Rule number="356.1.b.3" />
Its owner may therefore choose and pay an optional additional cost during finalization.<Rule number="355.1.a" /><Rule number="356.2.b.1" />
For example, <Accelerate /> remains available because it is an optional additional cost paid while playing the unit.<Rule number="805.2" />

## What happens if the replayed unit has a mandatory additional cost its owner can't pay? [#mandatory-additional-cost]

The attempted play is cancelled, and the unit returns to its owner's banishment.
Temporal Breach sets only the unit's base energy and power costs to zero, so mandatory additional costs must still be paid.<Rule number="356.1.b.1" /><Rule number="356.1.b.3" /><Rule number="356.2.a.1" />
If the owner cannot pay one, the play fails its cost check.<Rule number="358.2" />
The play is then cancelled and its actions are undone, returning the unit from the chain to its owner's banishment without undoing Temporal Breach's earlier, completed instruction.<Rule number="056.2" /><Rule number="354" /><Rule number="358.5" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
  If Temporal Breach banishes <Card name="Cruel Patron" /> and its owner has no other friendly unit, they can't pay Cruel Patron's mandatory additional cost to kill a friendly unit.
  Cruel Patron cannot finish finalizing and returns to its owner's banishment.
</Callout>

## If the unit banished by Temporal Breach has Hidden, can its owner hide it instead of playing it? [#hide-instead-of-play]

No.
Hide is a separate action, not a way to play a card.<Rule number="811.1.c.1" />
Playing puts the unit on the chain for finalization, while hiding would put it facedown at a battlefield.<Rule number="419.1" /><Rule number="421.1" />
The unit is also in banishment, but <Hidden /> permits hiding only from hand or the champion zone.<Rule number="811.1.b" />

## If Temporal Breach banishes and replays my only unit at a battlefield, can I score there on my opponent's turn? [#score-on-opponents-turn]

No, because the pending replay keeps the chain in a closed state through cleanup, so you do not lose control of the battlefield.
Losing control of an empty battlefield during cleanup requires the turn to be in an open state.<Rule number="190.4.c" /><Rule number="323.6" />

1. Banishment makes the battlefield empty and incurs a cleanup, but that cleanup waits for Temporal Breach to finish resolving.<Rule number="319.6" /><Rule number="321.1" />
2. The replay instruction moves the unit to the chain as a pending item, where its play process also waits for Temporal Breach to finish.<Rule number="354" /><Rule number="354.2" /><Rule number="354.3" />
3. The pending unit keeps the chain in existence and the turn closed throughout the cleanup, so you retain control.<Rule number="330" /><Rule number="331.1" />
4. After outstanding tasks finish, the unit finalizes and returns to the same battlefield.<Rule number="334.2" /><Rule number="337.1" /><Rule number="359.2.c" />

Because you never lose and regain control, this sequence does not let you conquer the battlefield.<Rule number="469.1" />
Hold scoring occurs only during your Beginning Phase.<Rule number="469.2" />

## What happens if another effect prevents the unit's owner from replaying it? [#prevented-replay]

The unit remains banished.
Temporal Breach still resolves, but it skips the prevented replay as an impossible action.<Rule number="358.3.a" />
The earlier banish instruction remains completed.

### Rockfall Path [#rockfall-path]

The unit remains banished because <Card name="Rockfall Path" /> prevents its owner from playing it back to that location.

### Brynhir Thundersong [#brynhir-thundersong]

If <Card name="Brynhir Thundersong" />'s play effect has resolved and prevents the unit's owner from playing cards, the unit remains banished.
