## When I play Temporal Breach from Hidden, can it banish a unit at a different battlefield? [#hidden-targeting-restriction]

No.
"Banish a unit" targets the mentioned unit,<Rule number="355.10" /> and when you play Temporal Breach from <Hidden />, you must choose its targets from the battlefield where it was hidden.<Rule number="811.1.d.2" />
A unit at another battlefield or a base is therefore not a legal target.

If you play Temporal Breach normally, the <Hidden /> restriction does not apply.
You may target an otherwise legal unit at any battlefield or base.<Rule number="355.9.a" /><Rule number="811.3" />

## Does a unit keep temporary modifications or statuses when Temporal Breach banishes it and its owner plays it again? [#replayed-unit-state]

No.
Moving from the board to banishment makes the unit a new game object and clears its temporary modifications and statuses.<Rule number="056.1" /><Rule number="124" /><Rule number="124.1" />
For example, <Card name="Stupefy" />'s might reduction no longer applies, and the unit is no longer <Empowered />.<Rule number="124.2" />

## What happens to equipment attached to a unit banished by Temporal Breach? [#attached-equipment]

The equipment detaches at the unit's previous location and does not reattach when the unit is played again.
Moving the unit from the board to banishment detaches its attached cards at that last location.<Rule number="719.5" /><Rule number="435.4.b" />
The unit entering the board is a new game object, and Temporal Breach does not instruct the equipment to attach again.<Rule number="124" />

Detached equipment at a battlefield is recalled to its controller's base during the next cleanup.<Rule number="435.4.a" />
Detached equipment at a base its controller does not control is recalled to its controller's base during cleanup.<Rule number="323.7" />
Equipment already at its controller's base remains there.<Rule number="323.7" />

## Can Temporal Breach cause a unit's attack or defend ability to trigger again during the same combat? [#replayed-combat-triggers]

Yes, if the unit enters the battlefield where combat is occurring and its attack or defend condition is otherwise fulfilled.<Rule number="383.4.e.2.b" /><Rule number="383.4.f.2.b" />
The unit entering the battlefield is a new game object, so its attack or defend condition has not been checked during that combat.<Rule number="124" /><Rule number="383.4.e.2.a" /><Rule number="383.4.f.2.a" />
During the cleanup after it re-enters, it gains the same attacker or defender designation as its controller.<Rule number="464.2.c.3.a" />
Gaining that designation checks the corresponding attack or defend trigger.<Rule number="383.4.e" /><Rule number="383.4.f" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
  <Card name="Ahri, Inquisitive" />'s "When I attack or defend" ability triggers when she first gains the defender designation.
  If Temporal Breach then banishes her and her owner plays her again at that battlefield, the new Ahri object gains the designation during cleanup and can trigger the ability again.
</Callout>

## Does Ravenbloom Conservatory trigger again if Temporal Breach banishes my only defending unit and I play it again? [#ravenbloom-conservatory]

No.
"You" in <Card name="Ravenbloom Conservatory" />'s ability means its controller, so if you control Ravenbloom Conservatory, it checks its "When you defend here" condition when you gain the defender designation as combat opens.<Rule number="190.6.d" /><Rule number="383.4.f" /><Rule number="464.2.c.3" />
That defend condition is checked only once per combat.<Rule number="383.4.f.2.a" />

The unit becomes a new game object, but Ravenbloom Conservatory does not.<Rule number="124" />
As the defending player, you keep the defender designation until combat ends, so the unit's return does not cause you to gain it again.<Rule number="466.7.a" />

## What happens if Temporal Breach banishes a token unit? [#token-unit]

The token ceases to exist in banishment, so its owner cannot play it again.
Tokens can exist only on the board or the chain and cease to exist immediately after entering another non-board zone.<Rule number="056.1" /><Rule number="186" /><Rule number="186.1" />

## Can I pay optional additional costs, like Accelerate, when playing a unit banished by Temporal Breach? [#optional-additional-costs]

Yes.
Playing the unit "ignoring its cost" sets only its base energy and power costs to zero.<Rule number="356.1.b.1" />
Additional costs apply afterward and can raise the total cost above zero.<Rule number="356.1.b.3" />
As the unit's owner, you may therefore choose and pay an optional additional cost during finalization.<Rule number="355.1.a" /><Rule number="356.2.b.1" />
For example, <Accelerate /> remains available because it is an optional additional cost paid while playing the unit.<Rule number="805.2" />

## What happens if a unit's owner can't pay a mandatory additional cost when Temporal Breach instructs them to play it? [#mandatory-additional-cost]

The attempted play is cancelled, and the unit returns to its owner's banishment.
Temporal Breach sets only the unit's base energy and power costs to zero, so mandatory additional costs must still be paid.<Rule number="356.1.b.1" /><Rule number="356.2.a.1" />
If the owner cannot pay one, the play fails its cost check.<Rule number="358.2" />
The play is then cancelled and its actions are undone, returning the unit from the chain to its owner's banishment without undoing Temporal Breach's earlier, completed instruction.<Rule number="354" /><Rule number="358.5" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
  If Temporal Breach banishes <Card name="Cruel Patron" /> and its owner has no other friendly unit, they can't pay Cruel Patron's mandatory additional cost to kill a friendly unit.
  Cruel Patron cannot finish finalizing and returns to its owner's banishment.
</Callout>

## If the unit banished by Temporal Breach has Hidden, can its owner hide it instead of playing it? [#hide-instead-of-play]

No.
Hide is a separate action, not a way to play a card.<Rule number="811.1.c.1" />
Playing puts the unit on the chain for finalization, while hiding would put it facedown at a battlefield.<Rule number="419.1" /><Rule number="421.1" />
The unit is also in banishment, but <Hidden /> permits hiding only from hand or the champion zone.<Rule number="811.1.b" />

## Can Temporal Breach make me conquer the same battlefield again on my opponent's turn? [#score-on-opponents-turn]

No.
You retain control of the battlefield while Temporal Breach banishes your only unit there and that unit is played again, so you do not conquer it again.<Rule number="469.1" />
The unit remains pending on the chain through cleanup, so you do not lose and regain control of the battlefield.<Rule number="323.6" /><Rule number="330" /><Rule number="334.2" />
Cleanup can remove your control of an empty battlefield only when no chain exists.<Rule number="190.4.c" /><Rule number="309.1" />

## Can Rockfall Path or Brynhir Thundersong stop the unit from being played after Temporal Breach banishes it? [#prevented-replay]

The unit remains banished if either effect prevents its owner from playing it.
Temporal Breach still resolves, but the applicable effect prevents the instructed play.<Rule number="358.3.a" />
The earlier banish instruction remains completed.
The unit remains banished because <Card name="Rockfall Path" /> prevents its owner from playing it back to that location.
If <Card name="Brynhir Thundersong" />'s play effect has resolved and prevents the unit's owner from playing cards, the unit remains banished.
