## When I play Consuming Curse from my trash, does it count itself for the bonus damage? [#self-count]

No.
The first step of playing Consuming Curse moves it from your trash to the chain.<Rule number="354" />
Its damage instruction executes before it returns to your trash.<Rule number="359.3.d" />
Only other cards named Consuming Curse that remain in your trash add bonus damage.
