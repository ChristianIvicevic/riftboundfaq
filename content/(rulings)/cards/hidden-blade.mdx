## If Hidden Blade's kill is prevented or replaced, does the target's controller draw? [#draw-if-not-killed]

If the targeted unit remains on the board, its controller draws 2; if a replacement moves it off the board, no player draws.
The phrase "its controller" links the draw to the unit affected by the preceding kill instruction.<Rule number="359.3.e.14" />
If the legal target can't be killed, the [Vendetta FAQ](https://playriftbound.com/en-us/news/rules-and-releases/vendetta-rules-faq-and-clarifications) classifies the kill instruction as negated rather than ignored, so the linked draw still executes.
Replacing that kill does not itself stop the linked draw because the draw instruction does not refer to the kill action.<Rule number="359.3.e.14.b" />

<Callout type="warn" title="Rules citation needed">
	Core Rules 1.4 still calls instructions prevented by other circumstances "ignored."<Rule number="359.3.e.6" />
	The linked Vendetta FAQ narrows that term to mistargeting and expressly takes precedence on this point until a later Core Rules Document says otherwise.
</Callout>

- **The unit remains on the board:** Hidden Blade can identify its controller, and that player draws 2.<Rule number="359.3.e.12.a" />
  For example, <Card name="Zhonya's Hourglass" /> recalls the unit to base, where it remains on the board under its controller.
- **The unit moves off the board:** Hidden Blade can no longer identify a controller for that card, so no player draws.<Rule number="359.3.e.12" />
  For example, a unit previously affected by <Card name="Smite" /> is banished instead when Hidden Blade would kill it.

## If Hidden Blade's target becomes illegal, does that target's controller draw? [#draw-if-illegal]

No.
An illegal target is unaffected by Hidden Blade, so the kill instruction is ignored.<Rule number="359.3.e.5" />
Hidden Blade's draw is linked to its kill instruction because "its controller" refers to the unit affected by that instruction.<Rule number="359.3.e.14" />
When an earlier linked instruction is ignored, its later linked instructions are also ignored, so the controller does not draw.<Rule number="359.3.e.14.a" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	You play Hidden Blade targeting an opponent's unit at a battlefield.
	They respond by moving it to their base using <Card name="Flash" />.
	Hidden Blade resolves.
	The unit is no longer at a battlefield, so it is an illegal target.
	The unit is not killed and the opponent does not draw.
</Callout>
