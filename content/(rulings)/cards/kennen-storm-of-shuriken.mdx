## Can Kennen grant Flow to a spell put into the trash by Minefield? [#minefield-flow]

No.
Kennen's conquer ability must choose a spell already in your trash when the ability is finalized on the chain.<Rule number="355.5" /><Rule number="355.8" />

Kennen's ability and <Card name="Minefield" />'s ability trigger from the same conquer and are added to the chain as pending items.<Rule number="383.3.d" />
Regardless of their order, every pending item must be finalized before any item can begin resolving.<Rule number="337.3" /><Rule number="337.4" />
Minefield therefore cannot put its two cards into your trash until after Kennen has chosen a spell, and that choice cannot later be changed.<Rule number="355.15" />
