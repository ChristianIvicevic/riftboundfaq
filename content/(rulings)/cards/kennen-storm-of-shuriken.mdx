## Can Kennen grant Flow to a spell put into the trash by Minefield's ability triggered by the same conquer? [#minefield-flow]

No.
You must choose a spell already in your trash for Kennen's conquer ability when the ability is finalized on the chain.<Rule number="355.5" /><Rule number="355.8" />

Kennen's ability and <Card name="Minefield" />'s ability trigger simultaneously from the same conquer and are added to the chain as pending items.<Rule number="383.3.d" />
Their order does not change the result because every pending item must be finalized before either ability can resolve.<Rule number="337.1" /><Rule number="337.3" /><Rule number="337.4" />
Minefield therefore cannot put its two cards into your trash until after you have chosen a spell for Kennen's ability, and you cannot later change that choice.<Rule number="355.15" />
