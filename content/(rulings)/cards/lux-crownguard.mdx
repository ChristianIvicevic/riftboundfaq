## Can I use Lux, Crownguard's ability to pay for Hard Bargain? [#hard-bargain-payment]

No.
You can use Lux's ability to add energy to pay the costs of playing a spell, but <Card name="Hard Bargain" />'s payment is not a spell play cost.

Paying a spell's costs is part of <Term item="finalization">finalizing</Term> that spell.<Rule number="357.1" />
During that payment, its controller can activate a <Reaction /> ability that adds resources.<Rule number="357.1.a" />

Once the spell finishes finalizing, opponents receive the opportunity to play Reactions such as Hard Bargain.<Rule number="359.3.a" /><Rule number="359.3.c" />
Hard Bargain's instruction, "Counter a spell unless its controller pays <Energy value={2} />," is executed later, when it <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term>.<Rule number="158.1" />
The payment is therefore not part of playing the targeted spell.
You may still activate Lux's ability,<Rule number="429.3" /> but the energy it adds can be spent only to play spells and cannot be spent on Hard Bargain's payment.
