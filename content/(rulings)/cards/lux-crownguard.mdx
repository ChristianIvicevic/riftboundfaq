## Can I use Lux, Crownguard's ability to pay for Hard Bargain? [#hard-bargain-payment]

No.
Lux's ability can add energy for the costs of playing a spell, but <Card name="Hard Bargain" />'s payment is not a spell play cost.

Paying a spell's costs is part of finalizing that spell.<Rule number="357.1" />
During that payment, its controller can activate a <Reaction /> ability that adds resources.<Rule number="357.1.a" /><Rule number="429.3" />

Once the spell finishes finalizing, opponents receive the opportunity to play Reactions such as <Card name="Hard Bargain" />.<Rule number="359.3.a" /><Rule number="359.3.c" />
Hard Bargain's instruction, "Counter a spell unless its controller pays <Energy value={2} />," is executed later, when it resolves.<Rule number="158.1" />
The payment is therefore not part of playing the targeted spell.
You may still activate Lux's ability, but the energy it adds can be spent only to play spells and cannot be spent on Hard Bargain's payment.
