## Can I channel with Ruined Monastery if I have no cards in hand? [#empty-hand-channel]

Yes, you still channel 1 rune exhausted with Ruined Monastery if you have no cards in hand.
Its "discard 1 and channel 1 rune exhausted" wording does not use the "[do X] to [do Y]" form that creates a cost within instructions, so discarding is part of the effect rather than a cost.<Rule number="740.4.a" />
The impossible discard instruction is therefore negated, and the channel instruction still happens.<Rule number="055" /><Rule number="423.4" />

If it instead said "discard 1 to channel 1 rune exhausted," discarding would be a cost.
With an empty hand, that cost would be impossible to pay, so you would not channel.<Rule number="203.3" />
