## Can I use Arcane Shift to banish the only unit I own and control at a battlefield and replay it there? [#replay-at-same-battlefield]

Yes, because you still control the battlefield when the pending unit finalizes and you choose where it will enter.
Arcane Shift starts playing the banished unit during its own resolution, so the unit waits on the chain as a pending item.<Rule number="354.2" /><Rule number="354.3" />
The pending unit remains on the chain through the following cleanup.<Rule number="354.4" />
Because that chain item remains, the chain exists and the turn is closed.<Rule number="330" /><Rule number="331.1" />
If the turn were open, that cleanup would make you lose control of the empty battlefield; because the turn is closed, you retain control instead.<Rule number="323.6" />
It remains a valid location for the unit you play and control.<Rule number="191.1" /><Rule number="355.2.a" />

See [Outside a showdown, do I lose control of a battlefield as soon as my last unit leaves it?](/general-rules/chain-and-priority#empty-battlefield-control) for the general control rule.

### Resolution Sequence [#replay-sequence]

When Arcane Shift resolves, the following happens:

1. Arcane Shift banishes the friendly unit, leaving the battlefield empty and incurring a cleanup.<Rule number="319.6" />
2. Its replay instruction moves the unit to the chain as a pending item.<Rule number="354.2" />
   The play process waits while Arcane Shift continues resolving.<Rule number="354.3" />
3. Arcane Shift deals 3 to an enemy unit, banishes itself, and finishes resolving.
4. The board-change cleanup remains outstanding until Arcane Shift finishes resolving.<Rule number="321.1" />
   The pending unit remains on the chain while that cleanup runs.<Rule number="354.4" />
5. The remaining chain item keeps the turn closed.<Rule number="331.1" />
   You do not lose control of the empty battlefield during that cleanup.<Rule number="323.6" />
6. After outstanding tasks finish, the pending unit continues finalizing.<Rule number="334.2" /><Rule number="337.1" />
   You choose the still-controlled battlefield as the location for the unit you play and control.<Rule number="191.1" /><Rule number="355.2.a" />
7. The unit immediately resolves.<Rule number="337.2" />
   It enters that battlefield exhausted.<Rule number="359.2.c" />
