## Can I use Arcane Shift to banish the only unit I own and control at a battlefield I control, then play it there again? [#replay-at-same-battlefield]

Yes, as long as the unit is not a token and you control that battlefield.
For a non-token unit, by the time battlefield control is checked, the unit's owner has started playing it, leaving the unit <Term item="pending">pending</Term> on the <Term item="chain">chain</Term> while Arcane Shift finishes <Term item="resolution">resolving</Term>.<Rule number="321.1" /><Rule number="354.2" /><Rule number="354.3" />
That pending unit keeps the chain active, so you keep control of the battlefield and may choose it for the unit's return.<Rule number="323.6" /><Rule number="330" /><Rule number="331.1" /><Rule number="355.2.a" />

A token unit instead ceases to exist immediately after moving to banishment, before Arcane Shift can instruct its owner to play it again.<Rule number="186.1" />

### Resolution Sequence [#replay-sequence]

<Steps>
<Step>
Arcane Shift banishes the unit, causing a <Term item="cleanup">cleanup</Term>.<Rule number="319.6" />
Arcane Shift then instructs the unit's owner to play it, moving the unit to the chain as a pending item before the spell finishes resolving.<Rule number="354.2" /><Rule number="354.3" /><Rule number="419.3" />
</Step>
<Step>
Cleanup runs while the unit remains pending, so the active chain prevents you from losing control of the empty battlefield.<Rule number="321.1" /><Rule number="323.6" /><Rule number="330" /><Rule number="331.1" /><Rule number="354.4" />
</Step>
<Step>
During <Term item="finalization">finalization</Term>, you choose the still-controlled battlefield as the unit's location.<Rule number="355.2.a" />
The unit then enters there.<Rule number="359.2.c" />
</Step>
</Steps>
