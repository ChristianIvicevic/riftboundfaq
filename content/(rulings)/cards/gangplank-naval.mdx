## If Gangplank, Naval replaces one part of a spell or ability, does the rest still resolve? [#partial-resolution]

Yes, only the qualifying stun, might decrease, or return-to-hand event is replaced; the spell or ability's other instructions continue unless a later linked instruction specifically requires the replaced action to have occurred.<Rule number="359.3.e.14.b" />
A replacement effect only replaces the specific event or instruction that qualifies for it.<Rule number="370.1" /><Rule number="370.1.b" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	You play <Card name="Star-Crossed" />, targeting your own <Empowered /> Gangplank as the friendly unit and an opponent's <Card name="Elder Dragon" /> as the enemy unit.

	The return event affecting Elder Dragon would not return Gangplank, so his replacement effect does not apply to it; Elder Dragon is returned to hand normally.
	Returning Gangplank is the qualifying event: instead of going to hand, he gets +3 might.
	The rest of Star-Crossed resolves normally; it simply produces a different result for the one return event involving Gangplank.
</Callout>

## What happens when Switcheroo would reduce an Empowered Gangplank, Naval's might? [#switcheroo-might-reduction]

Under the August 2026 [Vendetta FAQ](https://playriftbound.com/en-us/news/rules-and-releases/vendetta-rules-faq-and-clarifications), the might reduction is replaced with +3 might, while the modifier applied to the other unit is unchanged.
<Card name="Switcheroo" /> chooses Gangplank when the spell is finalized.<Rule number="355.5" /><Rule number="355.10" />
When it resolves, Switcheroo determines one difference and creates a separate increase and decrease.<Rule number="433.1.a" /><Rule number="433.1.b" />
If the modifier affecting Gangplank would decrease his might, that event is replaced with +3 might because it comes from a spell that chose him.
If it would increase his might, it does not match the events his ability replaces and applies normally.<Rule number="370.1" /><Rule number="370.1.b" />
The modifier affecting the other unit is unchanged.
Under that ruling, Switcheroo does not recalculate either fixed amount after Gangplank replaces his decrease.

<Callout type="warn" title="Rules citation needed">
	Core Rules 1.4 does not yet make this no-recalculation result explicit, and its swap rules need clarification.
</Callout>

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	An <Empowered /> Gangplank with 6 current might is swapped with a <Card name="Determined Sentry" /> with 1 current might at the same battlefield via Switcheroo.

	The swap action calculates a difference of 5, then creates two modifiers: -5 might for Gangplank and +5 might for Determined Sentry.
	Gangplank's replacement effect intercedes on his -5 might modifier because decreasing his might is one of the events it replaces, and turns it into +3 might instead.
	The +5 might modifier applies only to Determined Sentry, so Gangplank's replacement effect does not affect it.

	Gangplank ends at 9 might (6 current might + 3 might); Determined Sentry ends at 6 might (1 current might + 5 might).
</Callout>
