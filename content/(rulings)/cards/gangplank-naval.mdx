## If Gangplank, Naval replaces one part of a spell or ability, does the rest still resolve? [#partial-resolution]

Yes, only the qualifying stun, might decrease, or return-to-hand event is replaced; the spell or ability's other instructions continue unless a later linked instruction specifically requires the replaced action to have occurred.<Rule number="359.3.e.14.b" />
A replacement effect only replaces the specific event or instruction that qualifies for it.<Rule number="370.1" /><Rule number="370.1.b" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	You play <Card name="Star-Crossed" />, targeting your own <Empowered /> Gangplank as the friendly unit and an opponent's <Card name="Elder Dragon" /> as the enemy unit.

	Gangplank's replacement effect does not apply to the separate return event affecting Elder Dragon, so Elder Dragon returns to its owner's hand normally.
	Returning Gangplank is the qualifying event: instead of going to hand, he gets +3 might this turn.
	The rest of Star-Crossed <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term> normally; it simply produces a different result for the one return event involving Gangplank.
</Callout>

## What happens when Switcheroo would reduce an Empowered Gangplank, Naval's might? [#switcheroo-might-reduction]

When Switcheroo would decrease an <Empowered /> Gangplank's might, Gangplank gets +3 might this turn instead, while the increase affecting the other unit remains unchanged.
<Card name="Switcheroo" /> chooses Gangplank when the spell is <Term item="finalization">finalized</Term>.<Rule number="355.5" /><Rule number="355.10" />
When it <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term>, Switcheroo first determines one difference, then creates separate might-increase and might-decrease effects for that fixed amount; replacing one does not recalculate or alter the other.<Rule number="434.1.a" /><Rule number="434.1.b" /><Rule number="434.1.d" />
Gangplank's replacement effect replaces the decrease affecting him because it comes from a spell that chose him, but an increase does not match any event his ability replaces and applies normally.<Rule number="370.1" /><Rule number="370.1.b" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	An <Empowered /> Gangplank with 6 current might is swapped with a <Card name="Determined Sentry" /> with 1 current might at the same battlefield via Switcheroo.

	The swap action calculates a difference of 5, then creates two effects: -5 might for Gangplank and +5 might for Determined Sentry.
	Gangplank's replacement effect replaces the -5 might decrease affecting him with +3 might this turn.
	The +5 might effect applies only to Determined Sentry, so Gangplank's replacement effect does not affect it.

	After Switcheroo resolves, Gangplank has 9 might (6 current might + 3 might), and Determined Sentry has 6 might (1 current might + 5 might).
</Callout>
