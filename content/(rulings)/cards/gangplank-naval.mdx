## If Gangplank, Naval replaces one part of a spell or ability, does the rest still resolve? [#partial-resolution]

Yes, only the qualifying stun, might decrease, or return-to-hand instruction is replaced; the spell or ability's other instructions continue resolving.
A replacement effect only replaces the specific event or instruction that qualifies for it.<Rule number="370.1" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	A player plays <Card name="Star-Crossed" />, targeting their own <Empowered /> Gangplank as the friendly unit and an opponent's <Card name="Elder Dragon" /> as the enemy unit.

	Returning Elder Dragon doesn't choose Gangplank, so his replacement effect has nothing to apply to there; Elder Dragon is returned to hand as normal.
	Returning Gangplank is the qualifying event: instead of going to hand, he gets +3 might.
	The rest of Star-Crossed resolves normally; it simply produces a different result for the one instruction that involves Gangplank.
</Callout>

## What happens when Switcheroo would reduce an Empowered Gangplank, Naval's might? [#switcheroo-might-reduction]

The might reduction is replaced with +3 might, while the modifier applied to the other unit is unchanged.
<Card name="Switcheroo" /> chooses Gangplank when the spell is finalized.<Rule number="355.5" /><Rule number="355.10" />
When it resolves, Switcheroo determines one difference and creates a separate increase and decrease.<Rule number="433.1.a" /><Rule number="433.1.b" />
If the modifier affecting Gangplank would decrease his might, that event is replaced with +3 might because it comes from a spell that chose him.
If it would increase his might, it does not match the events his ability replaces and applies normally.
The modifier affecting the other unit is unchanged.
Switcheroo does not recalculate either fixed amount after Gangplank replaces his decrease because the effects already have their amounts.<Rule number="433.1.a" /><Rule number="433.1.b" />
See [How does Switcheroo work?](/cards/switcheroo#might-swap) and [What happens when later modifiers change?](/cards/switcheroo#later-modifier-changes) for the shared Swap treatment.

Gangplank must already be <Empowered /> for his replacement effect to apply.
By default, his <Empower /> ability cannot be activated in response to Switcheroo or another spell;<Rule number="381" /><Rule number="309.1.a" /> see [When can I activate an Empower ability?](/mechanics/empower#empower-timing).

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	An <Empowered /> Gangplank at 6 might is swapped with a 1 might <Card name="Determined Sentry" /> at the same battlefield via <Card name="Switcheroo" />.

	The swap action calculates a difference of 5, then creates two modifiers: -5 might for Gangplank and +5 might for Determined Sentry.
	Gangplank's replacement effect intercedes on his -5 might modifier because decreasing his might is one of the events it replaces, and turns it into +3 might instead.
	The +5 might modifier applies only to Determined Sentry, so Gangplank's replacement effect does not affect it.

	Gangplank ends at 9 might (6 base + 3); Determined Sentry ends at 6 might (1 base + 5).
</Callout>
