## Does Gravity Field's cost depend on who performs the move or who controls the moved unit? [#move-responsibility]

The player performing the move must pay <Energy value={1} /><Universal /> if they are an opponent of Gravity Field's controller, regardless of who controls the moved unit.

The player who performs a game action is responsible for it.<Rule number="412.1" />
<Card name="Charm" /> and <Card name="Siren's Song" /> each instruct you to move an enemy unit, so you perform the move even though your opponent controls that unit.
If an opponent controls Gravity Field at the destination, you must pay the cost; if you control it there, neither player pays.<Rule number="204.4" /><Rule number="204.4.a" />
By contrast, <Card name="Call to Battle" /> instructs the chosen opponent to move one of their units, so that opponent must pay the cost if you control Gravity Field at the destination.

## Must an opponent pay Gravity Field's cost when an effect tells them to move a unit to its battlefield, or can they decline? [#opponent-move-cost]

They must pay <Energy value={1} /><Universal /> to perform the move, but they may decline.
If they decline or cannot pay, they move no unit.

For example, <Card name="Angler Wyrm" /> and <Card name="Call to Battle" /> each instruct a chosen opponent to perform the move, so that opponent is responsible for it.<Rule number="412.1" />
Gravity Field therefore applies its cost to that move action.<Rule number="204.4" /><Rule number="204.4.a" />
Applied costs are paid as their associated game actions are performed.<Rule number="204.4.b" />

The word "must" does not remove the option to decline an applied cost.
If the opponent declines or cannot pay, they cannot perform the move action.<Rule number="204.4.c" />
The move instruction is negated rather than ignored because it cannot be followed for a reason other than an illegal target or unavailable information.<Rule number="359.3.e.6" /><Rule number="407.5" />
