## When I play Shadowblade Lurker from my trash, does it discount its own cost? [#self-count]

No.
The first step of playing Shadowblade Lurker moves it from your trash to the chain.<Rule number="354" />
Its total cost is determined afterward, so only other cards named Shadowblade Lurker that remain in your trash reduce its cost.<Rule number="356" />
