## When I play Shadowblade Lurker from my trash, does it count itself for its cost reduction? [#self-count]

No.
The first step of playing Shadowblade Lurker moves it from your trash to the <Term item="chain">chain</Term>, so that copy is no longer in your trash.<Rule number="354" />
The number of matching cards is checked afterward when its discount is applied, so only other cards named Shadowblade Lurker in your trash at that time count toward the reduction.<Rule number="356.4.g" />
