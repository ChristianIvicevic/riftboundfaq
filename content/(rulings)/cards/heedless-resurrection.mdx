## If Heedless Resurrection is countered, is the killed unit returned? [#countered-additional-cost]

No.
Killing a friendly unit is an additional cost paid while playing Heedless Resurrection.<Rule number="356.7" /><Rule number="357.2" />
Countering the spell later does not refund that cost, so the killed unit is not returned.<Rule number="426.1.c" /><Rule number="426.1.c.1" />

## Can I kill a unit with Heedless Resurrection's cost and then resurrect that same unit? [#self-resurrection]

No.
During the process of playing Heedless Resurrection, you choose a unit card already in your trash during step 2, while the friendly unit you intend to kill is still on the board.<Rule number="355.5" /><Rule number="355.8" /><Rule number="355.9.a" />
You pay the kill cost during step 4 of that process, after the target choice has been locked in.<Rule number="357.2" /><Rule number="355.15" />

## Does Heedless Resurrection's "ignoring its cost" instruction also ignore the resurrected unit's additional costs, such as Accelerate? [#ignore-additional-costs]

No.
"Ignoring its cost" sets only the resurrected unit's base energy and power costs to zero; additional costs, such as <Accelerate />, are applied afterward and are not waived.<Rule number="356.1.b.1" /><Rule number="356.1.b.3" /><Rule number="805.2" />
If the resurrected unit has an optional additional cost you may choose whether to pay it;<Rule number="356.2.b.1" /> if it has a mandatory additional cost you must pay it.<Rule number="356.2.a.1" />

## If Heedless Resurrection gains Repeat, do I need to kill a friendly unit for each repetition? [#repeat-cost]

No.
Heedless Resurrection is played only once, so you pay its additional cost to kill a friendly unit only once.<Rule number="357.2" /><Rule number="820.3.a" />
<Repeat /> executes only the spell's <Term item="resolution">resolution</Term> instructions again; instructions not performed on resolution, such as the additional-cost instruction, are not repeated.<Rule number="820.1.d.1" /><Rule number="820.1.d.2" />
