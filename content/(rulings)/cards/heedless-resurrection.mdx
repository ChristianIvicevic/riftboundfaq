## If Heedless Resurrection is countered, is the killed unit returned? [#countered-additional-cost]

No.
Killing a friendly unit is an additional cost paid while playing Heedless Resurrection.<Rule number="356.7" /><Rule number="357.2" />
Countering the spell later does not refund that cost, so the killed unit is not returned.<Rule number="425.1.c" /><Rule number="425.1.c.1" />

## Can I kill a unit with Heedless Resurrection's cost and then resurrect that same unit? [#self-resurrection]

No.
The resurrection target must already be a valid unit in a trash when you choose it during step 2 of finalization.<Rule number="355.5" /><Rule number="355.8" /><Rule number="355.9.a" />

At that point, the unit you intend to kill is still on the board because the additional cost is not paid until step 4.<Rule number="357.2" />
The target choice is already locked in before that payment, so you cannot switch to the killed unit afterward.<Rule number="355.15" />

## Does Heedless Resurrection's "ignoring its cost" instruction also ignore the resurrected unit's additional costs, such as Accelerate? [#ignore-additional-costs]

No.
"Ignoring its cost" only sets the resurrected unit's base energy and power costs to zero; additional costs are not waived.

When Heedless Resurrection instructs you to play a unit from the trash "ignoring its cost," that instruction sets the resurrected unit's base energy cost and base power cost to zero.<Rule number="356.1.b.1" />
Additional costs, such as the optional <Accelerate /> cost, are applied in a separate step after the base cost is zeroed out and are unaffected by the instruction.<Rule number="356.1.b.3" /><Rule number="805.2" />
If the resurrected unit has an optional additional cost you may choose whether to pay it;<Rule number="356.2.b.1" /> if it has a mandatory additional cost you must pay it.<Rule number="356.2.a.1" />

## If Heedless Resurrection gains Repeat, do I need to kill a friendly unit for each repetition? [#repeat-cost]

No.
The additional cost to kill a friendly unit is paid once while playing Heedless Resurrection.<Rule number="357.2" />
<Repeat /> executes the spell's effect instructions additional times during resolution, but the spell is still played only once.<Rule number="820.1.d.1" /><Rule number="820.3.a" />
It therefore does not repeat the earlier kill cost.
