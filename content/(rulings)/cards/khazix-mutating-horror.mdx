## Can an opponent play a unit in reaction to Kha'Zix, Mutating Horror's trigger to prevent the effect? [#unit-play-in-response]

No.
Even if an opponent can legally play a unit in reaction, that unit's arrival does not undo Kha'Zix's fulfilled trigger condition.

Kha'Zix's ability reads "When I attack or defend, if an enemy unit is alone here, give me +2 Might this turn and gain 2 XP."
The "if" clause immediately follows the initial trigger clause, so it is part of the trigger condition.<Rule number="383.2.a.1" />
The condition is evaluated once, when Kha'Zix gains the attacker or defender designation.<Rule number="383.4.e.2.a" /><Rule number="383.4.e.2.b" /><Rule number="383.4.f.2.a" /><Rule number="383.4.f.2.b" />
If the enemy unit is alone then, the ability is placed on the chain.<Rule number="383.3" />
Because the "if" clause is part of the condition rather than the effect, the effect does not check it again during resolution.<Rule number="383.2.b" /><Rule number="406.5" />
If the enemy unit is not alone then, the ability does not trigger even if another enemy unit is later removed.

Playing a legally timed unit places it on the existing chain as a pending item.<Rule number="330.2" /><Rule number="419.1" />
After it finalizes, the unit resolves immediately and enters the board.<Rule number="337.2" /><Rule number="359.2.c" />
This later entry does not repeat Kha'Zix's earlier trigger-condition check.

## Can Kha'Zix, Mutating Horror trigger multiple times in the same turn? [#trigger-per-combat]

Yes.
In each combat, Kha'Zix can trigger when he first attacks and when he first defends, if an enemy unit is alone at his battlefield at each check.
Attack and defend trigger conditions are checked separately when a unit first gains the matching designation during a combat.<Rule number="383.4.e" /><Rule number="383.4.f" />
Each matching check happens only once per combat, rather than once per turn.<Rule number="383.4.e.2.a" /><Rule number="383.4.f.2.a" />

See [When do attack and defend triggers happen?](/general-rules/showdowns#attack-defend-triggers) for designation timing and late arrivals during combat.
