## When do I choose whether to pay the power for Rebuttal? [#power-payment-timing]

You choose the affected spell during Rebuttal's finalization, before opponents can react<Rule number="355.5" /><Rule number="359.3.c" />, but you do not decide whether to pay <Universal /> until Rebuttal resolves.
The optional payment is not a cost paid to play Rebuttal; it is a game action Rebuttal instructs you to perform during resolution.<Rule number="135.2.b.3" /><Rule number="205" />
Opponents therefore know which spell you chose, but not whether you will pay to gain control of it or decline and have Rebuttal attempt to counter it.

## What choices can I remake when Rebuttal takes control of a spell? [#new-choices]

Under the August 2026 [Vendetta FAQ](https://playriftbound.com/en-us/news/rules-and-releases/vendetta-rules-faq-and-clarifications), you may either leave every target and mode unchanged or remake any subset.
If you change nothing, existing choices may remain illegal.
If you remake at least one choice, every resulting choice must be legal, including choices you left unchanged.
Targets and modes are among the choices normally made during finalization.<Rule number="752" /><Rule number="752.1" />
The Core Rules permit remaking any subset and prohibit illegal new choices, but they do not expressly address the unchanged choices.<Rule number="753" /><Rule number="753.1" />

<Callout type="warn" title="Rules citation needed">
	Core Rules 1.4 does not independently supply the distinction between remaking at least one choice and leaving every choice unchanged.
	The cited FAQ notes that this Core Rules section will be revised in a future update.
</Callout>

## Can Rebuttal redirect a spell played from Hidden to another location? [#hidden-target-restriction]

No.
Taking control of the spell and making new choices for it does not remove the targeting restriction imposed by <Hidden />.

Rebuttal allows you to remake a finalized spell's targets,<Rule number="752.1" /> but every new target must still be legal.<Rule number="753.1" />
When a spell is played from <Hidden />, its targets must be chosen from among the options at the battlefield where it was hidden unless the spell's own targeting restriction makes that impossible.<Rule number="811.1.d.2" />
Changing the spell's controller does not change where it was played from, so this restriction continues to apply.

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	An opponent plays <Card name="Hidden Blade" /> from <Hidden /> and chooses a unit at that battlefield.
	If you gain control of Hidden Blade with Rebuttal, you may choose another legal unit at the same battlefield.
	You cannot choose a unit at another battlefield, and you cannot choose a unit at base because Hidden Blade itself requires a unit "at a battlefield."
</Callout>

## Whose "when you play a spell" abilities trigger for a spell Rebuttal takes control of? [#play-trigger-controller]

Under the August 2026 [Vendetta FAQ](https://playriftbound.com/en-us/news/rules-and-releases/vendetta-rules-faq-and-clarifications), if the spell resolves after Rebuttal takes control of it, the spell's new controller's applicable "when you play a spell" abilities trigger; the original player's abilities do not.
These abilities trigger once the spell has finished resolving.<Rule number="419.4.a" />
Rebuttal has made the player who gained control of the spell its controller by then.

<Callout type="warn" title="Rules citation needed">
	Core Rules 1.4 establishes when "when you play" abilities trigger, but it does not independently establish responsibility for completing a spell's play after its control changes.
	The outcome above follows the official FAQ ruling.
</Callout>

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	Suppose you and your opponent each control <Card name="Ravenbloom Student" />, and you use Rebuttal to gain control of your opponent's <Card name="Gust" />.
	Your Ravenbloom Student triggers when Rebuttal resolves under your control.
	If Gust then resolves under your control, your Ravenbloom Student triggers again, while your opponent's Ravenbloom Student does not trigger for Gust.
</Callout>
