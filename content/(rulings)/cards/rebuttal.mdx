## When do I choose whether to pay the power for Rebuttal? [#power-payment-timing]

You choose whether to pay <Universal /> when Rebuttal resolves, not while it is being finalized.

When Rebuttal is finalized, you choose the spell with energy cost no more than <Energy value={4} />.<Rule number="355.5" />
The optional power payment is not an additional cost to play Rebuttal; it is a cost within the spell's instructions.<Rule number="204.2" /><Rule number="204.3" />
Costs within a spell's instructions are paid during that spell's resolution.<Rule number="204.3.b" />

Opponents can therefore react to Rebuttal knowing which spell it chose, but without knowing whether you will pay <Universal /> to gain control of that spell or decline to pay and counter it.
