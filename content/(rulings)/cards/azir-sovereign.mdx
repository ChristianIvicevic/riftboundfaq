## If Azir, Sovereign is killed or returned to his base before his attack trigger resolves, do token units still move? [#attack-trigger-without-azir]

If Azir is killed, no token units move; if he is returned to base, no token units move unless he returns to a battlefield before the move instruction executes.
Removing Azir does not by itself stop the triggered ability from resolving.<Rule number="406.2" /><Rule number="406.5" />
"This battlefield" refers to information drawn from Azir as the ability's source, and the game checks that information when the instruction executes.<Rule number="359.3.f.1" /><Rule number="359.3.f.2" />

If Azir is at base or no longer on the board then, he has no battlefield for the instruction to use.<Rule number="359.3.e.12" />
Without a battlefield to use, no token units move.

A recalled Azir remains the same permanent on the board, so returning him to a battlefield before execution supplies a battlefield for the instruction.<Rule number="455" /><Rule number="124" />
A killed Azir moves from the board to the trash.<Rule number="428.2" />
That card becomes a new object for tracking.<Rule number="124" />
Returning it to the board therefore does not restore the source tracked by the ability.
