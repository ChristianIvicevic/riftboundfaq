## If Azir, Sovereign is killed or returned to his base before his attack trigger resolves, do token units still move? [#attack-trigger-without-azir]

If Azir is killed, no token units move; if he is returned to base, no token units move unless he returns to a battlefield before the move instruction executes.
Removing Azir does not by itself stop the triggered ability from resolving.<Rule number="425.1" /><Rule number="425.2.a" />
"This battlefield" refers to information drawn from Azir as the ability's source, and the game checks that information when the instruction executes.<Rule number="359.3.f.1" /><Rule number="359.3.f.2" />

If Azir is at base or no longer on the board then, he has no battlefield for the instruction to use.<Rule number="359.3.e.12" />
The instruction cannot be followed and is ignored.<Rule number="359.3.e.6" />

A recalled Azir remains the same permanent on the board, so returning him to a battlefield before execution supplies a battlefield for the instruction.<Rule number="455" /><Rule number="456.2" />
A killed Azir moves to a non-board zone and becomes a new object, so that card returning to the board does not restore the source tracked by the ability.<Rule number="124" />

See [Does removing a triggered ability's source stop that ability?](/general-rules/abilities#source-removal) for the distinction between an ability remaining on the chain and a source-dependent instruction failing.
