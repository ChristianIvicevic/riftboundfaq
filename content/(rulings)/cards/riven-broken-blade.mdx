## When do I spend Riven, Broken Blade's buff for her second and third modes? [#modal-buff-costs]

Riven's buff costs appear later in her triggered effect, so you spend them during <Term item="resolution">resolution</Term>, not during <Term item="finalization">finalization</Term>.

The "Spend my buff to..." wording makes spending the buff a cost within instructions for the linked <Stun /> or ready effect.<Rule number="740.4.a" />
Riven's effect starts with "choose one," so those costs appear later in the triggered effect.
Costs that appear later in a triggered effect are paid during resolution.<Rule number="204.3.a" />

<Callout type="warn" title="Rules citation needed">
	Core Rules 1.5 is internally inconsistent here.
	One rule broadly says that a triggered ability's cost within instructions becomes its base cost.<Rule number="404.1.b.1" />
	That wording does not distinguish later modal costs such as Riven's and conflicts with the position-based rule above.
	This ruling follows the position-based rule; official clarification is still needed.
</Callout>

Choose the mode and any target it requires during finalization.<Rule number="355.10" /><Rule number="403.2" />
If Riven has no buff when resolution reaches the chosen instruction, you cannot spend one.<Rule number="702.2.b.1" />
Because the cost cannot be paid, the linked <Stun /> or ready effect does not occur.<Rule number="203.3" />

## Can I choose Riven's "Buff me" mode if she already has a buff? [#already-buffed-mode]

Yes, as long as that mode has not already been chosen this turn.
A unit that already has a buff can still be chosen for an effect that would buff it.<Rule number="427.1.c" />
You choose Riven's mode during <Term item="finalization">finalization</Term>.<Rule number="403.2" />
When the ability <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term>, Riven receives no additional buff, so the chosen mode has no effect.<Rule number="427.1.b.1" />
