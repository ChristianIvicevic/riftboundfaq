## How are targets chosen when playing Alpha Strike? [#targeting]

When finalizing Alpha Strike, choose a friendly unit, then choose up to one enemy unit at a battlefield for each point of that friendly unit's might at that time.<Rule number="355.5" /><Rule number="355.14.b" /><Rule number="355.14.c" />

The friendly unit is a [target](/general-rules/targeting#target-definition) because the spell mentions it.<Rule number="355.10" />
Each chosen enemy unit is also a target because it receives split damage.<Rule number="355.14.a" />
The descriptions "friendly unit" and "enemy units at battlefields" are [targeting restrictions](/general-rules/targeting#target-restrictions), so each unit must meet the applicable description when chosen.<Rule number="355.9.b" />

Choosing an enemy unit with <Deflect /> also imposes its Deflect cost because Alpha Strike's controller is an opponent of that unit's controller.<Rule number="809.1.c" />
This mandatory additional cost is added during finalization.<Rule number="356.2.a.2" /><Rule number="809.1.d" />

Once finalization is complete, these target choices cannot change unless a spell or ability instructs a player to make new choices.<Rule number="355.15" />

## When and how is Alpha Strike's damage distributed? [#split-distribution]

When Alpha Strike's damage instruction can execute, its controller uses the friendly target's might at resolution to determine and distribute the available damage.<Rule number="355.14.e" /><Rule number="359.3.f.2" />
Effects that change the friendly target's might before resolution therefore change the available damage.

When making that distribution, Alpha Strike's controller must assign at least 1 damage to each enemy target the spell can still affect.<Rule number="355.14.f" /><Rule number="355.14.g" />
The controller distributes all available damage among those targets.

If the friendly target is illegal, its might is null and the damage instruction is ignored.<Rule number="359.3.e.12" /><Rule number="359.3.f.2.a" />
The instruction also does not execute if no enemy target remains legal.<Rule number="359.3.e.7" />

### What if the friendly unit's might is reduced? [#might-reduction]

A might reduction lowers Alpha Strike's available damage; if there isn't enough to assign at least 1 damage to every affected enemy target, exactly enough of them must cease being targets.<Rule number="359.3.f.2" /><Rule number="355.14.f" /><Rule number="355.14.g" /><Rule number="355.14.h" />

Alpha Strike's controller chooses which targets cease being targets, but cannot drop extra targets to concentrate the damage on fewer units.<Rule number="355.14.h" /><Rule number="355.14.h.1" />
A might below 0 is treated as 0 when Alpha Strike references it.<Rule number="143.2.b" />
With 0 available damage, every enemy target ceases being a target because only positive damage can be dealt.<Rule number="355.14.h" /><Rule number="417.1.e" />

A unit ceasing to be a target does not undo any effect already triggered when that unit was chosen.<Rule number="355.14.i" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	You chose a friendly <Card name="Pakaa Cub" /> with 3 might, then chose 3 enemy units.
	Before Alpha Strike resolves, an effect reduces Pakaa Cub to 2 might.
	You must choose exactly 1 enemy unit to cease being a target.
	You cannot drop a second target to deal both points of damage to one unit.
</Callout>

## What if the chosen friendly unit leaves the board or becomes illegal before Alpha Strike resolves? [#illegal-friendly-target]

Alpha Strike's damage instruction uses the chosen unit's might only if, when the spell resolves, that unit is friendly, on the board, and has not changed to or from a non-board zone; otherwise, the instruction is ignored.

Alpha Strike does not require the chosen friendly unit to be at a particular location, so moving between board locations does not make it illegal if it is a friendly unit when Alpha Strike resolves.<Rule number="359.3.e.2" />
If it temporarily stops being a unit or stops being friendly but meets those restrictions again at resolution, it is also legal.<Rule number="359.3.e.3" />

If the target changes to or from a non-board zone, it remains illegal even if it returns before resolution because it is a new object.<Rule number="359.3.e.4" />
If it still does not meet the friendly-unit restriction at resolution, it is likewise illegal.<Rule number="359.3.e.2" />

Alpha Strike still resolves, but the [illegal target is unaffected](/general-rules/targeting#illegal-targets).<Rule number="359.3.e.1" /><Rule number="359.3.e.5" />
The illegal friendly target's might is therefore null, so the damage calculation and related instructions are ignored.<Rule number="359.3.e.12" /><Rule number="359.3.f.2.a" />

## What if a chosen enemy unit leaves a battlefield or becomes illegal before Alpha Strike resolves? [#illegal-enemy-target]

It receives damage only if it is an enemy unit at a battlefield when Alpha Strike resolves and has not changed to or from a non-board zone.

Whether the unit becomes legal again depends on whether it temporarily stopped meeting a targeting restriction or changed to or from a non-board zone.
If the unit moves to base or otherwise stops meeting those restrictions but meets them again before resolution, it is legal again.<Rule number="359.3.e.2" /><Rule number="359.3.e.3" />
If it changes to or from a non-board zone, it remains illegal even if it returns.<Rule number="359.3.e.4" />

An [illegal target](/general-rules/targeting#illegal-targets) is unaffected.<Rule number="359.3.e.5" />
If at least one enemy target remains legal, Alpha Strike still distributes its damage among those targets.<Rule number="359.3.e.8" />
If every chosen enemy unit is illegal, the damage instruction does not execute.<Rule number="359.3.e.7" />
