## Is an equipment's effect text active while Lady of Clockwork makes it a unit gear? [#equipment-effect-text]

No. An equipment's effect text is inactive while Lady of Clockwork makes it a unit gear.
Effect text is inactive unless its card is attached to another card.<Rule number="136.2.b" />
For example, a <Card name="Cloth Armor" /> unit gear's <Shield value={2} /> ability is inactive, so it does not get +2 might while defending.<Rule number="721.2" />

## What happens when Lady of Clockwork makes attached equipment into a unit gear? [#attached-equipment]

The equipment detaches as it becomes a unit gear and remains at the same location as the card it was attached to.<Rule number="718.6.a" /><Rule number="436.4" />
After it detaches, its effect text becomes inactive and its printed rules text becomes active again.<Rule number="436.1.c" />
The card it was attached to stops receiving the equipment's effect text and might bonus.<Rule number="436.1.d" /><Rule number="436.1.e" />

## What happens when I activate Equip on equipment that Lady of Clockwork made into a unit gear? [#unit-gear-equip]

You can activate and pay for the <Equip /> ability, but the unit gear does not attach when the ability <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term>.
<Equip /> is an activated ability.<Rule number="818.1" />
You choose its unit target and pay its cost before resolution.<Rule number="818.1.b.1" /><Rule number="403.2" /><Rule number="405.1" />
Its effect then instructs the source gear to attach to that unit.<Rule number="818.1.c.2" />
A unit cannot be attached, so the attach action does nothing.<Rule number="435.1.h" /><Rule number="435.1.h.1" />

## What happens when Shady Spectacles or Mirror Image copies a non-unit gear that Lady of Clockwork made into a unit? [#copying-unit-gear]

With either <Card name="Shady Spectacles" /> or <Card name="Mirror Image" />, the resulting copy is a gear, not a unit.

While Lady of Clockwork's effect applies, the gear's current unit type satisfies the unit requirement for each effect's choice.<Rule number="141.1.a.3" /><Rule number="355.9.b" />
Lady of Clockwork changes the gear's actual type and might without changing its copyable traits.<Rule number="479.1.c" /><Rule number="479.2" />
Copy effects use the gear's printed traits, the traits it had when created as a token, or traits supplied by an existing copy effect, so they do not include Lady of Clockwork's changes.<Rule number="479.1.b" /><Rule number="479.1.b.1" />

## What happens when Lady of Clockwork makes Deploy gear into a unit at an uncontested battlefield its controller does not control? [#deploy-contest]

The gear applies contested status to that battlefield, and the next <Term item="cleanup">cleanup</Term> stages combat if an opposing unit is there or a non-combat showdown otherwise.
That combat or showdown opens after the <Term item="chain">chain</Term> is empty.

When Lady of Clockwork's ability <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term>, the <Deploy /> gear becomes a unit at its existing location.
A unit that becomes present at a battlefield its controller does not control applies contested status there.<Rule number="190.3.a" />
The resulting cleanup stages a showdown.<Rule number="323.7" />
If opposing units are present, it also stages combat.<Rule number="323.8" />
A staged showdown or combat waits until no items remain on the chain before opening.<Rule number="323.11" /><Rule number="323.12" /><Rule number="331.2" />

## When does Lady of Clockwork's unit gear become a non-unit gear again? [#choosing-another-gear]

Lady of Clockwork's previous effect stops granting the old gear its unit type and might as soon as her controller chooses another gear during <Term item="finalization">finalization</Term>, before the new ability <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term>.
If this leaves it as an unattached non-unit gear at a battlefield and it does not have <Deploy />, the <Term item="cleanup">cleanup</Term> after finalization recalls it before players can respond.

Lady of Clockwork's gear choice is made during finalization.<Rule number="403.2" />
Lady of Clockwork's stated duration therefore ends when the new gear is chosen, not when the ability resolves.
Finalizing the ability makes a cleanup outstanding.<Rule number="319.4" />
That cleanup must finish before play proceeds.<Rule number="334.2.a" />
The new ability cannot resolve during it.<Rule number="320" />
<Term item="priority">Priority</Term> is not passed or awarded during it.<Rule number="320.1" />
During that cleanup, an unattached non-unit gear at a battlefield is recalled unless it has <Deploy />.<Rule number="459.1" />

## What happens to attached equipment when Lady of Clockwork's unit gear stops being a unit? [#equipment-on-unit-gear]

The equipment stays attached when Lady of Clockwork's unit gear stops being a unit.
The equipment is the attached card, while the unit gear is the top-most card.<Rule number="717" />
Only the attached card must remain a non-unit.<Rule number="718.6" />
It remains attached until something detaches it.<Rule number="718.1" />
If the gear is recalled after losing its unit type, the attached equipment changes locations with it.<Rule number="719.3.a" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	Lady of Clockwork makes <Card name="Helm of Suppression" /> a unit gear, then <Card name="Svellsongur" /> attaches to it.
	Svellsongur copies Helm of Suppression's printed rules text into its effect text for as long as it remains attached.<Rule number="479.1.b.1" />
	When Lady of Clockwork chooses another gear, Helm of Suppression stops being a unit but Svellsongur remains attached.
	The copied effect text remains appended to Helm of Suppression's rules text, so Helm of Suppression is a non-unit gear with two instances of its printed rules text.<Rule number="719.1" />
</Callout>
