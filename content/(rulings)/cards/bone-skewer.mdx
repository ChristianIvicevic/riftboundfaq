## Can Bone Skewer choose a unit that says it "can't be chosen by enemy spells and abilities"? [#targeting-protection]

Yes.
The unit's untargetability ability is inactive while the card is in hand, and Bone Skewer's choice does not target it.

"I can't be chosen by enemy spells and abilities" is a passive untargetability ability.<Rule number="757" /><Rule number="757.1" />
Passive abilities of permanents are typically active only while they are on the board.<Rule number="365.1" />
An ability that functions in another zone describes that context itself,<Rule number="366.1" /> which this ability does not, so it has no effect in hand.
Separately, a card in hand is not a target because the hand is not a public zone.<Rule number="355.10.a" />

## Does Bone Skewer stun a unit that can't be chosen by enemy spells or abilities? [#stun-targeting]

Yes.
The reflexive trigger that stuns the unit does not ask its controller to choose anything.

If the opponent plays the unit, Bone Skewer's "then do this" wording creates a reflexive trigger.<Rule number="387.1" /><Rule number="387.2" />
The trigger is linked to the preceding ability, and "it" can only refer to the unit played by that ability.<Rule number="394.1" /><Rule number="397" />
The unit is therefore programmatically determined rather than chosen by the trigger's controller, so it is not a target.<Rule number="355.10.d" /><Rule number="355.10.d.1" />

Untargetability does not prevent the stun because it only prevents the unit from being a legal target.<Rule number="758" />

## If a unit chosen by Bone Skewer has Hidden, can the opponent hide it instead of playing it? [#hide-instead-of-play]

No.
Hide is a separate action, not a way to fulfill Bone Skewer's instruction to play the unit.<Rule number="811.1.c.1" />
Bone Skewer instructs the opponent to play the unit as a limited action, following the normal play process except where the effect says otherwise.<Rule number="419.3" /><Rule number="419.3.b" />

## Can a unit played for free by Bone Skewer still get an effect for paying an optional additional cost? [#optional-additional-costs]

Yes.

The phrase "any and all costs" in Bone Skewer's text is broader than the standard "ignoring its cost" wording.
Standard cost-ignoring only zeroes out a card's base energy and power costs.<Rule number="356.1.b.1" />

<Steps>
<Step>
The opponent chooses whether to pay an optional additional cost while making the relevant choices for the unit.<Rule number="355.1.a" />
</Step>
<Step>
A chosen additional cost is included when determining the unit's total cost, which Bone Skewer then sets to zero.<Rule number="356.2" /><Rule number="356.5.a" />
</Step>
<Step>
The optional additional cost counts as paid based on that decision, regardless of the amount actually spent.<Rule number="356.4.f.1" />
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
	You play Bone Skewer and choose <Card name="Nami, Headstrong" /> from your opponent's hand.
	Your opponent chooses to pay Nami's optional additional cost of <Calm />, and Bone Skewer sets the resulting total cost to zero.
	When Nami completes being played, her play effect triggers because the additional cost counts as paid, allowing your opponent to stun an enemy unit.<Rule number="419.4.a" />
</Callout>
