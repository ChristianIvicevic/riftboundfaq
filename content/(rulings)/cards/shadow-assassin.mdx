## Does Shadow Assassin count itself when I play it from my trash? [#self-count]

No.
When played from your trash, Shadow Assassin moves from your trash to the chain, then leaves the chain as it enters the board.<Rule number="354" /><Rule number="359.2" /><Rule number="359.2.c" />
By the time its ready condition applies, that copy is no longer in your trash, so it enters ready only if a different card named Shadow Assassin remains in your trash.
