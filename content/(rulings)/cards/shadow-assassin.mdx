## Does Shadow Assassin count itself when I play it from my trash? [#self-count]

No.
The first step of playing Shadow Assassin moves it from your trash to the chain.<Rule number="354" />
It is therefore no longer in your trash when its replacement effect, which changes how it enters the board, applies.<Rule number="369.3" />
It enters ready only if another card named Shadow Assassin remains in your trash.
