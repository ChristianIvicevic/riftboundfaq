## Can Iron Revenant banish a unit or gear put into my trash as an additional cost to help pay that play's power cost? [#additional-cost-payment]

No, because you pay the played card's energy and power costs before paying its additional cost by discarding a card or killing a unit.

You choose whether to pay an optional additional cost before determining the card's total cost.<Rule number="355.1.a" />
For <Card name="Atakhan" /> and <Card name="Commander Ledros" />, the variable discount from that choice is checked during total-cost determination, before payment begins.<Rule number="356.4.g" />
During payment, you first pay the combined energy and power costs.<Rule number="357.1" />
You then pay non-standard costs, such as discarding a card or killing a unit; "in any order" applies only among those non-standard costs.<Rule number="357.2" />

During the energy and power payment step, you may activate Iron Revenant's <Reaction /> ability to <Add /> power.<Rule number="357.1.a" />
At that point, the card that will enter your trash for the additional cost isn't there yet.
Therefore, Iron Revenant can't banish a unit or gear discarded for <Card name="Zed, From the Shadows" /> to help pay Zed's power cost.
It also can't banish a unit killed for Atakhan or Commander Ledros to help pay the power cost of the card being played.
