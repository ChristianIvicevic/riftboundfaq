## Can I play a unit from face down after Graves, Blasting Through's ability kills all my defenders? [#facedown-unit]

Yes, at your next legal opportunity, if the unit was hidden at that battlefield on an earlier turn and can otherwise be legally played.

The <Term item="cleanup">cleanup</Term> after Graves's ability may kill your last defender,<Rule number="323.4" /> but the ongoing combat prevents control of the battlefield from changing.<Rule number="190.4.b" />
You therefore still control the battlefield, and the facedown card stays there.<Rule number="811.1.b" /><Rule number="323.6.a" />
The facedown unit can still be played.<Rule number="811.6" />

<Callout type="idea" title="Example">
  Graves attacks alone, and his ability kills every defending unit.
  At your next legal opportunity, you play <Card name="Evelynn, Entrancing" /> from face down at that battlefield.
  Graves deals no combat damage, and Evelynn's 2<Might /> deals only 2 damage to the 4<Might /> Graves, so neither unit has lethal damage.<Rule number="142.4.b" /><Rule number="467.2.b" /><Rule number="467.2.c" /><Rule number="467.2.d" />
  Because Evelynn remains as a defender, Graves is recalled.<Rule number="468.1.a.2" />
  Evelynn's controller retains control, so Graves's controller does not conquer the battlefield.<Rule number="468.3.d" /><Rule number="468.5" /><Rule number="471.1" />
</Callout>
