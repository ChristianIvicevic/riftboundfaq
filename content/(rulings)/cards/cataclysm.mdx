## Does Cataclysm still move a unit if the chosen battlefield is unoccupied when it resolves? [#unoccupied-destination]

No, if the chosen battlefield is unoccupied when Cataclysm <Term item="resolution">resolves</Term>, the chosen unit does not move.

Cataclysm requires the move's destination to be "an occupied enemy battlefield," and a move's source defines any restrictions on its legal destinations.<Rule number="451.1" />
You choose the destination while playing the spell and cannot change that choice later unless a spell or ability instructs you to.<Rule number="355.4" /><Rule number="355.15" />
Once the chosen battlefield is unoccupied, it no longer satisfies Cataclysm's destination restriction, so the move cannot occur.

The battlefield is a restriction on the move's destination, not a target.<Rule number="355.10.b" />
Cataclysm therefore does not mistarget solely because the battlefield becomes unoccupied.
Its move instruction is instead impossible and negated.<Rule number="359.3.e.6" />
