## Can Flash target a unit that is already in base? [#target-in-base]

Yes, Flash can target a friendly unit that is already in base.<Rule number="355.9.a" /><Rule number="355.9.b" />
Flash's card text fixes base as the move destination, superseding the generic rule that instructs you to choose a destination for each move.<Rule number="002" /><Rule number="355.4" />
Because you make no destination choice, the restriction that a chosen destination must differ from the unit's current location does not affect whether the unit is a legal target.<Rule number="355.4.a" />

When Flash resolves, the unit cannot move because it would not change from one board space to another.<Rule number="446.1" />
The impossible instruction is ignored.<Rule number="055" />
